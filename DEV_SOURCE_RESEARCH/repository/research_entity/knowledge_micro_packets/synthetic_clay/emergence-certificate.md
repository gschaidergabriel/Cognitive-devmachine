### Core
A recurring or novel assembly should not be called emergent merely because it appears. The G1 protocol requires preregistered recurrence/prediction, lesion, rescue, OOD stability, multiscale or synergistic causal contribution, independent evaluation and cost/robustness shadows before an `emergent_organ` designation.

### Evidence / boundary
This is a research-method criterion, not evidence that FRANK currently satisfies it. Positive interaction terms alone remain mechanism signals.

### Implication
The emergence trigger can test specific candidates without collapsing the whole concept into one scalar score.

### Next test
Choose one recurring candidate assembly, preregister its functional prediction, then perform lesion/rescue and held-out context tests.
