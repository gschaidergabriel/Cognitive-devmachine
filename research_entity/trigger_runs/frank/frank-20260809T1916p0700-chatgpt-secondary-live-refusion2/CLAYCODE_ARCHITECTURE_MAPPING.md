## Human mapping

The current evidence-supported H1 candidate is deliberately neither a bag of independent services nor an undifferentiated monolith.

The final physical target remains one Clay binary, one local UnifiedDB and one Clay-native Qwen-class 4B on Andreas' Ubuntu developer system. H1 keeps logical boundaries visible only where they are needed to measure, lesion, replay, fuzz or differentially translate behavior into H2.

The main cognitive/effect path is:

`input/percept -> fresh cognitive snapshot -> prepared ActiveAssembly -> transient topology/Hyperposition -> GWT selection/broadcast/use -> local 4B transform -> proposal only -> fresh dependency/capability revalidation -> deterministic runtime authority/effect gate -> receipt/outcome -> provenance-bound re-entry -> typed local recurrence control -> bounded semantic delta/backflow`

### UnifiedDB

UnifiedDB owns durable semantic identity, epochs, provenance, conflict, replay identity and effect/outcome linkage. It is not a dump of every transient activation. Derived views may be rebuilt from it.

### ActiveAssembly

ActiveAssembly is a bounded, rebuildable projection prepared for the local 4B. Its purpose is to preserve decision-relevant evidence under a small context/KV budget while keeping provenance, explicit counterevidence and stable re-expansion references.

The new adversarial correction is crucial: ancestry diversity and contradiction observability are different. Copies from one ancestry must not earn independent support, but a same-ancestry contradiction must also not disappear merely because a family cap is one.

### GWT and Hyperposition

GWT is the measurable competition/ignition/broadcast/reconstruction/uptake/causal-use/re-entry plane. Hyperposition preserves alternatives and higher-order codependencies. Neither creates truth or execution authority.

### 4B model

FRANK-level recurrence is initially runtime recurrence around a conventional local 4B adapter. Model-internal recurrent depth is a separate later comparison. The model transforms bounded state and produces proposals; it never executes external effects directly.

### Snapshot -> commit promotion barrier

The cognition snapshot and the later effect transaction are intentionally separated. Before a proposal becomes durable or external, the runtime opens a fresh commit transaction and revalidates the declared effect-relevant dependency closure plus capability/resource epoch. Unrelated state changes need not abort; a newly discovered dependency absent from the snapshot forces reassembly.

This is motivated by the hosted SQLite WAL discriminator where an old read snapshot could not safely be upgraded after a concurrent writer (`SQLITE_BUSY_SNAPSHOT`). It remains a reference/runtime-proxy result until reproduced in target-shaped H1/Ubuntu tests.

### Effect authority

Capabilities must be opaque/sealed and runtime-minted. Model output, GWT, Needle, Retina, peers, graph activation and recurrence control may all propose or inform, but none may mint authority. Cumulative loop/effect budgets, revocation, idempotent receipts and recovery remain mandatory.

### Loop-of-loops

FRANK is tested as heterogeneous typed local loops with different state, timescale, budget and stopping conditions. A fused loop controller must combine regime semantics with readout identity/version/freshness/uncertainty. Low state delta plus high unresolved pressure means STALLED, not converged. Productive bounded cycles are not automatically failures. GWT focus may be an input but not the controller itself.

### Peers and backflow

Strong peers remain independent cognitive instances. Rich semantic synchronization may occur upstream, but local backflow should be bounded, provenance-rich and evidence preserving. A full-state mirror or peer authority collapse is not the target.

### H1 -> H2 -> H3

H1 first implements and falsifies these semantics in ordinary executable host code. H2 must reproduce the frozen accepted semantics in ClayCode and differential-test them. H3 may then collapse physical boundaries into the final one-module/binary architecture without collapsing logical authority, provenance, replay or intervention boundaries.
