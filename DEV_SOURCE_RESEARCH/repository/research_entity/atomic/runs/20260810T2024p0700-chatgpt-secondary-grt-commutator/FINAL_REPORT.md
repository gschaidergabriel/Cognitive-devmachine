Full two-order state replay was the exact reference. CCF evaluated only the dependency slice for pairs that could interact. A conservative read/write overlap graph was the simple baseline.

Primary seed-42 run, N=30,000:
- exact local CCF vs full replay: 30,000 / 30,000 matching labels;
- full replay total: 1.4590 s;
- sparse local CCF total: 0.10369 s;
- conservative RW conflict total: 0.02383 s;
- CCF speedup vs full replay: 14.07x;
- safe independence: CCF 51.5967% vs RW conflict 34.70%, +16.8967 percentage points;
- RW conflict produced 5,069 false conflicts but no unsafe false commute in this generated class.

Five seeds, N=10,000 each:

- speedup vs full replay range 9.86x–14.98x, mean 12.75x;
- safe-independence gain vs RW conflict range 16.49–17.42 pp, mean 16.864 pp.

Controls:
- shuffled pair identities collapsed precision/recall to ~0.484 and caused ~24.97% unsafe-parallel decisions;
- a stale-state control changed 1.6433% of labels and caused 0.84% unsafe-parallel decisions, making epoch/state-instance binding a hard invariant rather than metadata;
