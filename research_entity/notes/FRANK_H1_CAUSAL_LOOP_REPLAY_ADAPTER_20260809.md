## Why this delta exists

The current FRANK loop trajectory probe has already been hardened against false convergence, stale/high-uncertainty residual readouts, monotone-trend cycle aliases, history-only utility proxies, and unclassified single-axis growth. The remaining high-information gap is causal: a readout can classify a trajectory, but classification alone does not establish that a proposed regulator caused useful loop behavior.

The next discriminator therefore needs matched frozen-episode interventions:

`BASELINE -> LESION -> RESCUE`

plus matched policy/re-entry comparisons:

- fixed recurrence budget vs adaptive recurrence;
- truthful re-entry vs shuffled re-entry;
- truthful re-entry vs no re-entry.

The hardened match basis binds the same frozen input hash, loop identity/family, semantic state epoch, graph epoch, controller-policy hash, residual-estimator identity/version and regulator. Mismatched episodes are invalid rather than weak evidence. Lesion/rescue arms require intervention references, and a successful rescue requires a rollback reference.

## Evidence semantics

The adapter makes three distinctions explicit:

1. `HISTORY_PROXY` is not `VALIDATED_NEXT_STEP_ESTIMATE`.
2. A predicted next-step utility is calibrated against the actually observed next-step utility delta; historical tail utility is reported separately and cannot become validated next-step evidence by naming alone.
3. Provenance origin and attestation layer are separate. `SELF_ATTESTED` or `UNVERIFIED` observations cannot produce strong candidate causal support even when a matched lesion/rescue pattern appears.

Strong attestation candidates are currently restricted to target-runtime receipts or an external control plane. This incorporates the live research-center provenance boundary: a provenance label generated by the same cognitive/tenant path is not equivalent to independently anchored provenance.

## Lesion/rescue decision states

For a matched baseline/lesion/rescue triple:

- `INVALID_MATCH`: frozen input / loop / semantic epoch / graph epoch / controller policy / residual-estimator identity / regulator mismatch;
- `NEGATIVE_RESULT_CANDIDATE`: lesion did not create the preregistered material harm;
- `OPEN`: harm exists but rescue is insufficient, outcomes are not verified, or attestation is weak;
- `CANDIDATE_CAUSAL_SUPPORT`: matched lesion harm + rescue recovery + verified outcomes + strong attestation.

Even `CANDIDATE_CAUSAL_SUPPORT` is not a scientific promotion and not independent replication.
