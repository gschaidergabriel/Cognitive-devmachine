## 2. Exact progress doctrine

The owner requires an exact dedicated progress display.

Therefore `666` MUST NEVER invent a percentage from a guessed 400 MB denominator.

Before a complete source manifest is frozen:

- exact assimilation coverage for that source is `0.00%` if no frozen analysis obligations exist;
- inventory state is shown separately as `INDEXING/FREEZE_PENDING`;
- no pseudo-precise percentage is allowed.

After manifest freeze, exact progress is computed from **atomic analysis obligations**, not subjective effort estimates.

For every semantically eligible file/artifact, create deterministic obligations as applicable:

1. `IDENTIFIED` — stable source identity/provenance recorded;
2. `CONTENT_COVERED` — full relevant content read/extracted or a reproducible structured extractor receipt exists;
3. `CLASSIFIED` — mechanism/module/paper/experiment/log/config/data/negative-result/etc. class assigned;
4. `ANALYZED` — claims, mechanisms, equations, couplings, successes, failures and open questions extracted as applicable;
5. `LINEAGE_LINKED` — chronology/predecessor/successor/duplicate/supersession relationships resolved as far as evidence permits;
6. `INTEGRATED` — material deltas written into current RAE archaeology/Topic Packets/branch feedback, or explicit `NO_RESEARCH_DELTA` receipt recorded.

Additional required obligations may exist for special source classes:

- paper: argument/evidence/method mapping;
- code/module: interface/state/coupling/failure mapping;
- experiment/log: execution/result/evidence-class mapping;
- commit: changed mechanism and historical interpretation;
- database/snapshot: schema/state lineage and safe structured extraction;
- binary/non-semantic asset: identity/hash/classification plus explicit `NO_SEMANTIC_ANALYSIS_REQUIRED` rather than pretending its bytes were semantically read.

Exact coverage:

`coverage = completed_atomic_obligations / frozen_total_atomic_obligations`

A source unit contributes no completed obligation until a machine-readable receipt marks that obligation complete.

Changing/adding source data after freeze invalidates the denominator and creates a new manifest epoch; the old percentage remains historically reproducible.
