# FUSION 2 — prior-art destruction and recombination

## What collapsed

External research destroys any claim that CARF invented semiring provenance, factorized provenance circuits, minimal-contingency responsibility, incremental maintenance, content-addressed provenance, or one stored circuit with multiple evaluators. The local factorization benchmark strengthens that destruction: a strong conventional factorized dependency DAG has exactly the same node count as CARF in the tested structured family.

## What remains testable

A narrower Frank-specific contract remains: every material slow-plasticity update should be linked to a current, provenance-valid, outcome-verified internal dependency witness that distinguishes alternative and joint derivations, and should ABSTAIN rather than invent causal credit when stale, broken or computationally unresolved.

This is internal dependency responsibility, not real-world causal truth.

## Reduced semantic obligations

1. `ProvenanceRoot`: current episode-scoped derivation root.
2. `UseWitness`: only reconstructed-and-used inputs enter candidate causal cone.
3. `ResponsibilityWitness`: exact/bounded responsibility over internal dependency structure.
4. `PlasticityGate`: verified outcome + current witness required for material slow updates.

Storage may be a normal factorized DAG. No CARF-specific physical engine is required unless later matched measurements prove a benefit.

## Strongest surviving counterhypothesis

Existing typed receipt/dependency DAG + explicit responsibility evaluator + current epoch checks can satisfy the complete semantic contract with less compiler/runtime/storage complexity. This counterhypothesis is currently favored at runtime-data-structure level.
