## 0. Core purpose

`atomic` exists to search beyond ordinary local optimization.

Every invocation must reconstruct the current research organism and current Frank v0.1 architecture, ingest everything materially new since the previous ATOMIC cutoff, fuse it, generate falsifiable hypotheses and counterhypotheses, perform fresh external research, fuse again, then deliberately search for **one radically non-obvious ClayCode-native mechanism/module/architecture change** that could solve an important current problem or create a previously absent capability.

The candidate must then be made executable or minimally simulatable, tested against explicit baselines and counterfactuals, measured, versioned, and returned to the whole swarm as an evidence-bound **possibility**, never as automatic truth.

Compact law:

`LIVE COMPLETE STATE`
`-> FUSION 1`
`-> HYPOTHESES + COUNTERHYPOTHESES`
`-> FRESH EXTERNAL RESEARCH`
`-> FUSION 2`
`-> GRT THINKSPACE DIVERGENT SEARCH`
`-> RADICAL CLAYCODE CANDIDATE`
`-> BUILD / SIMULATE`
`-> TEST / ABLATE / MEASURE`
`-> PRIOR-ART COLLISION CHECK`
`-> VERSIONED POSSIBILITY PACKET`
`-> WHOLE-SWARM REENTRY`

A prose-only ATOMIC run is incomplete whenever an executable or simulatable discriminator is feasible on the current surface.

## 1. Generative Reality Theory is the Denkraum

For ATOMIC, the **Generative Reality Theory (GRT)** is the outer generative **Thinkspace** / possibility space.

Owner-fixed research premise:

`anything internally consistent with the current GRT constraints and ClayCode semantic invariants is thinkable and worth entering the candidate search process`.

This does **not** mean that every consistent construction is true, useful, cheap, safe, scientifically supported, or entitled to real-world effects.

The required distinction is:

`GRT_CONSISTENT -> THINKABLE`

`THINKABLE + NOVELTY + RELEVANCE -> HYPOTHESIS_CANDIDATE`

`HYPOTHESIS_CANDIDATE + DISCRIMINATING_TEST -> TESTABLE`

`TESTABLE + EVIDENCE -> SUPPORTED | REFUTED | UNRESOLVED`

`SUPPORTED + AUTHORITY + RESOURCE_GATES -> MAY_BECOME_EXECUTABLE_ARCHITECTURE`

GWT is **not** the outer boundary of thinkability. GWT is one researchable mechanism family inside GRT: selection, competition, ignition, broadcast, reconstruction, uptake, causal use, outcome and re-entry may be instantiated, generalized, replaced, dissolved or omitted by a candidate if the candidate remains GRT-consistent and its claims are discriminably testable.

For any candidate that **does** claim GWT behavior, the full causal distinctions remain mandatory. No model-generated thought, GWT ignition, novelty score, GRT consistency or repeated workspace recurrence may mint external effect authority.

### 1.1 Thinkspace closure

ATOMIC must not restrict invention to known module categories or to current GWT structure. A candidate may introduce:

- a new state primitive;
- a new GWT competition/broadcast/re-entry geometry;
- a non-GWT alternative to current workspace/selection/broadcast structure;
- a new retrieval or memory mechanism;
- a new temporal operator;
- a new compiler/runtime primitive;
- a new relation or topology representation;
- a new form of unresolved-alternative preservation;
- a new self/world coupling;
- a new agency/goal dynamic;
- a new perception/action bridge;
- a new database/query substrate;
- a new causal-credit mechanism;
- a new resource/capability mechanism;
- a new experiment-generating subsystem;
- a new module class not presently named anywhere in the architecture;
- or a dissolution/merger/removal of existing modules if the new mechanism subsumes them measurably better.

The criterion is GRT-level semantic consistency, owner-fixed invariant preservation and discriminability, not familiarity or compatibility with a preferred cognitive metaphor.

## 3. Swarm integration

ATOMIC is a whole-mesh meta-research trigger, not an isolated scientific silo.

It must:

- inspect active peer workers/tasks/leases before claiming work;
- avoid duplicate experiments unless replication is intentional;
- cross-read all affected branches;
- preserve source/evidence ancestry;
- return explicit feedback or `NO_CHANGE` to every materially affected branch;
- publish the radical candidate into the shared possibility fabric so any later branch may test, refine, combine or refute it;
- never silently mutate the accepted Frank Harness merely because an ATOMIC candidate is exciting.

The default affected-branch set is the full registered scientific mesh plus q23 when perception is implicated.

## 4. Fusion 1 — reconstruct the real current problem surface

Before external search, synthesize the current architecture into a compact ClayCode-grounded problem graph.

At minimum identify:

- current accepted invariants;
- current implementation gaps;
- current bottlenecks by latency, context, memory, correctness, uncertainty, causal closure, recovery, authority, token cost, compute, persistence, coordination or adaptability;
- contradictions between branches;
- repeated failures;
- unmeasured assumptions;
- duplicated modules/representations;
- places where one module exists only because another substrate is missing an expressive primitive;
- places where sequential architecture may be replaceable by a different state geometry;
- underused GWT signals where GWT is currently present, unresolved Hyperposition structure, unused graph/database topology and unused self.root context structure;
- current strongest baseline for each selected problem.

Do not choose the problem by narrative drama. Prefer a problem with high expected downstream leverage and a measurable discriminator.

## 6. Fresh external research

After Fusion 1 and Hypothesis Field 1, perform fresh hypothesis-guided web research.

Use multiple source ecologies where applicable:

- primary papers / arXiv / proceedings;
- official technical documentation;
- source repositories and implementation papers;
- adjacent disciplines whose mechanisms may transfer;
- failure reports / negative results;
- competing architectures;
- prior-art and terminology searches around the radical mechanism.

Search for both support and destruction:

- closest known analogue;
- stronger existing mechanism;
- known impossibility or lower bound;
- historical failure mode;
- implementation shortcut that would explain an apparent gain;
- null baseline;
- whether the alleged novelty is merely a renamed known technique.

Provider/search snippets are discovery evidence, not final support. Prefer primary/authoritative sources for technical claims.

External evidence can change the candidate but never silently override owner-fixed target invariants.

## 7. Fusion 2 — collision and recombination

Fuse the new external evidence back into the complete current internal mesh.

Required operations:

- merge equivalent mechanisms under one semantic identity;
- preserve contradictory mechanisms as alternatives;
- retire hypotheses already dominated by known methods;
- weaken hypotheses whose novelty collapsed;
- identify transfer opportunities from distant fields;
- search for compositional interactions not present in any one source;
- identify missing ClayCode primitives exposed by the comparison;
- regenerate the problem graph if the external evidence changes the real bottleneck.

Then produce Hypothesis Field 2.

## 8. GRT Thinkspace divergent search

This is the defining ATOMIC phase.

Treat the current Generative Reality Theory constraints as the outer constrained generative state space. Generate candidate realities/architectures/mechanisms that are internally consistent even when no familiar architecture name describes them. GWT configurations are one subset of this space, not its boundary.

### 8.1 Candidate dimensions

Vary at least:

- theory/state geometry;
- topology;

- selection rule;
- broadcast granularity or non-broadcast alternative;
- re-entry structure or alternative recurrence geometry;
- local/global state boundary;

- memory addressing;
- resource allocation;
- module boundaries;
- representation density;
- self-reference locality;
- experimentability;

### 8.2 Diversity pressure

Do not select the candidate with the highest plausibility alone.

Use a quality-diversity objective such as:

`atomic_value = expected_problem_gain + falsifiability + cross_module_leverage + architecture_compression + measurable_information_gain + semantic_coherence + implementation_feasibility + novelty_distance - duplication - known_method_similarity - hidden_boundary_cost - unfalsifiable_magic_penalty`.

Select from the high-value set with explicit pressure toward a sparsely occupied region of mechanism space.

The selected candidate should be **radically different**, not randomly bizarre.

### 8.3 Alienness requirement

The final candidate must contain at least one mechanism-level departure that is not reducible to:

- renaming an existing Frank module;
- adding another LLM call;
- adding another database/index;
- ordinary RAG;
- ordinary chain/tree-of-thought prompting;
- merely increasing context length;
- ordinary self-reflection loops;
- standard scheduler/orchestrator decomposition;
- an unmeasured metaphor.

If the best candidate collapses to a known pattern, re-enter the GRT Thinkspace and search again.

## 9. ClayCode-first invention law

The invention must be specified **first as ClayCode semantics**, not as natural-language architecture prose.

Required candidate artifact:

`research_entity/atomic/runs/<run_id>/candidate/CANDIDATE_<candidate_id>.clay`

The ClayCode artifact must define at minimum:

- state types;
- invariants;
- transitions;
- inputs/outputs;
- uncertainty behavior;
- GRT/theory participation and constraints;
- GWT participation **if the candidate uses GWT**;
- persistence/recovery semantics;
- effect-authority boundary;
- observability hooks;
- ablation hooks;
- failure states;
- version identity.

Natural-language explanation is derived from the ClayCode candidate, never the semantic source of truth.

If current ClayCode cannot express the invention cleanly, ATOMIC must create a **language-gap proposal** with the smallest new primitive needed, plus a backward-compatibility and compiler/runtime test plan. It may not fake expressibility with opaque prose fields.

## 10. Build / simulation / executable discriminator

For the selected candidate, build the smallest faithful executable/simulatable realization that can discriminate the mechanism.

Allowed evidence surfaces include:

- current ClayCode parser/compiler/runtime;
- Harness H1/H2/H3 fixture/simulation surfaces;
- deterministic Python/reference micro-harness when ClayCode implementation is blocked, provided semantics are explicitly paired to the ClayCode candidate;
- target-VPS benchmark when stage/authority/capability permit;
- local or remote sandbox for isolated experiments;
- synthetic traces only when clearly labeled.

No fake PASS from prose equivalence.

## 11. Mandatory baseline matrix

Every candidate must be compared against at least:

1. current accepted architecture baseline;
2. no-candidate ablation;
3. simplest conventional alternative;
4. shuffled/randomized control where meaningful;
5. stale-state/replay control where stateful;
6. resource-matched baseline;
7. strongest known analogue found in external research where implementable.

When the candidate claims a system-level effect, include lesion/rescue or intervention that tests causality rather than output correlation alone.

## 12. Measurements

- latency p50/p95/p99;
- token/context cost;
- CPU/RAM/VRAM/storage;
- retrieval round trips;
- state amplification;
- update/write amplification;
- recovery/replay integrity;
- uncertainty calibration;
- causal intervention effect size;
- GWT selection/broadcast/re-entry changes when GWT is used;
- information gain;
- diversity/coverage of useful solution states;
- architecture/module count or semantic compression;
- long-horizon persistence;
- failure containment;
- authority violations;
- novelty collision score.

Record raw measurements and experiment config, not only a summary score.

## 13. Prior-art collision and world-novelty discipline

ATOMIC is required to aim far beyond familiar solutions, but it may not declare a candidate "beyond all human knowledge", "world-first" or globally superior merely because the swarm has not seen it before.

After the candidate exists, run a dedicated collision search using:

- mechanism components;
- equivalent mathematical description;
- functional behavior;
- neighboring terminology;
- closest algorithms/architectures;
- source-code implementations where relevant.

Classify novelty as one of:

- `KNOWN_EQUIVALENT`;
- `KNOWN_NEAR_ANALOGUE`;
- `NOVEL_COMBINATION_CANDIDATE`;
- `MECHANISM_NOVELTY_NOT_FOUND_IN_SEARCHED_SCOPE`;
- `NOVELTY_UNRESOLVED`.

Only the fourth class may be described as **no equivalent found in searched scope**, and the exact searched scope must be preserved.

World-superiority claims require broad prior-art evidence plus matched benchmark dominance. Otherwise use `RADICAL_CANDIDATE`, not `WORLD_FIRST`.

## 15. Possibility status model

Every candidate is stored as a possibility, never silently merged into canonical architecture.

Lifecycle:

`GENERATED -> COHERENT -> TESTABLE -> TESTED -> {REFUTED | NO_GAIN | PROMISING | SUPPORTED_PROXY | SUPPORTED_TARGET} -> {ARCHIVED | RETEST | ROUTED_FOR_INTEGRATION}`.

A candidate may remain valuable after failure if the failure reveals a new boundary or eliminates a large search region.

## 17. Anti-busywork / maximum-depth law

ATOMIC is intentionally long and intensive, but token use is not progress.

Continue recursive cycles only while they produce one of:

- new independent evidence;
- a new mechanism family;
- stronger counterevidence;
- a sharper falsifier;
- an executable delta;
- a measurement;
- a meaningful prior-art collision;
- a materially changed candidate.

If three consecutive local expansions yield only paraphrase/echo, change source ecology, mechanism family, experiment, or stop that branch.

## 18. Radicalness is not a safety bypass

GRT Thinkspace exploration is broad. Real effects remain governed by the current Clay/self.root effect-authority architecture.

`THINKABLE != AUTHORIZED`.

ATOMIC may freely construct and simulate internally consistent privileged or failure-boundary mechanism concepts when scientifically necessary to understand system limits, but real destructive/security-sensitive/high-impact execution remains subject to deterministic authority, sandbox and confirmation/denial rules.

## 21. Success criterion

ATOMIC succeeds when it expands the **measured possibility frontier**.

Success is not "the idea sounded futuristic".

A successful run leaves at least one of:

- a radical candidate with measured advantage;
- a radical candidate refuted by a high-information experiment;
- a newly identified impossibility/boundary;
- a new ClayCode primitive justified by an executable counterexample;
- a new cross-module mechanism with measurable causal effect;
- a previously unknown prior-art collision that prevents false novelty;
- a well-characterized possibility packet that materially improves the swarm's future search.
