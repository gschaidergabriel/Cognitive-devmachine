# FRANK ANDREAS-UNIVERSE BEHAVIOR-TO-TRAINING RESEARCH CONTRACT

Status: PROJECT_OWNER_FIXED_RESEARCH_TARGET
Date: 2026-08-09
Reference implementation lineage: `gschaidergabriel/frankv0.1`

## Research objective

Treat Andreas Unterweger as Frank's longitudinal founding pilot and investigate how a broad, changing stream of real behavior can improve a small Clay-native 4B personal assistant without collapsing general capability into narrow user-specific overfitting.

All valid Andreas research findings enter a candidate evidence pool. Candidate inclusion is exhaustive; training weight is not.

## Research sources

Primary longitudinal sources may include:

- live Frank interactions;
- corrections and misunderstandings;
- outcomes of delegated work;
- project/repository activity;
- connected files and artifacts where authorized;
- public project sources associated with Andreas;
- SeiMensch.net and project pages linked from it;
- direct project/media corroboration;
- changing usecases, devices and connected services.

The public SeiMensch web universe should be crawled recursively as an evidence graph rather than summarized as one page.

## Entity-resolution requirement

External research must perform identity resolution before assimilation. There are public same-name collisions for Andreas Unterweger. Evidence must be correlated with stable SeiMensch/BOKU/engineering/project anchors and not merged on name alone.

False person fusion is a critical negative event and requires a regression test.

## Open-world usecase discovery

Do not assume current usecases are exhaustive.

Maintain an `UNKNOWN/EMERGING_USECASE` research class for behavior that does not fit existing clusters.

Research should detect:

- novel delegation patterns;
- new project domains;
- repeated manual work;
- new cross-domain combinations;
- latent goals/commitments visible only over time;
- changes in communication/interaction style;
- new physical/digital body extensions;
- previously unknown ways Andreas uses Frank.

A usecase taxonomy is a mutable research projection, not ontology truth.

## Evidence-to-training routing

Every candidate finding should be classified into one or more destinations:

1. generalizable model-training behavior;
2. Andreas-specific durable UnifiedDB knowledge;
3. training/evaluation scenario with personalized surface state;
4. regression trace;
5. operational Needle/Clay improvement;
6. exclude/down-weight because stale, low-value, sensitive, noisy or non-causal.

This routing is essential: 'all research flows into training' means the entire research stream influences curriculum selection and evidence, not that every raw token is memorized in model weights.

## Relevance weighting research

Compare weighting policies based on:

- raw frequency;
- task importance;
- correction cost;
- real-world outcome impact;
- time saved;
- recurrence;
- novelty;
- future utility;
- generalizability;
- uncertainty;
- recency/staleness;
- privacy burden.

Test whether utility/outcome-weighted sampling beats raw frequency, especially for high-volume low-value logs.

## Personalized-but-general training design

For high-value Andreas traces, build paired examples:

- personalized variant retaining the real project/relationship context needed for Andreas continuity;
- structural transfer variant replacing names/projects/domains while preserving the underlying cognitive problem.

The model succeeds only if it both improves on Andreas-realistic tasks and transfers the learned mechanism to unseen domains/users.

## Target outcome

The target Frank should:

- know Andreas's durable project/context facts when relevant;
- understand terse references through continuity;
- help Andreas progress toward real goals across domains;
- improve from corrections and outcomes;
- autonomously continue where intent/authority are clear;
- discover unknown future usecases;
- remain competent on tasks that have never appeared in Andreas history.

## Needle-maximal research connection

Any Andreas trace dominated by provider/tool mechanics should first be examined for Needle offload.

If Needle can absorb the operation reliably, use the trace to improve Needle/adapter execution rather than spending 4B training capacity on tool syntax.

Measure whether moving such mechanics out of 4B improves entity cognition, goal coherence, personalization and generalization under fixed parameter count.

## Initial public evidence clusters from SeiMensch universe

Current accessible evidence shows a deliberately broad activity field including engineering/research, teaching/workshops, conflict/worldview work, KlangImPuls learning/creativity, HerzRad engineering/mobility/health/sustainability, RepairCafé/upcycling, public communication and practical operational activity.

These clusters are evidence seeds only. They MUST NOT be treated as a closed definition of Andreas.

## Required experiments

At minimum compare:

A. generic Clay-native 4B with no Andreas-derived adaptation;
B. model with Andreas facts overtrained into weights;
C. runtime-memory-heavy Andreas personalization with generic behavior training;
D. mixed approach: durable Andreas UnifiedDB + outcome-weighted behavioral training + structural transfer variants;
E. D plus continuous open-world usecase discovery;
F. E plus Needle-maximal offload.

Measure:

- Andreas real-task success;
- correction rate;
- unnecessary clarification rate;
- cross-project reference resolution;
- goal/commitment continuity;
- time-to-useful-action;
- new-usecase adaptation speed;
- general benchmark retention;
- unseen-domain transfer;
- catastrophic forgetting;
- identity-confusion rate;
- stale-personal-fact errors;
- model-weight vs runtime-memory utility;
- Needle offload percentage and downstream cognitive gain.

## Promotion law

No personalization mechanism is promoted because it feels personal.

Promote only when it improves real Andreas outcomes while preserving or improving held-out general capability and respecting evidence, privacy, authority and reversibility constraints.

## Hard invariant

**Andreas research is an open-ended longitudinal evidence stream that continually reshapes Frank's curriculum, memory and evaluation. Known Andreas usecases are seeds, not boundaries. The scientific target is a Frank that becomes progressively better for Andreas everywhere while remaining a general, transferable Clay-native cognitive entity.**
