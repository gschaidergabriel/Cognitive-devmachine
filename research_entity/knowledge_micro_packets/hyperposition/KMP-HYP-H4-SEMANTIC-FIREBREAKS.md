### Core
The owner-fixed Clay-native target should use one semantic language but multiple non-interchangeable semantic domains. `FACT/EXACT`, `OBSERVATION`, `ACTIVATION_PRIOR`, `PROPOSAL`, `FORM`, `AUTHORITY/CAPABILITY` and `EFFECT_INTENT` are candidate classes, not final taxonomy.

### Rule
Cross-class conversions are explicit trusted operations. Activation cannot silently become truth; proposal cannot silently become authority; form/data cannot silently materialize as executable/effectful behavior.

H4 synthetic cascade tests support privilege/effect blast-radius containment under a constructed typed policy. A concurrent C6 simple 8B prompt-injection proxy was non-discriminating: typed and untyped forms both answered 8/8 correctly. Therefore textual tags alone are not evidence; runtime enforcement is the hypothesis.
