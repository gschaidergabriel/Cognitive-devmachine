## Why this emerged

Six current lanes independently exposed variants of the same structural failure class:

1. **Storage/boot:** V3 could correctly identify the private SQLite object while import-capable Python had already been influenced by system-site startup. The target-supported V4 successor moves the boundary earlier to `python -I -S`, but real service/entrypoint composition remains open.
2. **Capabilities:** a pathname was not enough identity; same-path byte replacement could leave stale successful tool trials actionable. The candidate binds trials to executable bytes/filesystem identity and rechecks at use.
3. **Needle:** provider/adapter output may describe authority-like fields but cannot overwrite the externally admitted authorization/reservation/execution-generation/capability bindings.
4. **Reward:** valid terminal receipts are rereadable. Without an exactly-once consumption rule, persistent plasticity can be applied more than once despite valid source provenance.
5. **Heldout/training data:** binding a semantic-audit receipt hash closes one lineage gap, but the receipt's content/correctness still needs verification. Identity-of-evidence and validity-of-evidence are distinct.
6. **GWT:** on accepted target, caller-spliced episode/signal/causal identities still reached DONE and learning. Causal ancestry must therefore be producer/storage/receipt enforced rather than trusted because the identifiers are present.

UnifiedDB/ClayGraph adds the composition lesson: separately green repaired-runtime runs do not imply an atomic whole-product run.
