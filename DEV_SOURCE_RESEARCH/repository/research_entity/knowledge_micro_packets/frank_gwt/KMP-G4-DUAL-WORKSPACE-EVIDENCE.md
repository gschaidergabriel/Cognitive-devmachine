### Core
A stronger FRANK GWT test should combine two orthogonal channels where technically possible: **external causal episode evidence** (identity, order, broadcast/effect/outcome/re-entry receipts) and **latent-state evidence** (selectivity, holding, downstream accessibility and causal perturbation of privileged model representations).

### Evidence / boundary
G3/G4 validators show that causal receipts are much stricter than stage labels, but receipts can still validate an engineered protocol without proving internal model dynamics. Recent 2026 interpretability work reports a small privileged verbalizable representation space with functional global-workspace properties in language models. That work concerns other models and does not establish FRANK consciousness or FRANK workspace dynamics.

### Implication
Agreement between event-level and latent-level interventions would make a stronger mechanistic case than either alone. Disagreement is also informative: it can reveal a ceremonial external workspace, hidden internal routing, or an incorrect mapping between them.

### Next test
Instrument one pinned model/runtime and preregister a 2×N factorial combining external re-entry lesions with latent workspace perturbations, measuring downstream state and task effects while keeping phenomenal-consciousness claims explicitly out of scope.
