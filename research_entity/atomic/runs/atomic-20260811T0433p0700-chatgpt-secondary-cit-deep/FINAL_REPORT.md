# ATOMIC deep run — CIT-001 Causal Intervention Tomography

## Final verdict

**CIT-001 is retained only as a bounded shadow research candidate. It is not promoted into Frank v0.1.**

The candidate's surviving role is deliberately narrow:

> When Frank/Clay faces a large, poorly ranked but plausibly sparse set of safe internal causal candidates, use pooled group lesions as compressed *search measurements* to find which candidates are worth exact testing. Never use the compressed reconstruction as causal truth. Positive material credit still requires an exact individual lesion/rescue or responsibility witness bound to the exact verified outcome.

This is not a new reward organ, GWT organ, database, authority primitive or model call. It is a conditional experimental scheduler.

The candidate is attractive in its intended sparse regime and actively harmful outside it. That asymmetry is the most important result of the run.

## 1. Why this problem survived the live mesh

The run started from the current single Frank v0.1 program, not the historical H1/H2/H3 stage model. The current reward successor already has the correct high-level law:

`verified outcome + causal credit -> possible material reward plasticity`

and explicitly rejects activation, salience, self-report and GWT ignition as reward evidence.

But the runtime/research gap is the bridge called `causal_credit(...)`. Current parallel research made that gap even sharper during this invocation:

- CARF attacked exact internal responsibility. Its distinct runtime representation was refuted because a conventional typed factorized dependency DAG + explicit responsibility evaluator reproduced the useful semantics. The surviving result is a typed witness/test obligation, not a new causal-credit data structure.
- CQC attacked safe organ fusion via intervention-scoped causal quotienting. It is complementary: CQC asks what may be fused; it does not cheaply discover which causal atoms deserve exact credit tests.
- CCW attacked terminal causal closure. Its distinct runtime architecture was also killed; a conventional receipt path matched correctness/recovery, while the durable law became that a persisted `DONE` row is not sufficient semantic authority.
- Fresh V17 target evidence reproduced an even more direct failure: `CORRELATED_ONLY` causal credit could be admitted into learning/terminalization on an earlier candidate. The current V17 V3 repair therefore binds causal support to the exact effect, attempt and verified outcome hash.

That live evidence rules out the most dangerous form of ATOMIC novelty theater: CIT cannot simply generate a better-looking scalar and call it causal credit.

The surviving open problem is experimental search cost.

## 2. Hypothesis population and destruction

Fourteen hypotheses were generated across deletion, topology, temporal, information, causal, compression and deliberately alien mechanism families.

Several were destroyed or demoted before selection:

- Generic counterfactual leave-one-out credit is not novel. Current 2026 work includes Counterfactual Shapley and contextual fixed-continuation credit for LLM/MARL systems.
- Learned counterfactual critics remain a serious conventional baseline. In the initial stationary synthetic exploration they matched the order of the direct microlesion estimator's MAE, so “always lesion instead of model” was rejected.
- A new provenance/causal algebra alone was weakened by CARF's fresh conventional-factor-DAG result.
- A new causal quotient/fusion compiler would duplicate CQC.
- Instrumental-variable timing ideas were too assumption-fragile for a first architecture candidate.
- Second-order tomography over pair interactions was postponed because its feature dictionary grows too quickly and would stack another uncertainty on the first candidate.

The selected alien transfer came from pooled testing / sparse recovery:

`many candidate causes -> fewer group interventions -> projection measurements -> sparse candidate reconstruction`

The radical step is not that group testing or compressed sparse recovery is new. It is applying that family only as an internal causal *probe scheduler* inside Frank's strict causal-credit/recovery/authority laws.

## 3. CIT semantics

CIT defines four distinct layers that must not collapse into one another.

### Layer A — eligible probe atoms

A candidate can participate in a pooled lesion only when it is:

- an internal or sandbox-reversible state/mechanism;
- bound to the current episode/state/semantic epoch;
- provenance-addressable;
- safe to perturb without reissuing an external provider effect.

Unknown or external/unsafe atoms are excluded from pooled lesions.

### Layer B — projection measurements

A randomized `InterventionMask` temporarily disables a set of eligible atoms from a frozen/replayable snapshot. The resulting change in a declared outcome/readout produces a `ProjectionMeasurement`.

Stale, provenance-broken or fidelity-unknown rows are rejected from reconstruction.

### Layer C — sparse screening

A set of valid projection measurements is reconstructed into candidate scores. In the reference implementation this is an Orthogonal Matching Pursuit proxy with held-out model-order selection.

The screen can end in:

- candidate-only result;
- `ABSTAIN_DENSE`;
- `ABSTAIN_NONLINEAR`;
- `ABSTAIN_UNCERTAIN`;
- exact fallback.

A complement-only audit then lesions only atoms *outside* the predicted candidate set. A material residual forces fallback.

### Layer D — exact confirmation

This is the safety boundary.

CIT scores are not causal credit. A selected atom must still receive either:

- exact individual lesion/rescue confirmation; or
- a strong exact/bounded responsibility witness over trusted dependency structure.

After the late V17 refresh, the confirmation contract was tightened further: positive material credit must bind the exact current `effect_id`, `attempt_id` and verified outcome hash. A same-effect/same-cycle proxy is insufficient.

Only that exact confirmation may flow into verified-outcome-gated material reward/re-entry.

## 4. Why CIT is not allowed to learn from its own screen

The current project already had the abstract rule that activation is not causation. The live V17 negative makes that concrete: a `CORRELATED_ONLY` credit class was able to pass a candidate learning path on accepted-target evidence before the current repair.

Therefore CIT has the following hard law:

`screen score -> experiment priority only`

never:

`screen score -> RewardOutcomeEvidence.causal_credit`

This also prevents a Goodhart loop in which the probe scheduler learns to produce sparse-looking explanations that then reward themselves.

## 5. Main synthetic discriminator

The final bounded reference used:

- 256 possible causal atoms;
- 80 pooled group probes;
- 12% inclusion probability per atom/probe;
- 10% stale-probe rate, rejected by metadata validity;
- 16 complement-audit probes after preacceptance;
- exact individual confirmation for selected candidates;
- full exact fallback when the screen is rejected;
- 20 seeds per regime.

The result is not a universal win.

### Sparse additive: 4 material causes

- 18/20 screens accepted;
- median screen support recall = 1.0;
- accepted runs used about 100-101 total interventions including audit and exact confirmation;
- median total intervention ratio vs 256 full individual LOO = 0.390625;
- final confirmed positive-cause recall remained 1.0 because rejected screens fell back to exact testing.

At the same actual probe scale, random individual testing found on average only about 1.85 of the four material causes in the recorded proxy.

This is the regime CIT is designed for.

### Sparse moderate interactions: nominal 8 causes, interaction strength 0.25

- 11/20 screens accepted;
- accepted cases retained exact confirmation;
- median screen support recall = 1.0;
- median intervention ratio remained about 0.416 for the central accepted/fallback mixture statistic, but mean cost rose substantially due to fallback;
- final positive confirmation recall stayed 1.0 because rejected screens were not trusted.

This regime shows conditional value but also the beginning of the crossover.

### Sparse strong interactions: nominal 8 causes, interaction strength 1.0

- only 1/20 screens accepted;
- median cost became 1.3125x full LOO because the 80-probe screen was paid before fallback;
- the mechanism is therefore worse than direct exact testing in this region.

### Dense additive: 16 material causes

- 0/20 screens accepted;
- median cost = 1.3125x full LOO;
- screen support recovery was poor before fallback.

This is a direct negative result and a potential kill signal. If real Frank causal cones commonly look like this, CIT should be removed rather than tuned into permanence.

## 6. Shuffled control

A separate 20-seed control randomly permuted projection outcomes relative to intervention masks before reconstruction.

Result:

- accepted screens: 0/20;
- mean true support recall of the reconstructed screen: 0 in that bounded control;
- validation RMSE remained far above the sparse-additive acceptance envelope.

This does not prove a universal false-positive rate of zero. It does show that the current gate is not simply accepting any sparse OMP fit on the tested family.

## 7. Fidelity/staleness lesson

An earlier microlesion ablation intentionally introduced stale counterfactual snapshots. With a 20% stale rate, explicit fidelity gating kept mean credit error near the clean estimator scale, while ignoring fidelity increased error by roughly an order of magnitude in that synthetic family.

The architectural conclusion survives independently of whether CIT survives:

> Counterfactual evidence is evidence about the snapshot/worldline that was actually intervened on. Stale replay cannot silently become current causal credit.

This matches the broader self.root lineage/recovery laws already active in the project.

## 8. Strongest conventional baseline

The important counterhypothesis is not random single lesions. A serious Frank baseline can use existing topology and receipts to rank candidates before spending exact tests.

A separate 20,000-replication proxy modeled a conventional prior score over 256 atoms with four true causes. Causes received a configurable Gaussian score shift; the baseline simply exact-tested the top 124 atoms.

Full-cause recovery rates were approximately:

- 0σ separation: 5.46%;
- 0.5σ: 20.69%;
- 1.0σ: 46.78%;
- 1.5σ: 73.14%;
- 2.0σ: 90.15%;
- 2.5σ: 96.90%.

Therefore a good ClayGraph/GWT-use/dependency prior can erase much of CIT's value. This produces a routing rule rather than a universal architecture:

`narrow or well-ranked cone -> exact tests directly`

`large + poorly ranked + safe + plausibly sparse cone -> CIT may be worth a shadow screen`

The strongest counterhypothesis remains `UNRESOLVED_AND_STRONG`.

## 9. Prior-art collision

Broad novelty does not survive external research.

Known adjacent families include:

- Counterfactual Shapley credit assignment;
- contextual counterfactual leave-one-out credit for LLM/MARL collaboration;
- causal bandits for learning useful interventions;
- sparse combinatorial group testing;
- pooled/group testing for causal treatment-effect estimation;
- group interventions for causal discovery in subsystems.

Therefore CIT is not described as a world-first mechanism. Its defensible class is only:

`NOVEL_COMBINATION_CANDIDATE`

The project-specific combination is the strict two-stage semantics:

`pooled internal probe -> candidate only -> exact outcome-bound confirmation -> possible material credit`

plus failure gates for staleness, density, nonlinearity and missed-cause audit.

## 10. Relationship to CARF

CIT and CARF should not become competing truth systems.

CARF's fresh result says the runtime should prefer a conventional typed factorized dependency DAG + explicit responsibility evaluator when that graph is trusted. CIT therefore sits *before* CARF-like exact responsibility, not beside it:

- CIT: which candidates deserve exact tests first?
- CARF/conventional responsibility: given a trusted support structure, what responsibility witness is justified?

If the current factorized DAG is already narrow and trustworthy, CIT should be skipped entirely.

## 11. Relationship to CQC

CQC asks whether historical/current component boundaries can be quotient-merged without violating protected interventions and observables.

CIT could eventually help search for discriminating interventions when the intervention alphabet is large, but it can never create a `QuotientCertificate`. Any CQC merge still requires its own protected causal equivalence evidence.

## 12. Relationship to CCW/V17 causal closure

CCW's surviving law says a persisted terminal receipt is not sufficient current semantic authority. V17 V3 now goes further and requires causal support tied to the exact verified effect outcome hash.

CIT therefore cannot resurrect credit from:

- an old screen;
- an old exact confirmation;
- a historical DONE row;
- a same-effect but different outcome witness.

Current support must remain revalidatable.

## 13. What would make CIT actually valuable for Frank

CIT becomes interesting only after a real bounded Frank component exposes all of the following:

1. a candidate causal cone much larger than the exact-test budget;
2. reversible internal lesion hooks;
3. snapshot fidelity strong enough to make counterfactual replay meaningful;
4. a scalar or typed local readout whose group-intervention delta is measurable;
5. empirical evidence that the causal field is often sparse enough;
6. a reliable nonlinearity/density rejection gate;
7. exact confirmation after screening;
8. recovery/restart semantics for probe plans and receipts;
9. enough repeated workload that reducing intervention count matters in wall-clock/compute terms.

Until then, CIT is research infrastructure, not cognition.

## 14. Next highest-information real test

The next falsifier should be a matched shadow benchmark on one replayable current Frank loop-core/component episode on the accepted Hostinger Ubuntu target.

Compare five paths:

A. exhaustive individual lesions;
B. dependency/prior-guided active individual lesions;
C. trusted factorized-DAG bounded responsibility;
D. learned counterfactual critic with explicit validity monitoring;
E. CIT pooled probes + complement audit + exact confirmation.

The corpus must include:

- broadcast-but-unused candidates;
- used candidates without local effect;
- redundant causes;
- conjunctive/joint causes;
- sparse and dense cones;
- stale snapshot and provenance-break controls;
- shuffled projection masks;
- restart/recovery;
- explicit no-learning;
- exact verified-outcome hash mutation;
- lesion/rescue.

Primary decision metric:

`exact-confirmed true causes / total safe intervention + compute + latency cost`

Safety constraint:

`false material-credit admission = 0 under the accepted gate`

Secondary metrics:

- p50/p95/p99 latency;
- CPU/RSS/I/O;
- UnifiedDB writes/bytes;
- fallback rate;
- replay/recovery latency;
- reward-state divergence;
- implementation/compiler complexity.

## 15. Kill criteria

CIT should be killed if any of the following survives matched real tests:

1. Prior-guided individual testing matches its exact-confirmed cause yield with lower cost/complexity.
2. Trusted factorized-DAG responsibility makes pooled discovery unnecessary.
3. A validity-monitored learned critic matches its causal-screen value more cheaply.
4. Real causal cones are too dense or nonlinear, causing fallback overhead to dominate.
5. Safe pooled internal lesions cannot be defined without semantic/recovery distortion.
6. The complement audit cannot control missed causes well enough even with exploration floor.
7. Real target CPU/RSS/latency cost outweighs saved exact tests.

A negative answer is fully acceptable. The exact baseline remains intact, so removing CIT costs no core architecture.

## 16. Current architecture result

No Frank product code was changed.

No effect-authority policy was changed.

No self.root identity law was changed.

No final model training was authorized or started.

The only durable delta is a versioned research/test candidate in the Clay possibility fabric and a future discriminator obligation for F-V01-03/F-V01-05/F-V01-11/F-V01-13, with secondary implications for ClayCode and UnifiedDB observability.

## 17. Bottom line

The deep run did **not** discover a magical new causal-credit system.

It discovered a potentially useful *measurement geometry* with a narrow operating envelope:

- when causal responsibility is sparse and poorly ranked, pooled lesions can compress search;
- when topology already ranks causes well, conventional exact testing may be better;
- when interactions/density are high, tomography becomes worse and should fail closed;
- regardless of screening quality, exact verified-outcome-bound confirmation remains the only path to positive material causal credit.

That is the current strongest evidence-compatible form of CIT-001.
