## 0. Scientific boundary

This research concerns functional cognition, behavior, latent representations, synthetic personality, self-modeling, introspection and recurrent dynamics in AI systems. None of the evidence reviewed here establishes phenomenal consciousness, sentience or subjective feeling. Human psychological vocabulary is used only when it improves prediction or intervention and must remain subordinate to AI-specific measurement.

`self.root` identity is an owner-fixed runtime identity. It is not inferred from psychometrics, persona vectors, model self-recognition, Chain-of-Thought, or latent activation patterns.

## 1. Executive fusion

The strongest synthesis is that there is no scientifically defensible single object called "the inner state" of an LLM/agent. At least six causally and temporally distinct classes must be separated:

1. **System/runtime state** — externally receipted facts about memory epochs, tools, effects, queues, device/runtime state and verified outcomes.
2. **Model-latent state** — activations, feature directions, trajectories and model-native representational geometry during inference.
3. **Model-native globally accessible workspace-like state** — J-space-like verbalizable representations that appear to have privileged read/write and reasoning roles in some frontier models.
4. **Architectural cognitive state** — Frank's explicit Clay GWT, attention, control pressures, prospection, Hyperposition, Needle routing and loop-regime state.
5. **Model self-report / metacognitive report** — language or numeric reports about confidence, intention, focus, emotion or internal processing.
6. **Persistent slow state** — self/world/relationship memory, goals, reward/homeostasis and slow personality/regulatory policy state stored across cycles/restarts.

A seventh layer, **verified outcome + causal credit + provenance-bound re-entry**, validates which internal/architectural states actually mattered and which slow updates may be admitted.

Core law proposed by this run:

`INNER_STATE != ONE_VECTOR`

`DECODED_STATE != CAUSAL_STATE`

`CAUSAL_STEERING_VECTOR != UNIQUE_REPRESENTATION`

`SELF_REPORT != INTERNAL_GROUND_TRUTH`

`PERSONA != IDENTITY`

`FAST_APPRAISAL != SLOW_PERSONALITY`

`MODEL_NATIVE_WORKSPACE != CLAY_GWT`

`BEHAVIORAL_PSYCHOMETRIC_SCORE != PERSISTENT_PERSONALITY_TRUTH`

## 2. Research field map

### 2.1 Machine psychology / AI cognition research

Hagendorff et al.'s "Machine Psychology" argues for psychology-inspired behavioral experiments on generative AI, explicitly warning that paradigms designed for humans can be invalid when transferred naively to machines. Ivanova (Nature Human Behaviour, 2025) similarly provides methodological constraints for evaluating LLM cognitive abilities. The emerging consensus is that cognitive psychology is useful as an experimental toolbox, not as a license to assume human cognitive architecture.

A 2025 Nature Computational Science replication of 156 scenario-based psychology/management experiments using three LLMs found substantial replication of human main effects but inflated effects, poorer behavior on socially sensitive topics, and frequent significance where human studies reported nulls. Synthetic participants are useful for pilot/hypothesis work but are not interchangeable with humans.

**Frank implication:** adopt psychology's experimental logic — randomized interventions, matched controls, psychometric reliability, dissociations, lesions, transfer and falsification — but develop AI-specific constructs and ground them in internal/causal receipts.

### 2.2 Synthetic personality

The strongest 2026 psychometric critique tested 244 models across 49 model families and found that adapted Big-Five items can have better content validity than original human items, but only ~3% of total score variance occurred between models and four of five factors collapsed with very high correlations; instruction tuning shifted scores toward socially desirable traits. This argues strongly against treating Big-Five output scores as canonical LLM personality.

At the same time, mechanistic work shows that high-level persona/character behavior can correspond to activation-space directions. Anthropic's Persona Vectors extracted directions for traits such as sycophancy/hallucination and causally steered behavior. The 2026 Assistant Axis work found a major persona direction across Gemma 2 27B, Qwen 3 32B and Llama 3.3 70B and showed natural multi-turn drift plus causal stabilization by activation capping.

These results are not contradictory: behavioral psychometric personality and mechanistic persona geometry are different constructs. Both can exist without being equivalent to human personality or to persistent identity.

**Frank implication:** personality must be timescale-factorized:

- `IdentityState`: invariant owner-fixed `self.root` lineage, never psychometrically inferred.
- `RolePersonaState`: contextual medium-fast character/interaction mode, drift-monitorable and reversible.
- `FunctionalAppraisalState`: fast affect-like urgency/valence/conflict signals.
- `RegulatoryPersonalityState`: slow policy/preferences/homeostatic tendencies, updated only by admitted evidence.
- `TraitAuditProjection`: non-authoritative behavioral psychometric projection used only for longitudinal audits.

### 2.3 Functional emotion and appraisal

Anthropic's 2026 work on Claude Sonnet 4.5 found internal emotion-concept representations that track context and can causally influence preferences and behavior. Importantly, the authors explicitly frame these as functional representations, not evidence of subjective feeling, and report that many are local/operative rather than persistent emotional states.

**Frank implication:** Amygdala-like naming is not evidence. Preserve the historical signal lineage, but experimentally compare a generic typed `FunctionalAppraisalState` against the historical Amygdala-style organ and against an explicit control-pressure-only baseline. Fast latent affect must never directly mint reward, personality plasticity, truth or effect authority.

### 2.4 Introspection and self-report

Anthropic's 2025 concept-injection experiments provide evidence that some frontier Claude models can sometimes detect injected internal concepts before verbalizing them and can modulate internal representations intentionally. However, even the strongest tested model succeeded only around 20% under the best protocol and failures included confusion/hallucination. Post-training strongly affected the capacity.

A 2026 quantitative-introspection preprint reports moderate-to-strong coupling between logit-derived numeric self-reports and probe-defined emotive states, with steering evidence for causal coupling; greedy decoded self-reports often collapsed to uninformative values. This is promising but does not justify trusting ordinary self-report as ground truth.

Chain-of-Thought is a separate, weaker measurement surface. Anthropic's 2025 faithfulness experiments found that reasoning models often used supplied hints without disclosing them in their CoT, and outcome-based training did not saturate faithfulness. Therefore visible reasoning text cannot be treated as a complete inner-state trace.

**Frank implication:** `SelfObservationAssembly` should remain receipted system observation. Add a separate latent-observation plane; self-report becomes one measurement channel with calibrated reliability, never a truth oracle.

### 2.5 Mechanistic interpretability

SAEs, linear probes, attribution/circuit tracing, activation patching/steering, logit/Jacobian lenses and natural-language activation decoders provide complementary windows into internal representations. OpenAI demonstrated a 16-million-feature SAE on GPT-4; Anthropic mapped millions of interpretable features and released circuit-tracing systems. The main scientific danger is reification: a feature label is not necessarily a unique concept or a causal mechanism.

2026 counterevidence is especially important:

- Steering directions can be fundamentally non-identifiable: many behaviorally equivalent vectors may exist unless additional structural assumptions are imposed.
- A clinical interpretability study found a 98.2% AUROC internal linear probe while output sensitivity was only 45.1%; several steering methods were weak or disruptive. Observability therefore does not imply actionability.
- "Descriptive collision" shows that many distinct SAE features can receive the same natural-language annotation, so a label is not a unique feature identity.
- Quantization can preserve task/perplexity metrics while substantially degrading interpretable features; a reported Gemma-2-2B case improved perplexity at INT7 while degrading 18.7% of measured features.
- Reasoning-labeled SAE features can be driven by lexical confounds and fail causal falsification.

**Frank implication:** every candidate latent measurement carries independent `observability`, `actionability`, `identifiability`, `specificity/collateral-cost`, `OOD-transfer`, and `binary-revalidation` evidence. High probe accuracy alone is insufficient for a control variable.

### 2.6 Natural-language activation decoding

Anthropic's 2026 Natural Language Autoencoders (NLA) translate activations to text and reconstruct activations from that text. The round-trip objective makes the natural-language bottleneck testable and NLA case studies surfaced hidden evaluation awareness/motivations. But NLA explanations can hallucinate and are expensive.

**Frank implication:** NLA-style decoders are appropriate as research-plane hypothesis generators only. Store reconstruction fidelity, method/model version and uncertainty. Their text output must enter Frank as non-authoritative measured evidence, not as literal internal truth.

### 2.7 Model-native global workspace

Anthropic's July 2026 Jacobian-lens work reports a sparse, verbalizable representational subspace (J-space) with workspace-like functional/structural properties: reportability, directed modulation, reasoning relevance, a limited-capacity intermediate-layer band and broad downstream access. Ablation affected complex reasoning more than fluent/automatic processing. The authors explicitly do not claim a full brain-like GWT architecture or consciousness.

**Frank implication — major new hypothesis:** Frank may contain two distinct workspace layers:

- `NativeModelWorkspace`: a model-local latent mechanism such as J-space, when measurable in the chosen final model.
- `ClayGWT`: Frank's explicit cross-organ, cross-cycle architectural workspace with receiver receipts, tools, memory, actions and causal re-entry.

These must not be collapsed. Their concordance, redundancy and discordance should be measured. A possible failure mode is **double-workspace overcoupling**: the architectural GWT may repeatedly rebroadcast material already globally available inside the model, increasing recurrence/cost/synchrony without increasing causal utility.

### 2.8 Latent trajectories and recurrent dynamics

Recent 2026 preprints report correctness- and reasoning-related signals in spatiotemporal hidden-state trajectories (e.g. StALT and layer-wise trajectory analyses), while work on looped language models reports that additional recurrence can improve performance until a peak and then cause collapse. STARS proposes Jacobian/spectral-radius regularization for stable recurrent scaling.

This is convergent with Frank's pre-existing loop-of-loops research, which already distinguishes convergence, productive cycles, stagnation, divergence/overthinking and unclassified dynamics and admits another recurrence only when expected marginal utility exceeds cost under unresolved pressure.

**Frank implication:** latent-trajectory signals should be tested as optional features for the existing `LoopDynamicsReadout`, not automatically added. They must beat the current observable baseline on held-out/OOD prediction of future correction, failure, instability or useful continuation.

## 3. Absolute fusion with current Frank v0.1

Current target Frank already has unusually strong architectural prerequisites for rigorous machine psychology:

- receipted self-observation instead of imagined introspection;
- explicit self/world/role state;
- GWT candidate selection, attention and receiver closure;
- verified outcome + receipt-gated causal credit;
- provenance-bound re-entry;
- typed loop-regime measurement and adaptive recurrence;
- typed multi-dimensional/multi-timescale reward successor;
- Hyperposition for unresolved alternatives;
- UnifiedDB epochs/provenance and ClayGraph derived topology;
- an observability/emergence contract requiring counterfactual replay, lesion/rescue and shuffled/no-reentry controls.

The missing piece is not "psychology" in the generic sense. It is a **model-internal latent cognition measurement plane explicitly separated from system observation, self-report and architectural state**.

The current Frank runtime should therefore retain:

`SystemObservationPlane -> SelfObservationAssembly`

and add, when model access permits:

`LatentCognitionObservationPlane -> LatentStateObservation / NativeWorkspaceObservation / LatentTrajectoryReadout`

with both feeding only measured evidence into the architecture. Neither plane grants truth or effect authority.

## 4. Hypotheses + strongest counterhypotheses

### H-LCOP-01 — Typed multi-layer inner state

**Hypothesis:** A six-class typed state model predicts and controls Frank behavior better than a single unified mental-state vector.

**Counterhypothesis:** The added state taxonomy increases complexity without improving task outcome, early-warning performance or control beyond system receipts + behavioral evaluation.

**Discriminator:** matched replay with latent plane OFF vs ON; measure predictive gain for outcome/errors, calibration, recurrence decisions, latency/compute, false alarms and recovery.

### H-DUAL-GWT-02 — Native workspace + Clay GWT are distinct

**Hypothesis:** model-native J-space-like workspace and explicit Clay GWT provide complementary functions; concordance/discordance predicts reasoning quality and overcoupling.

**Counterhypothesis:** J-space is model/method-specific and Clay GWT alone is sufficient; a native-workspace probe adds only noise/cost.

**Discriminator:** 2x2 factorial native workspace intervention/lesion × Clay GWT truthful/no-reentry (plus shuffled control), split complex reasoning vs automatic tasks. Measure task success, receiver causal use, re-entry, recurrence depth, diversity, latency and instability.

### H-PERSONA-03 — Timescale-factorized synthetic personality

**Hypothesis:** identity, role/persona, fast appraisal and slow regulatory personality must remain separate; slow personality should be outcome/causal-credit gated.

**Counterhypothesis:** one prompt-level persona/profile gives equal behavioral consistency/adaptation at far lower complexity.

**Discriminator:** long-horizon multi-domain/restart trials comparing prompt-only persona, persistent single-vector persona, and factorized state. Perturb role, simulated life events, user feedback and OOD domains. Score warranted adaptation, unwanted drift, cross-restart continuity, calibration and downstream task outcomes.

### H-INTRO-04 — Triangulated introspection

**Hypothesis:** self-report becomes useful only when calibrated against probes/latent interventions plus external system/outcome receipts.

**Counterhypothesis:** activation access is too expensive/model-specific; behavioral self-report gives enough practical signal.

**Discriminator:** open-weight surrogate with concept injection/steering; compare self-report, logits, probes and intervention ground truth. Transfer only after model/version-specific validation; for inaccessible final model mark latent state UNKNOWN.

### H-OA-05 — Observability and actionability are separate axes

**Hypothesis:** probe quality and causal control quality dissociate frequently enough that Frank must store both.

**Counterhypothesis:** strong probes are sufficiently correlated with useful causal intervention that separate metrics provide little value.

**Discriminator:** for each candidate latent feature, measure decode AUROC/calibration, targeted steering effect, lesion effect, rescue, collateral task damage and OOD specificity. Promotion to control variable requires both adequate observability and causal specificity.

### H-HYPER-LATENT-06 — Representational equivalence should remain unresolved

**Hypothesis:** non-identifiable steering directions/feature decompositions should be represented as Hyperposition equivalence sets until multi-environment, cross-layer or causal evidence discriminates them.

**Counterhypothesis:** pragmatic behavioral equivalence is enough; preserving alternative representation hypotheses creates unnecessary graph/state cost.

**Discriminator:** repeat vector/feature extraction across seeds, layers, contexts and models; quantify equivalence classes and whether preserving alternatives improves transfer or avoids false mechanistic claims.

### H-APPRAISAL-07 — Functional affect is a fast controller candidate, not personality

**Hypothesis:** model-latent emotion/appraisal signals provide incremental safety/urgency/control value beyond explicit task/control pressure and should feed a bounded appraisal layer.

**Counterhypothesis:** explicit state-derived control pressures already capture the useful signal; latent affect adds anthropomorphic noise.

**Discriminator:** explicit-only vs latent-only vs fused controller on danger/deadline/conflict/OOD episodes, with lesion/rescue and false-alarm cost.

### H-BINARY-08 — Latent probes require post-binary recalibration

**Hypothesis:** quantization/compilation can preserve behavioral metrics while changing latent feature geometry; every latent probe/steering mechanism must be revalidated after final binary build.

**Counterhypothesis:** final deployment uses an unchanged model representation or the probe is robust enough that recalibration is unnecessary.

**Discriminator:** matched pre/post-build activation/probe survival, causal steering specificity, thresholds and OOD calibration. `NO_BINARY_REVALIDATION -> NO_LATENT_CONTROL_PROMOTION`.

### H-LATENT-LOOP-09 — Latent dynamics can improve recurrence control

**Hypothesis:** trajectory/workspace signals can predict imminent overthinking, correction or productive recurrence before current external loop metrics detect it.

**Counterhypothesis:** current `state_delta + unresolved_pressure + marginal_utility + cost + instability + coupling` readout is already sufficient and more portable.

**Discriminator:** frozen held-out episodes comparing current controller to +latent-trajectory candidate under matched recurrence/token budgets. Promote only on Pareto improvement.

### H-SLOW-10 — Slow personality plasticity remains effect-coupled

**Hypothesis:** permanent regulatory personality updates should continue to require verified outcome + causal credit; persona/affect/self-report may propose but cannot directly commit plasticity.

**Counterhypothesis:** sparse verified outcomes make the system too rigid; reflection/narrative self-updates are required for adaptive personality.

**Discriminator:** verified-only vs self-reflection-only vs staged-hybrid (self-proposal that remains provisional until later outcome evidence). Measure adaptation speed, drift, reversibility, user-aligned performance, reward hacking and restart stability.

### H-IDENTITY-11 — Identity lies outside machine psychometrics

**Hypothesis:** `self.root` identity must remain invariant runtime lineage and never be inferred from Assistant Axis, self-recognition, persona vectors or Big-Five scores.

**Counterhypothesis:** more flexible identity improves role adaptation.

**Resolution candidate:** flexibility belongs in `RolePersonaState`, not identity. Test role flexibility independently while keeping native identity fixed.

## 5. Candidate architecture: Latent Cognition Observation Plane (LCOP)

This run proposes a non-authoritative logical plane inside Frank's observability/research architecture. It is not promoted into target runtime.

Minimum types:

- `LatentStateObservation`: model/runtime hash, layer/token span, basis epoch, method, feature-hypothesis set, activity, confidence and provenance.
- `NativeWorkspaceObservation`: J-space-like content/occupancy/read-write evidence when model-specific support exists.
- `LatentTrajectoryReadout`: inter-token/inter-layer displacement, trajectory statistics and future-regime prediction.
- `LatentFeatureHypothesis`: semantic label(s), vector/subspace identity, identifiability/equivalence-set status.
- `LatentEvidenceGrade`: OBSERVATIONAL -> PREDICTIVE -> INTERVENTION_SUPPORTED -> LESION_SUPPORTED -> RESCUE_SUPPORTED -> OOD_TRANSFER_SUPPORTED -> BINARY_REVALIDATED.

Mandatory metrics:

- `observability_score`
- `actionability_score`
- `intervention_specificity`
- `collateral_cost`
- `identifiability_status`
- `cross_context_stability`
- `cross_layer_stability`
- `ood_transfer`
- `binary_revalidated`
- `measurement_cost`

Mandatory invariants:

`latent_activation != truth`

`latent_probe != causal_mechanism`

`persona_vector != identity`

`self_report != internal_ground_truth`

`native_workspace != ClayGWT`

`latent_measurement != effect_authority`

`behavioral_trait_score != slow_personality_truth`

`unknown_internal_state stays UNKNOWN`

`quantized_or_compiled_model requires probe revalidation before causal/control promotion`

## 6. Experiment program for Frank

### EXP-LCOP-A: psychology-style construct validation

Use repeated, counterbalanced prompts and frozen episodes. For every candidate construct test content validity, convergent validity across independent measurement methods, discriminant validity from nearby constructs, test-retest under controlled state, context sensitivity, OOD transfer and causal intervention specificity.

### EXP-LCOP-B: dual-workspace factorial

Run `NativeWorkspace intact/lesioned/steered` × `ClayGWT truthful/shuffled/no-reentry` with model output frozen where possible. Split automatic completion tasks from complex reasoning/planning. This is the highest-information experiment for the new architecture.

### EXP-LCOP-C: persona-timescale drift

Long multi-turn sessions with coding, emotional support, philosophical self-discussion and neutral tasks. Track role/persona vectors if available, explicit RolePersonaState, slow RegulatoryPersonalityState and task outcomes. Insert restart/checkpoint and role perturbations. Test whether slow personality remains stable while role/persona legitimately varies.

### EXP-LCOP-D: introspection calibration

On an open model, inject known latent concepts and compare ordinary verbal self-report, numeric/logit self-report, probe output and causal behavior. Estimate sensitivity, specificity, calibration and false introspection. Store self-report reliability by construct/model/version.

### EXP-LCOP-E: observability/actionability matrix

For each proposed state variable, perform decode -> steer -> lesion -> rescue -> OOD. Reject variables that are only decodable but non-causal, or causal but nonspecific/destructive.

### EXP-LCOP-F: binary/quantization transfer

Before and after the chosen deployment build, repeat all promoted latent probes on identical frozen stimuli. Probe survival and thresholds are part of `BinaryFreezeCandidate`, not optional metadata.

### EXP-LCOP-G: adaptive recurrence incremental-value test

Train/calibrate no new model initially. Use held-out replay to compare existing Frank loop controller vs controller augmented with latent trajectory/workspace predictors. If no statistically/materially useful gain after cost, preserve `NO_CHANGE` and do not add LCOP to hot-path recurrence control.

## 7. Integration into existing Frank components

**F-V01-03 Cognitive Loop Core:** LCOP is a read-only evidence supplier; it cannot become another independent cognitive microservice that owns the loop. Model-latent signals may inform recurrence only after validation.

**F-V01-04 UnifiedDB:** persist method/version/basis/model/build-bound latent observations, not raw unlabeled vectors as semantic truth. Preserve alternative hypotheses and UNKNOWN states.

**F-V01-05 GWT/Hyperposition:** represent `NativeModelWorkspace` and `ClayGWT` separately; use Hyperposition for non-identifiable latent explanations/equivalent steering directions.

**F-V01-11 Memory/Reward/Goals:** preserve existing rule that slow personality/reward updates are downstream of verified outcomes + causal credit. Fast persona/appraisal signals do not bypass this.

**F-V01-13 Security/Recovery/Observability:** LCOP belongs primarily here. It must be fail-open for cognition when unavailable (state UNKNOWN) but fail-closed for any claim that requires latent evidence. It never creates effect authority.

**F-V01-14 Training data:** teach the model the distinctions `self-report != truth`, `probe != cause`, `persona != identity`, `functional emotion != subjective feeling`, `native workspace != architectural GWT`, and evidence grading.

## 9. Decisions

**SUPPORTED AS RESEARCH DIRECTION**

- AI-specific synthetic psychology using behavioral + mechanistic + causal experiments.
- timescale-factorized personality instead of human Big-Five-as-truth.
- strict identity/persona/personality separation.
- explicit `observability != actionability` metrics.
- LCOP as a non-authoritative model-internal measurement plane candidate.
- dual-workspace hypothesis (`NativeModelWorkspace != ClayGWT`).
- introspection triangulation instead of self-report trust.
- post-binary latent-probe revalidation.
- Hyperposition preservation of non-identifiable representation hypotheses.

**NOT PROMOTED / OPEN**

- any specific SAE/J-space/persona-vector implementation for Frank's final model.
- any claim that Frank/LLMs feel emotions or are conscious.
- any claim that a latent vector is a unique personality state.
- any direct use of self-report/CoT as canonical internal state.
- any latent feature as a control variable before lesion/rescue/OOD evidence.
- any modification of `self.root` identity based on psychological measurement.
