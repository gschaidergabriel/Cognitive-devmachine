## 0. Research question

Reconstruct what the feedback/control loop in the measured original Frank actually was, distinguish that from the loop it would have needed to be if its historical organ set had been perfectly coupled, then fuse current recurrent-reasoning/control/credit-assignment research into the smallest coherent feedback law for the target Clay-native one-module Frank loop core.

This note does **not** claim that a mathematically perfect universal cognitive controller exists. “Perfect” here means the best currently supportable engineering target: outcome-grounded, causally closed, adaptively recurrent, multiscale, observable, lesionable, recoverable, resource-aware and robust to stagnation/overthinking while preserving authority boundaries.

### 1.1 Historical feedback/control organs

**Thalamus** (`services/thalamus.py` ancestry): nine channels with gain and habituation, novelty/burst/attention/salience-breakthrough mechanisms, cognitive modes, and vigilance coupling into inhibition threshold.

**Amygdala** (`services/amygdala.py` ancestry): fast lexical/threat/salience path plus somatic-marker association; category + urgency outputs.

**ACC/conflict**: conflict-monitor role contributing a distinct control-pressure signal.

**Nucleus accumbens**: phasic and tonic reward state, reward-prediction error, wanting/liking separation, opponent-process dynamics, repetition boredom and habituation.

**Global workspace broadcast** (`services/workspace_broadcast.py` ancestry): BODY/PERCEPTION/ALARM/MOOD/MEMORY/THINKING_MODE competition; ignition threshold 0.55; one coherent focus; 60 s historical cadence; historical warning that workspace removal in 2026-03 caused months of silent cognitive disconnection.

**Consciousness daemon** (`services/consciousness_daemon.py` ancestry): historical loops for workspace update, mood recording, idle thinking, prediction, consolidation, feature training, perception feedback, experience space, attention control and goal management.

**Response analyzer**: explicit historical role `OUTPUT_FEEDBACK_TO_STATE`.

**Experience vector**: novelty score + annotations `novel|drift|shifting|recurring`.

**E-PQ**: slow homeostatic temperament variables precision/risk/empathy/autonomy/vigilance with event-weighted updates.

**Memory consolidator + dream daemon**: slower multi-timescale consolidation/replay.

**Needle**: historical tool-selection reflex, separate from effect authority.

### 1.2 What was missing from the original as a fully closed controller

The ancestry proves many feedback components existed, but does not establish one end-to-end causally receipted loop from observation through action to verified outcome and provenance-bound re-entry.

The central historical weakness is therefore best characterized as **fragmented feedback closure** rather than absence of recurrence.

Likely consequences to test rather than assume:

1. asynchronous services could observe different semantic epochs;
2. 60 s workspace cadence could be too coarse or phase-misaligned for request/tool feedback;
3. reward/state updates could be correlated with active state without sufficiently exact causal-use evidence;
4. separate databases/processes could turn re-entry into stale or duplicated propagation;
5. process-local workspace semantics could diverge from organism-level broadcast semantics;
6. local regulatory variables could exist without a common residual/error target;
7. loops could remain active because cadence existed, not because another recurrence had positive expected value.

These are hypotheses requiring H1 reconstruction/replay evidence.

## 2. Counterfactual baseline — what the old Frank loop would have been if “perfect”

A critical new experimental baseline is proposed:

`ORIGINAL_ANCESTOR` -> `PERFECTED_ANCESTOR` -> `CLAY_ONE_MODULE_LOOP_CORE`

The **PERFECTED_ANCESTOR** keeps historical organ/service boundaries as much as possible but repairs feedback semantics. This avoids a major confound: if the target one-module implementation beats the original, the gain might come from correctly closing the loop rather than from module fusion itself.

### 2.1 Perfected-ancestor causal cycle

The old organ set, if coupled ideally, would execute approximately:

`Observation/Event`
-> `epoch-locked state estimate (self/world/memory)`
-> `goal + residual/uncertainty/control-pressure vector`
-> `candidate/possibility generation`
-> `local Thalamus-like gain/habituation routing`
-> `workspace competition / ignition only when cross-organ coordination is worth its cost`
-> `intent + prediction + Needle tool/reflex route`
-> `external authority/commit boundary`
-> `action/effect`
-> `independent/material outcome verification`
-> `prediction innovation/residual`
-> `receipt-gated causal credit`
-> `bounded fast state update`
-> `slow homeostatic/personality/memory consolidation where appropriate`
-> `provenance-bound re-entry into the exact next semantic epoch`
-> `loop-regime classification + continue/halt/reseed/escalate/damp/rollback decision`.

### 2.2 Historical organ -> perfected controller mapping

- Thalamus gain -> per-route/per-loop gain, not global importance truth.
- Thalamus habituation -> refractory/damping for repeated nonproductive activation.
- Amygdala urgency -> deadline/risk/time-budget modifier, not truth authority.
- ACC conflict -> residual/conflict pressure component.
- NAcc phasic -> short-horizon outcome/value innovation / persistence evidence.
- NAcc tonic -> slower persistence/disengagement prior.
- GWT ignition/focus -> expensive cross-organ recurrent-compute admission/continuation router.
- Experience-vector novelty -> stale-attractor/anti-stagnation evidence and bounded reseed trigger.
- Response analyzer -> verified outcome + prediction-error compiler.
- E-PQ -> slow priors/regularizers only; never a fast unbounded actuator.
- memory/dream -> deferred consolidation/replay with source/outcome provenance.
- consciousness daemon -> multirate scheduler/supervisor; its historical process boundary is a candidate for later fusion/distribution.
- Needle -> low-latency internal route/reflex selector with typed receipts; external commit authority remains separate.

### 2.3 Timing law

The perfected ancestor should be **event-driven and multirate**, not a single global tick.

Each loop family keeps its natural cadence/budget. A cross-loop synchronization point is created only when causal interaction requires it. Tool outcomes can reopen the exact originating causal episode asynchronously. Slow loops must not block fast loops; fast loops must not silently mutate slow durable state without admission.

Historical 60 s workspace cadence remains a baseline measurement, not a target constant.

## 3. External primary-source research fused on 2026-08-10

### 3.1 Recurrent depth gives useful extra computation but overthinking is real

Kohli et al. (2026), **Loop, Think, & Generalize: Implicit Reasoning in Recurrent-Depth Transformers**, arXiv:2604.07822, show depth extrapolation by scaling inference-time recurrence and identify overthinking where excessive recurrence degrades predictions.

Transfer to Frank: recurrence count must be an adaptive control variable, not an unconditional virtue.

### 3.2 Looped models often approach cyclic fixed-point structure

Blayney et al. (2026), **A Mechanistic Analysis of Looped Reasoning Language Models**, arXiv:2604.11791, report cyclic recurrence in which layers can approach distinct fixed points and attention behavior stabilizes; recurrent block size, input injection and normalization affect stability.

Transfer to Frank: internal state trajectory/regime should be measured, but fixed-point convergence is not itself the objective.

### 3.3 Spectral stability can prevent recurrent-state explosion

Prairie et al. (2026), **Parcae: Scaling Laws For Stable Looped Language Models**, arXiv:2604.12946, frame looping as a nonlinear time-variant dynamical system and link instability in a linear approximation to large spectral norms in injection parameters; they stabilize looped models with constrained injection dynamics.

Transfer to Frank: local finite-difference Jacobian/spectral-radius proxies are useful tripwires for fixed-point-like local regimes, especially for runaway coupling, but cannot classify useful metastable/limit-cycle cognition alone.

### 3.4 Adaptive recursion is preferable to uniform depth when inputs differ

Bae et al. (NeurIPS 2025), **Mixture-of-Recursions: Learning Dynamic Recursive Depths for Adaptive Token-Level Thinking**, use lightweight routers to allocate different recursion depths to different tokens while sharing recurrent layers.

Graves (2016), **Adaptive Computation Time for Recurrent Neural Networks**, arXiv:1603.08983, established learned per-input computation depth/halting.

Transfer to Frank: recurrence budget should be local/typed and conditioned on unresolved pressure, expected benefit and resource cost, not globally fixed.

### 3.5 Planning time itself has cost

Lin et al. (2015), **Metareasoning for Planning Under Uncertainty**, arXiv:1505.00399, formalize the tradeoff between planning cost and expected policy improvement.

Transfer to Frank: halting/continuation is a metacontrol decision: another recurrence is justified only by expected marginal value.

### 3.6 Long-horizon agents need denser, more local credit than final outcome alone

Tan et al. (2026), **Hindsight Credit Assignment for Long-Horizon LLM Agents**, arXiv:2603.08754, use post-hoc hindsight to refine step-level credit.

Tao et al. (2026), **TRACE: Turn-level Reward Assignment via Credit Estimation for Long-Horizon Agents**, arXiv:2607.13988, represent agent rollouts as tool-call-boundary state transitions and derive dense turn-level rewards from temporal-difference changes in state values.

Transfer to Frank: Frank's receipt chain enables a stronger engineering version of local credit: only states/actions with exact causal-use receipts enter candidate credit; temporal-difference/hindsight estimates can then refine how much credit they receive. Outcome success must never reward every broadcast-active state.

### 3.7 Hierarchical inference supports different timescales/abstractions

Van de Maele et al. (2026), **Schema-based active inference supports rapid generalization of experience and frontal cortical coding of abstract structure**, arXiv:2601.18946, use hierarchical models linking abstract structure to lower-level state/action dynamics.

Transfer to Frank is limited but consistent with keeping fast local control and slower abstract/self/goal loops distinct while coupling them through typed grounded interfaces.

### 3.8 GWT-style recurrence supports ignition, broadcast and top-down maintenance — not truth

The project already treats GWT as a selection/broadcast/use/re-entry observability plane. Existing GNW computational work supports the general mechanism that recurrent top-down/bottom-up connectivity can create thresholded ignition and sustained representations; this does not establish consciousness in Frank and must not be used as such.

## 4. New proposed control law — Verified Adaptive Recurrent Loop Core

The target one-module core should implement **one causal loop with typed local subloops**, not one scalar global controller.

### 4.1 State carried at cycle t

Let the semantic control state include:

- belief/state estimate `z_t` with uncertainty and provenance;
- current goal/constraint vector `g_t`;
- residual vector `r_t` = unmet goal, prediction error, uncertainty, conflict, safety/authority constraints, unresolved external dependency;
- resource state `c_t` = latency/tokens/CPU/RAM/GPU/tool/API budgets;
- stability state `d_t` = state delta, gain/coupling estimates, phase/oscillation, recurrence depth, stagnation/overthinking evidence;
- alternative set/hyperposition `H_t`;
- causal episode identity `e_t`.

No one scalar replaces this vector.

### 4.2 Predict-before-act

For each candidate action/recurrent operation `a_i`, estimate:

- expected outcome distribution;
- expected residual reduction;
- expected information gain;
- cost;
- instability/overcoupling risk;
- reversibility/recovery cost;
- authority admissibility.

This creates feed-forward anticipation before feedback correction.

### 4.3 Workspace admission

GWT/workspace admission should occur when expected benefit from cross-organ availability exceeds local handling cost. Ignition is not required for every local update.

Workspace must preserve the chain:

`candidate -> selected -> broadcast -> addressable -> delivered -> reconstructed -> used -> local effect -> verified outcome -> credit -> admitted learning -> re-entry`.

### 4.4 Effect boundary

Model/core/Needle/workspace outputs are proposals. Consequential effect execution remains outside semantic cognition authority and must pass explicit capability/security/commit gates.

### 4.5 Outcome verification and innovation

After an action/effect or internal computation yields an observable result:

`innovation_t = verified_outcome_t - predicted_outcome_t`

where subtraction is typed/task-specific, not necessarily numeric.

Maintain separate:

- outcome success/failure;
- prediction error;
- uncertainty change;
- constraint violations;
- causal-use receipts.

Self-evaluation is acceptable only where external verification is impossible and must carry lower evidence class.

### 4.6 Receipt-gated causal credit

Candidate credit requires exact causal episode/provenance plus evidence that the state/action was actually used in the causal path.

Then estimate credit using one or more of:

- direct verified local effect;
- temporal-difference change across state transitions;
- hindsight counterfactual estimate;
- paired rerollout/branch intervention when high-impact;
- lesion/rescue differential;
- shuffled/no-reentry controls.

Credit should be typed and uncertainty-bearing. Correlated activation alone receives no durable learning authority.

### 4.7 Two-phase learning

**Fast/provisional update:** bounded short-lived adaptation for routing/attention/prediction within the episode.

**Durable update:** only after verified outcome + causal credit + semantic admission. Slow personality/homeostatic/memory updates use lower learning rates, evidence accumulation and rollback checkpoints.

This prevents one surprising event from rewriting durable identity/state.

### 4.8 Adaptive recurrence / halting law

Candidate semantic decision:

`continue iff E[marginal_verified_outcome_gain + information_gain] > compute_cost + instability_risk + opportunity_cost`

**and** unresolved material pressure remains.

Explicit regime actions:

- low state_delta + low residual -> candidate `CONVERGING_FIXED_POINT`; halt/commit if no higher-value pending dependency;
- bounded nonzero cycle + continuing verified utility -> `PRODUCTIVE_LIMIT_CYCLE`; preserve while marginal value remains positive;
- low state_delta + high residual -> `STALLED`; do **not** halt as success; retrieve new evidence, reopen Hyperposition, reseed, change route or escalate loop timescale;
- growing state/update/residual/cost or falling quality -> `DIVERGING_OR_OVERTHINKING`; damp gain, shorten budget, break phase lock, rollback or route alternate;
- insufficient evidence -> `CHAOTIC_OR_UNCLASSIFIED`; fail conservatively and increase observation before durable action.

### 4.9 Anti-windup / hysteresis / refractory controls

To avoid oscillation and controller chatter:

- clamp bounded gains and update magnitudes;
- use hysteresis around ignition/halting thresholds;
- refractory periods for repeated identical low-yield candidates;
- separate deadline urgency from truth confidence;
- prevent accumulated unresolved pressure from directly saturating effect authority;
- reset/integrator anti-windup after rollback or impossible goals;
- maintain last-known-good state and explicit recovery paths.

### 4.10 Receding-horizon prospection

Prefer short bounded prospection, act/verify one consequential step, then replan from the new verified state rather than executing a long speculative plan open-loop.

This is a semantic analogue of receding-horizon/model-predictive control and reduces compounding model error.

## 5. Synthetic mechanism checks run for this note

These are deterministic/synthetic sanity checks only, not Frank runtime evidence.

### 5.1 Adaptive recurrence versus fixed budgets

A seeded synthetic population of 20,000 heterogeneous tasks was generated with saturating useful recurrence, task-specific optimal depth, optional quadratic overthinking penalty and per-recurrence cost `lambda=0.018`.

Policies:

- fixed 4 recurrences;
- fixed 8;
- fixed 12;
- adaptive one-step marginal-gain policy using noisy local gain estimates (`noise_sd=0.008`), max depth 16.

Results:

| policy | mean quality | mean net utility | mean recurrences | p95 recurrences |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| fixed4 | 0.6449 | 0.5729 | 4.00 | 4 |
| fixed8 | 0.5940 | 0.4500 | 8.00 | 8 |
| fixed12 | 0.1386 | -0.0774 | 12.00 | 12 |
| adaptive | 0.7000 | 0.6021 | 5.45 | 9 |

Noise robustness for the adaptive policy remained above fixed4 net utility across tested local gain noise SDs 0.000, 0.004, 0.008, 0.016 and 0.030; at SD 0.030 mean net utility was 0.5903.

Interpretation: in this constructed heterogeneous family, adaptive marginal-value control allocates more recurrence only where useful and avoids much of the overthinking tail. The result validates the *testability* of the control criterion, not its Frank performance.

### 5.2 Why delta-only convergence is unsafe

A separate seeded synthetic set of 30,000 regime samples contained converging, stalled, productive-cycle and diverging states. A delta-only rule `state_delta < 0.02 => converged` falsely marked 41.5% of non-converging samples as converged because stalled states were deliberately constructed with low delta but high residual. Adding `residual < 0.05` eliminated that false-convergence class in this construction while preserving all constructed converging samples.

Interpretation: the distinction `low delta + high residual = STALLED` is not cosmetic; it prevents a structurally predictable false halt.

## 7. Strongest counterhypotheses

- **H0-PA1:** PERFECTED_ANCESTOR gains vanish when timing/epoch overhead is measured; the one-module architecture is needed mainly for transaction/latency reasons.
- **H0-PA2:** Adaptive recurrence adds controller noise and false halts; a small fixed recurrence budget plus explicit escalation is superior.
- **H0-PA3:** Local spectral diagnostics predict numerical behavior but have little relation to task utility; only outcome-gated metacontrol matters.
- **H0-PA4:** Receipt-gated credit is too sparse; hindsight/TD credit introduces bias that harms durable learning. A hybrid sparse-durable + dense-provisional scheme may be necessary.
- **H0-PA5:** Historical Thalamus/NAcc/Amygdala variables are redundant once residual/uncertainty/value are represented directly; neuro-inspired variables should be fused away after lineage tests.
- **H0-PA6:** One executable module hides failure domains and worsens recovery despite semantic elegance; internal isolation/checkpoint boundaries must remain explicit.
- **H0-PA7:** GWT is unnecessary for most cycles; local typed message passing plus rare global arbitration dominates continuous workspace recurrence.
- **H0-PA8:** Hyperposition reseeding increases instability/branch explosion more often than it rescues stagnation.
