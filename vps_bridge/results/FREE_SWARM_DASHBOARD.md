## What `go` triggers

1. VPS sandbox / bridge / storage preflight; repair first if degraded.
2. Read current `main`, supervisor and authority state.
3. Run specialized GO research roles for falsifiers, patches, tests, benchmarks, architecture deltas and open questions.
4. Research Distillery recursively decomposes open questions, harvests external mechanisms, filters and compresses candidates.
5. Evidence Forge reproduces candidate repos / patches in disposable environments and measures them.
6. Accepted knowledge is reinjected into later GO contexts; verified deltas enter the common evaluation ledger.
7. Dashboard recalculates concurrency, tokens, conversion funnel, quality, costs and GO pass-through.
