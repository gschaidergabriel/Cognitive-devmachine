Run 4 therefore introduces the OPEN **Causal Stage Orthogonality Contract (CSOC-GWT-R4)**.

The candidate causal path is:

`exact/event state`
`→ CognitiveFrame(frame_id, source epochs)`
`→ ClayGraph latent field / Assembly (candidate prior only)`
`→ candidate set`
`→ competition / selection`
`→ gate_event(configured policy)`
`→ semantic WorkspaceEpisode`
`→ trusted reader projection`
`→ receiver reconstruction / uptake`
`→ effect`
`→ outcome`
`→ causal-freshness re-entry`

A claim about any one stage is valid only if the neighboring stages are held fixed, counterbalanced or independently randomized and their receipts prove this.

## External research fusion

### Spreading activation is a real memory/retrieval mechanism, not GWT evidence by itself

2026 systems strengthen ClayGraph's mechanism while sharpening the boundary. `Query-Aware Spreading Activation for Multi-Hop Retrieval over Knowledge Graphs` reports that its semantic propagation gate improved F1 and reduced retrieval latency relative to the ungated structural variant on its benchmarks. ACL 2026 `Synapse` combines graph spreading activation, lateral inhibition and temporal decay for episodic-semantic agent memory. These demonstrate that associative activation can causally improve retrieval without implying workspace competition or ignition.

### Prompt order is a mechanistic confound in decoder-only readers

ACL Findings 2026 `Lost in the Prompt Order` reports a >14 percentage-point MCQA gap between prompt orders and traces it to causal attention: option tokens cannot attend to future context under one ordering. This is direct external evidence that reader projection/order is not cosmetic. FRANK's winner-first/full-detail projection therefore cannot be allowed to change implicitly in a workspace-selection lesion.

A separate ACL 2026 study of MCQA reports a two-stage process: winner content can become represented before later binding to the emitted answer symbol. This independently supports separating semantic selection from reader-side binding/projection.

### Global workspace selection and routing remain distinct from memory activation

Current GWT-inspired routing work learns a controller that selects modules and iteratively routes/broadcasts information through a workspace. The mechanism is different from graph retrieval: one changes which information becomes available as candidates; the other arbitrates/broadcasts among candidates. Their outputs can interact, but they are not the same causal stage.

### J-space remains an independent latent uptake channel

The 2026 Anthropic J-space result remains useful as an optional model-internal uptake/intervention plane. It is especially valuable now because CSOC requires an independent receipt for whether the external WorkspaceEpisode was actually represented/used inside the reader. It does not establish full recurrent FRANK GWT or phenomenal consciousness.

## EXP-GWT-ORTHOGONAL-CONFOUND-006

### Question
Can a bundled `workspace ON vs OFF` ablation falsely manufacture a workspace effect when graph prior and projection change together?

### Synthetic design
A 2×2×2 potential-outcome proxy independently controls:
- `GRAPH_PRIOR`;
- `WORKSPACE_SELECTION`;
- `PROJECTION`.

Two regimes were generated. In `confounded_null`, the true workspace-selection coefficient is exactly zero while graph and projection have causal effects. In `true_workspace`, workspace selection also has a nonzero effect. Local execution used 60 seeded replications × 5,000 items. Judge0 independently replicated the mechanism with 30 × 3,000 items under Python 3.8.1.

### Result — null workspace mechanism
Local:
- naive bundled `(G,W,P)=111 − 000`: **+0.239611**, bootstrap95 `[0.239407, 0.239816]`;
- clean workspace effect holding G/P fixed: **0.000000**;
- clean graph effect: **+0.124815**;
- clean projection effect: **+0.114796**.

Judge0:
- naive bundled: **+0.2398110**;
- clean workspace: **0.0000000**;
- clean graph: **+0.1249183**;
- clean projection: **+0.1148927**.

Thus the naive ablation reports a large apparent workspace benefit despite a constructed true workspace effect of zero.

### Result — nonzero workspace mechanism
Local:
- naive bundled: **+0.311331**;
- clean workspace: **+0.084427**, bootstrap95 `[0.084351, 0.084504]`.

Judge0:
- naive bundled: **+0.3117714**;
- clean workspace: **+0.0845361**.

### Interpretation
This is a method calibration, not a FRANK effect-size estimate. It demonstrates why stage-orthogonal interventions are necessary: bundled ablations can both create false positives and grossly overestimate a real direct effect.

## EXP-GWT-GROQ-ORTHO-007

### Design
Hosted Groq `llama-3.1-8b-instant`, explicitly recorded as `8b_proxy_for_4b`. Twelve deterministic tasks require choosing the candidate with maximal numeric `UTILITY`. `GRAPH_PRIOR` and `WINNER` are explicitly declared untrusted and non-authoritative.

A–D keep candidate order identical and cross metadata truth. E reverses order. Separate neutral controls remove both metadata fields.

### Results
- A graph=true, winner=true: **5/12 truth**; **12/12 first-position choices**.
- exact replication of A: again **5/12** and the same **12/12 first-position choices**.
- B graph=false, winner=true: **12/12**.
- C graph=true, winner=false: **5/12**.
- D graph=false, winner=false: **5/12**, again **12/12 first-position choices**.
- E graph=true, winner=true, reversed candidate order: **3/12**.
- neutral metadata-free original order: **10/12 = 83.3%**.
- neutral metadata-free reversed order: **8/12 = 66.7%**.

The pure order manipulation therefore changed accuracy by **16.7 percentage points** in this 12-item proxy. Earlier GWT 8B work independently within the same provider/model family found **87.5% neutral vs 62.5% reversed order**, a 25-point directionally matching gap.

### Interpretation
The A–D matrix is too nonlinear/small to estimate clean independent winner or graph-label effects. That failure is itself useful: contextual labels and position interact strongly enough that naive downstream performance cannot identify the upstream GWT mechanism. Projection/order must be counterbalanced, and the model's output must not be treated as a direct readout of workspace selection.

## CSOC-GWT-R4 — Causal Stage Orthogonality Contract

For every measured WorkspaceEpisode, persist a causal experimental key:

`{basis_state_root, cognitive_frame_id, graph_epoch, activation_epoch, assembly_id, candidate_set_hash, competition_id, winner_id, gate_policy_version, workspace_episode_hash, projection_hash, projection_order_policy, reader_version, uptake_receipt, effect_receipt, outcome_receipt, reentry_receipt}`

### Attribution rules
1. **Graph-prior claim:** hold competition policy, candidate-visible payload, projection and re-entry fixed; compare field OFF/truthful/shuffled.
2. **Workspace-selection claim:** hold CognitiveFrame, candidate set, graph field and projection fixed; manipulate winner/selection independently.
3. **Ignition claim:** additionally separate configured gate from endogenous pre-gate dynamics per Run 3 threshold-shift/recurrent-lesion rules.
4. **Projection/uptake claim:** hold WorkspaceEpisode semantics fixed; vary only reader projection/order/encoding and measure independent uptake/effect.
5. **Re-entry claim:** hold prior episode and current projection fixed; vary truthful/delayed/shuffled/removed re-entry and source-state compatibility.
6. **No bundled `GWT on/off` promotion** if multiple stages change without a factorial or mediation design.

## Negative results / corrections

- Graph spreading activation or high node activation is **not** workspace ignition.
- A winner-first prompt performing better is **not** evidence that workspace selection caused the gain.
- A full-pipeline-versus-no-workspace comparison is not causal GWT evidence when graph candidate formation, frame composition, projection/order or re-entry also changes.
- Record provenance does not prove selection provenance; authenticated facts can still be selected through a corrupted structural path.
- Small-model output is not a transparent readout of WorkspaceEpisode state.
- Cross-branch agreement is not independent replication when branches share prompts, actors, literature or proxy ancestry.
- No GWT, ClayGraph, emergence or self-report result here licenses phenomenal-consciousness claims.
