## Interpretation update

The parent cascade's phrases `sole orchestrator`, `ChatGPT alone coordinates`, and `workers do not recursively coordinate the system` mean:

- ChatGPT alone performs **global** scheduling/adjudication, final architecture acceptance/rejection, whole-cascade reprioritization and final merge;
- workers do not autonomously create an unbounded second scheduler hierarchy or promote architecture truth.

They do **not** prohibit intelligent direct worker-to-worker research coordination.

Within a bounded Frank worker batch, workers must use the repo-native Research Center as an active peer mesh. They may directly exchange evidence, questions, challenges, replication requests, handoffs and blocker notifications; inspect sibling work; form temporary subteams; merge duplicate lanes; and alter their local plan in response to material peer evidence.

Updated compact cascade:

`ChatGPT global synthesis/scheduling`
`-> bounded worker task graph`
`-> workers self-network inside Research Center`
`-> peer challenges/replications/handoffs/builds`
`-> networked worker result graph`
`-> ChatGPT global adjudication + executable validation`
`-> whole-mesh re-fusion`
`-> next bounded worker graph`

## Mandatory batch output change

A Frank worker batch is no longer represented only as a list of independent outputs. Where multiple workers were active, the batch result should preserve:

- worker/task dependency graph;
- direct peer messages that changed a decision;
- duplicate-lane merges;
- challenge/falsifier status;
- replication ancestry;
- handoffs/subteam epochs;
- unresolved contradictions;
- worker-local plan revisions caused by peer evidence.

## Hard guard

`direct peer coordination != global architecture authority`

`connected worker agreement != independent replication`

`networking quality is measured by blocker reduction, information gain, contradiction discovery and executable discrimination — not message volume`.
