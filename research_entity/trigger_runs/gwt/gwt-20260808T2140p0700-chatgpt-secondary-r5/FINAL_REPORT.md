## 3. External research delta

### 3.1 Model-internal global-workspace-like mechanism

Anthropic's July 2026 paper **Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models** provides unusually direct causal evidence for a workspace-like mechanism inside Claude. The identified `J-space` is a compact family of verbalizable latent directions that is:

- reportable and top-down modulable;
- used for unspoken intermediate reasoning;
- causally load-bearing under representation swaps;
- flexibly reused by multiple downstream computations;
- disproportionately connected to the rest of the network;
- selectively important for higher-order tasks while many automatic tasks remain intact after ablation.

Examples include swapping an internal `spider` representation for `ant`, which redirects a downstream legs answer, and swapping `France` for `China`, which redirects several distinct downstream factual computations.

This is stronger evidence for **internal workspace uptake** than ordinary prompt/output correlations. It still does not establish FRANK's external system GWT, does not demonstrate the same mechanism in Qwen-4B, and does not establish phenomenal consciousness.

### 3.2 Recurrence and externalization

The same paper notes a structural difference from biological GWT: a normal transformer is feedforward within a forward pass. On longer timescales, it can extend deliberation by externalizing intermediate state as tokens and re-ingesting it at later positions.

R5 transfers this into a falsifiable FRANK hypothesis:

> **truthful provenance-bound Clay/re-entry may function as an external recurrence scaffold when internal workspace depth is insufficient.**

The key discriminator is rescue: truthful re-entry should selectively restore multi-step performance after internal-workspace lesion, whereas shuffled/temporally invalid re-entry should not.

### 3.3 Competition is present but not equivalent to configured ignition

Anthropic's dual covert-task experiments show some capacity/competition structure: a concept plus an arithmetic result co-occupy a token much less often than a shuffled control, while both can still enter the broader workspace band. Other experiments show sharper commitment with depth. This supports competition as an internal property but does not justify equating a configured FRANK threshold with endogenous ignition.

R3/R4's ignition boundary therefore survives: configured `gate_event` remains operational policy unless an endogenous nonlinear/recurrent transition is independently demonstrated.

### 3.4 Projection remains a separate causal stage

ACL Findings 2026 reports >14 percentage-point MCQA differences from prompt order and traces the effect to causal-attention information availability. This independently reinforces R4: the model-facing serialization of a WorkspaceEpisode can change behavior even if upstream semantics are unchanged.

### 3.5 Biological GNWT remains contested

The 2025 large preregistered adversarial test of GNWT and IIT challenged important predictions of both theories. Biological GWT should therefore remain a mechanism source and experimental analogy, not a settled template that FRANK merely copies.

## 4. New synthesis: Nested Workspace Bridge (NWB-R5)

R5 proposes the OPEN architecture/research distinction:

### Level A — system workspace

`WorkspaceEpisode`

Functions:
- cross-organ candidate competition;
- bounded global availability;
- semantic winner identity;
- audience routing;
- effect/outcome/re-entry receipts.

### Level B — model-internal workspace

`InternalWorkspaceReceipt` / J-space-like uptake plane

Functions to test:
- whether the projected episode becomes a privileged latent representation;
- whether that representation causally mediates reasoning/action;
- whether capacity/competition appears internally;
- whether lesion can be rescued by truthful external re-entry.

These levels can interact but must never be conflated. A correct system winner may fail to enter the model's internal workspace; an internal workspace may form from information that was never selected by system GWT; and either can influence output without granting external execution authority.

## 5. Current-run method calibration

### EXP-GWT-R5-NESTED-IDENTIFIABILITY-001

A seeded 300,000-case synthetic factorial independently crossed:

- ClayGraph-like field: OFF / TRUTHFUL / SHUFFLED;
- system workspace: OFF / ON;
- internal workspace proxy: OFF / ON;
- re-entry: OFF / TRUTHFUL / SHUFFLED.

Constructed orthogonal contrasts recovered separate effects:

- system workspace: **+0.10045**;
- internal workspace proxy: **+0.12839**;
- truthful graph vs off: **+0.12522**;
- shuffled graph vs off: **−0.07926**;
- truthful re-entry vs off: **+0.12915**;
- shuffled re-entry vs off: **−0.13250**.

But a bundled `all-good − all-off` comparison produced **+0.49029**, mixing all causal stages and interactions.

The constructed truthful-re-entry rescue was larger with internal workspace OFF (**+0.17679**) than ON (**+0.08177**), demonstrating that the proposed rescue interaction is statistically identifiable in a factorial design.

**Boundary:** this experiment validates the experimental design, not the existence or magnitude of these effects in FRANK, Qwen, Claude or humans.

## 6. Hypotheses and counterhypotheses

### H-GWT-R5-001 — Nested workspace mediation

**Hypothesis:** system `WorkspaceEpisode` effects on complex Qwen decisions are partly mediated by a privileged model-internal workspace representation.

**Prediction:** holding candidate set/projection fixed, system selection changes downstream behavior most strongly when the selected content is measurable in the internal workspace; internal-workspace lesion attenuates the effect.

**Counterhypothesis:** the reader acts through ordinary distributed transformer computation and a J-space-like readout adds no mediating explanatory power.

**Falsifier:** system workspace effects persist unchanged after targeted internal-workspace intervention, or no stable privileged uptake plane can be identified in target Qwen.

### H-GWT-R5-002 — External recurrence rescue

**Hypothesis:** truthful Clay/re-entry can compensate for limited internal workspace depth by externalizing intermediate state and reintroducing it later.

**Prediction:** after internal-workspace lesion, truthful re-entry selectively rescues multi-step tasks; shuffled/revoked/temporally torn re-entry does not.

**Counterhypothesis:** any rescue is ordinary extra-context/token-budget benefit independent of causal continuity.

**Falsifier:** matched shuffled re-entry rescues equally, or the effect disappears under equal token/content controls.

### H-GWT-R5-003 — Candidate topology precedes workspace attribution

**Hypothesis:** collection/query semantics and ClayGraph activation can alter candidate membership strongly enough to dominate apparent workspace effects.

**Prediction:** fixing `WorkspaceEpisode` policy while varying only typed collection/field semantics changes candidate availability but not the direct selection coefficient.

**Counterhypothesis:** upstream differences are negligible on real FRANK workloads.

**Falsifier:** target traces show candidate sets invariant across realistic query/lowering alternatives under matched semantics.

### H-GWT-R5-004 — Internal competition is capacity-bounded but not a configured threshold

**Hypothesis:** target Qwen exhibits capacity/competition in a privileged latent plane, but its transition dynamics are distinct from FRANK's configured system gate.

**Prediction:** dual-task internal uptake shows interference/capacity structure even when external gate configuration is fixed.

**Falsifier:** no selective capacity/competition is detectable, or it is fully explained by projection/token competition.

### H-GWT-R5-005 — Self-model separability

**Hypothesis:** workspace functionality and the trained assistant self-model are separable mechanisms.

**External prior:** Anthropic observes the workspace-like structure already in a pretrained model before a stable assistant identity is installed.

**Falsifier:** in target models the claimed workspace measure appears only after persona/self-model training and vanishes when self-related features are controlled.

## 7. ClayGraph interaction

### Supported

- ClayGraph sparse activation is a plausible associative **candidate-prior** mechanism.
- Its output can causally alter what becomes available before workspace competition.
- G12 collection-topology results increase the need to hash/query-bind candidate formation.

### Challenged

- graph spreading/activation is not system workspace ignition;
- retrieval success is not broadcast/uptake/effect evidence;
- authentic graph ancestry does not prove cognitive relevance;
- a truthful field must beat shuffled-field controls under equal visible facts/order/token budget.

### Unresolved

The decisive joint experiment is:

`GRAPH_FIELD {off, truthful, shuffled}`
`× SYSTEM_WS {off, on}`
`× INTERNAL_WS {intact, lesioned}`
`× REENTRY {off, truthful, shuffled}`

with candidate facts, projection order, token budget, reader version and effect policy pinned.

## 11. Final synthesis

GWT research in FRANK has moved from a broad `broadcast workspace` idea to a causal measurement stack. R5's main contribution is to add a missing inner boundary:

> **selection by FRANK is not the same thing as uptake by the model.**

The most promising path is now measurable end-to-end:

`candidate formation → system selection → projection → internal privileged uptake → effect → outcome → truthful re-entry`.

Anthropic's 2026 causal intervention evidence makes the internal leg of this chain technically plausible and experimentally accessible; their released Qwen-compatible Jacobian-lens tooling makes a direct target-model test realistic. The project should therefore prioritize `EXP-LIVE-GWT-NESTED-QWEN` over another architecture-only GWT cycle.
