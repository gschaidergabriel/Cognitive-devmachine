# KMP-HYP-H5-NARY-REDUCIBILITY

- **epistemic class:** OPEN mechanism/architecture constraint
- **claim:** Higher-order semantic identity must survive lowering, but native hyperedges are not universally required.
- **allowed forms:** intrinsic pairwise; proven weighted-pairwise reduction; typed relation-node/incidence graph; native hyperrelation.
- **H5 local result:** naive entity-pair co-occurrence retained ~0.999 recall but mean precision fell from 0.99895 at 5k relation instances to 0.17179 at 100k because false completions accumulated.
- **external boundary:** peer-reviewed 2026 work demonstrates exact pairwise reduction for a class of higher-order dynamics; other work shows naive graph expansions can lose higher-order information.
- **rule:** choose the simplest operation-specific topology with an equivalence proof/receipt. Lossy lowering must declare loss and cannot silently support effect-critical reasoning.
- **falsifier:** a universal pairwise lowering preserves all target role/query/effect semantics and dominates cost.
- **status:** OPEN/HOLD
