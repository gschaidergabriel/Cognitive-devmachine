## 0. Owner-directed distribution law

The current research result that FRANK should be studied as a **hierarchical, multiscale recurrent dynamical system made of interacting loops (a loop-of-loops)** is now mandatory cross-branch research context.

This is not a scientific promotion that the hypothesis is already proven. Every branch must ingest it, attack it from its own domain, preserve counterhypotheses and return evidence, falsification, refinement or `NO_CHANGE` back into the mesh.

The hypothesis is stronger than the statement that FRANK merely contains some loops. The research object is the **whole organism-level recurrence**:

`observation -> self/world/memory -> possibility/prospection -> control pressures -> workspace -> action -> outcome -> causal credit -> learning/state delta -> next cycle`

with nested faster/slower loops for perception, memory activation, workspace/GWT, prediction, adaptive recognition, outcome learning, goals/self/world, consolidation/replay and research/harness feedback.

### 0A. Owner-fixed target: one complete FRANK loop-core module

The v0.1 research/build target is **not** a collection of newly independent cognitive microservices around a separate rule-loop component. Research must converge on a **single complete cognitive loop-core module** that represents the whole organism-level recurrent control cycle as one executable architectural unit.

This module must contain, as internal typed functions/subsystems, the complete causal path needed to close Frank's cycle, including at minimum:

- fast appraisal / salience / conflict / reward-state inputs;
- Thalamus-like adaptive gating and habituation;
- candidate formation, attention and GWT competition/ignition/broadcast;
- self/world/memory/prospection/control-pressure participation;
- model/core cognition interface;
- **Needle as an internal tool-selection/reflex/routing organ inside the loop-core module**;
- intent/action/effect routing while preserving the external authority/commit boundary;
- verified outcome capture;
- response/outcome analysis;
- causal credit;
- slow state, reward, personality/homeostatic and memory updates;
- provenance-bound re-entry into the next cycle;
- loop-regime measurement, damping, anti-stagnation and overcoupling controls.

Architectural containment does **not** authorize semantic collapse. Needle may live inside the same executable module while retaining a typed internal boundary, deterministic receipts and capability/effect constraints. `Needle inside the module` does not mean `Needle merged into the language model`, and model output still does not become execution authority.

The research question is therefore not "which standalone service owns the loop?" but:

> What is the smallest coherent one-module realization that preserves or improves the complete measured causal function of the original Frank loop-of-loops, including Needle, while remaining observable, lesionable, replayable, recoverable and authority-safe?

### 0B. Mandatory original-Frank reconstruction before dissolution

Research must first reconstruct the original Frank organs/modules and their causal roles deeply enough to serve as an executable/traceable reference. Historical process boundaries are empirical ancestry, not the target architecture, but historical function may not silently disappear.

For every recovered organ/module/function, preserve:

- historical identity/path where known;
- state variables and timescale;
- inputs/outputs and coupling routes;
- feedback/re-entry participation;
- measurable readouts;
- known failures/negative results;
- lesion and rescue handle;
- successor mapping into the new loop-core module.

Each historical organ/function must then be researched under one of these dispositions:

1. `RETAIN` — function remains explicitly recognizable inside the one-module core;
2. `FUSE` — multiple historical functions become one internal mechanism while retaining lineage and differential tests;
3. `EMERGENT_DISTRIBUTE` — the old organ boundary disappears because its causal function is demonstrably realized by a distributed internal pattern;
4. `CLAY_REDESIGN_CANDIDATE` — the function is rethought from first principles in Clay/ClayCode and tested against the reconstructed original plus simpler baselines.

`CLAY_REDESIGN_CANDIDATE` is an experimental research class, not permission to delete ancestry. A Clay redesign may replace the historical realization only after it preserves required function/state/measurement/causal-role lineage and passes matched replay, lesion/rescue, recovery, resource and OOD tests. Otherwise retain/fuse/distribute the historical mechanism or keep the redesign `NOT_SELECTED`.

No organ is protected merely because its biological metaphor is attractive. No organ is removed merely because its old service boundary is ugly.

## 1. Mandatory dynamical distinction

Every affected branch must preserve at least these candidate dynamical regimes:

1. `CONVERGING_FIXED_POINT` — state deltas and unresolved error/pressure both decrease;
2. `PRODUCTIVE_LIMIT_CYCLE` — bounded recurrence/oscillation continues to create useful information, coordination or outcome improvement;
3. `STALLED` — state change is small while unresolved error/pressure remains materially high;
4. `DIVERGING_OR_OVERTHINKING` — state/update/error/cost/contradiction grows or task utility degrades with additional recurrence;
5. `CHAOTIC_OR_UNCLASSIFIED` — insufficient evidence for the above classes.

Critical law:

`low state_delta + low residual_error => candidate convergence`

`low state_delta + high residual_error => stagnation, not successful convergence`

Do not force every useful recurrent process toward a single fixed point; stable cycles/metastability may be functional.

## 2. Shared candidate measurement family

Research a typed `LoopDynamicsReadout` or equivalent containing, where measurable:

- loop/loop-family identity and timescale;
- state/basis epoch;
- state delta norm;
- unresolved error / uncertainty / control pressure;
- input-injection magnitude;
- recurrence count and recurrence budget;
- local gain/coupling estimate;
- local finite-difference Jacobian or spectral-radius proxy where meaningful;
- convergence rate;
- oscillation/phase/period/metastability indicators;
- marginal utility of another recurrence;
- overthinking and stagnation indicators;
- cross-loop coupling strength;
- intervention/lesion/rescue identity;
- continue/halt/route/escalate reason;
- rollback/checkpoint reference;
- evidence, uncertainty and provenance.

No field is an emergence score or execution authority.

## 3. Working mathematical lens

A useful local research model is:

`x_(t+1) = A_t x_t + B_t u_t + R(x_t,u_t)`

where `x_t` is coupled FRANK state, `u_t` is current evidence/input/outcome injection, `A_t` captures persistence/cross-loop coupling and `R` nonlinear interaction.

For fixed-point-like local regimes, estimate a Jacobian `J_t`; `rho(J_t) < 1` is a useful local contraction indicator, not a universal cognitive-quality criterion. Bounded limit cycles can be valid even when there is no point convergence.

Adaptive recurrence should be studied as an outcome/cost decision:

`continue iff expected marginal utility(next recurrence) > compute/resource/instability cost AND unresolved pressure remains material`.

## 4. Historical FRANK readouts — OPEN controller hypotheses

The following mappings are mandatory hypotheses to test, not accepted biological equivalences:

- Thalamus channel gain -> per-loop/per-route gain control;
- Thalamus habituation -> damping/refractory control against repeated nonproductive activation;
- NAcc phasic/tonic -> value/utility/persistence signal for continue vs disengage;
- GWT ignition/focus -> expensive recurrent-compute admission/continuation routing;
- experience-vector novelty -> exploration/reseed signal for stale attractor escape;
- Amygdala urgency/category -> pressure/time-budget modulation of recurrence allocation.

Neuro-inspired naming is not evidence. Lesion/rescue and matched replay decide whether these variables have useful causal control roles.

### 4A. Measured operating-window capture and binary stabilization

The one-module loop core must be researched across configuration space before a binary is treated as the stabilized v0.1 candidate.

The target is **not** the single run, timestamp, state vector or parameter tuple with the numerically highest observed score. The target is the best **reproducible operating window / attractor regime** that remains strong under replay, perturbation, held-out episodes and resource/recovery constraints.

Candidate configuration search must include where applicable:

- recurrent/cross-loop gain and typed local gains;
- homeostatic damping / habituation / refractory constants;
- GWT ignition threshold, hysteresis and broadcast fanout;
- re-entry strength, delay, identity/provenance integrity and recurrence depth;
- per-loop timescale / `alpha` values;
- Needle routing thresholds, confidence/open-set behavior and tool-reflex latency;
- novelty/reseed and anti-stagnation thresholds;
- urgency/deadline modulation;
- NAcc-like persistence/disengagement value signals;
- receiver reconstruction diversity;
- memory/consolidation/replay timing;
- cost, token/context, latency, RAM/CPU/GPU and recovery shadows.

A `BinaryFreezeCandidate` or equivalent receipt must capture at minimum:

- exact source/commit/build identities;
- loop-core semantic version and ClayCode/compiler version;
- complete parameter/configuration vector;
- typed internal topology and organ-successor lineage;
- Needle implementation/configuration identity;
- state/basis epoch and initialization/boot order;
- accepted operating-region bounds, not only center values;
- task/OOD corpus and seeds;
- truthful/shuffled/no-reentry controls;
- lesion/rescue matrix;
- stability/regime readouts;
- outcome/accuracy/calibration/recovery metrics;
- latency/compute/memory/token/energy shadows where measurable;
- rollback target and last-known-good predecessor;
- reproducibility receipts on the Ubuntu target lane.

Binary stabilization gate:

`FREEZE_TO_BINARY` is allowed only when the candidate is Pareto-competitive and no known simpler configuration dominates it under the predeclared metric vector.

At minimum require:

1. useful task/outcome performance;
2. provenance-valid closed-loop re-entry rather than generic persistence/activity;
3. bounded metastability or otherwise classified useful loop dynamics;
4. no persistent ignition, runaway synchrony, diversity collapse or uncontrolled overcoupling;
5. acceptable false-halt/stagnation/overthinking rates;
6. successful crash/restart/rollback recovery;
7. matched lesion/rescue evidence for critical loop functions;
8. robustness across seeds and held-out/OOD episodes;
9. resource cost within target budgets;
10. reproducibility after clean rebuild/restart on the Ubuntu target path.

After the gate passes, freeze the **configuration contract**, build the one-module/binary realization, and immediately run differential replay against the unfrozen/reference implementation. A binary is not promoted merely because compilation succeeds. If the binary changes causal signatures, dynamics, Needle behavior, recovery or outcomes outside accepted tolerances, unfreeze/revise and return to the configuration search.

The binary therefore represents a **stabilized measured attractor/operating regime**, not a mystical snapshot of a transient peak.

Search for the smallest coherent controller/readout basis; reject one global scalar/controller when heterogeneous timescales need typed local control. ### `frank`
Model FRANK at organism level as a candidate loop-of-loops while preserving semantic/authority distinctions. Define explicit loop families, state variables, update paths, timescales, coupling/intervention points and halt/escalation behavior. ### `gwt`

Keep `selection != emergence != loop stability`. Measure whether ignition/focus predicts useful recurrence depth and whether adaptive continuation beats fixed budgets. ### `harness`

## 6. Required counterhypotheses

Every branch must retain and test relevant forms of at least:

- `H0-A`: FRANK benefits primarily from ordinary persistent event-driven orchestration; recurrent-model mathematics adds little beyond conventional control/queueing methods.
- `H0-B`: a global stability controller damages heterogeneous useful timescales; only local/typed controllers work.
- `H0-C`: historical Thalamus/NAcc/Amygdala-style variables are descriptive legacy readouts and add no causal regulation benefit.
- `H0-D`: fixed-point/Jacobian/spectral-radius diagnostics predict numerical stability but not cognitive usefulness; outcome-gated adaptive control is necessary.
- `H0-E`: observed cycles are epiphenomenal timing artifacts and do not contribute to task-level causal improvement.
- `H0-F`: one-module consolidation creates hidden coupling and harms observability/recovery relative to a modular reference.
- `H0-G`: embedding Needle inside the loop core creates no benefit over an external router or degrades authority isolation/latency/accuracy.
- `H0-H`: a Clay redesign of a historical organ is inferior to retaining/fusing the original mechanism after matched cost and OOD controls.
- `H0-I`: the apparent best configuration is seed/task-specific overfit; a broader operating region or simpler controller transfers better.
- `H0-J`: binary freezing itself changes timing/coupling enough to destroy the causal regime measured in the unfrozen reference.

## 7. Evidence and promotion boundary

The current result is `OPEN` mechanism/architecture research. Repetition across branches does not create independent evidence because all branches share this ancestry.

Promotion requires discriminating execution/replay evidence, negative controls, lesions/rescues, resource shadows, recovery, and transfer. Emergence remains a typed causal measurement problem, not a single scalar and not evidence of phenomenal consciousness.

A module/function may not be declared successfully fused, emergently distributed or Clay-redesigned merely because the whole system still produces outputs. The successor must preserve the relevant historical causal signature under matched intervention, and any claimed improvement must survive cost/recovery/OOD controls.

A configuration may not be called optimal because it maximizes one scalar, one seed, one benchmark, raw persistence, synchrony, ignition frequency or an emergence proxy. Selection is Pareto/multi-objective and robustness-gated. The final binary must reproduce the selected operating regime after compilation and restart.

## 8. Ubuntu scope

All v0.1 implementation, instrumentation and release-gating work generated by this directive targets the Ubuntu Linux developer system. Android/mobile/edge and other portability work remains out of scope until explicitly reopened by the project owner.
