# FUSION 1 — current problem surface

The live Frank v0.1 frontier changed after the previous CJC ATOMIC run. CJC remains a causal-order/coordination possibility with no architecture promotion. The higher immediate blocker is ClayCode WP04: current mesh evidence reports 27 exact target+arity obligations, 26 unresolved signatures plus one effect-boundary signature obligation, and zero execution admitted when no independently justified exact `CallContract` exists. Caller constraints, type-name resemblance, basename/suffix similarity, historical role similarity and external examples are explicitly insufficient.

At the same time, owner-fixed target semantics require `self.root` server-binary identity, current semantic epoch, provenance separation and deterministic effect authority. A guessed signature can therefore become more than a compiler error: it can contaminate identity binding, effect classification, state transitions and replay.

## Problem
Can an unresolved finite CallContract domain be reduced by executable, sandboxed distinguishing probes **without inferring semantics from names**, while preserving fail-closed behavior when the oracle is weak, stale, unsafe or under-identifying?

## Strongest counterhypothesis
No. If exact authenticated static metadata is available, direct resolution dominates any tomography. If metadata is unavailable and the callable is behaviorally under-identifiable or cannot expose trustworthy distinguishing diagnostics, black-box probing cannot prove an exact contract; ECT must remain `UNKNOWN`.

## Selection criterion
A viable mechanism must show: (1) zero false contract admission under stated oracle model, (2) stale/identity mismatch rejection, (3) no effect-authority minting, (4) bounded query reduction relative to blind finite search, and (5) explicit defeat by exact trusted metadata when available.
