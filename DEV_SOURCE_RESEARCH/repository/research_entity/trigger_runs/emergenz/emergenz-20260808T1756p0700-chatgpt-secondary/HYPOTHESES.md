## H-E1-01 — Role-aware recurrence
**Mechanism:** A genuinely recurring assembly must preserve causal-role/relation identity, not merely member overlap or embedding similarity.  
**Counterprediction:** Bag-of-members recurrence is sufficient.  
**Falsifier:** role-aware identity adds no reduction in false recurrence on role-swap controls.  
**Test:** replay identical members with permuted roles; compare recurrence classifiers.  
**Affected:** hyperposition, claygraph, frank, claycode.

## H-E1-02 — Mechanistic rescue, not endpoint rescue
**Mechanism:** Rescue supports causal necessity only if the lesioned mechanism/path is restored; directly patching the output can recover present behavior while future/re-entry dynamics remain broken.  
**Counterprediction:** behavioral endpoint recovery alone is adequate rescue evidence.  
**Falsifier:** output-only rescue and mechanism restoration produce indistinguishable downstream/re-entry trajectories across OOD tests.  
**Affected:** gwt, frank, clay.

## H-E1-03 — Stage-orthogonal emergence attribution
**Mechanism:** graph prior, Assembly membership, workspace selection, projection/uptake and re-entry are separate causal stages; an emergent-organ claim is identifiable only when neighboring stages are pinned/counterbalanced.  
**Counterprediction:** bundled whole-pipeline lesion is sufficient.  
**Falsifier:** bundled and stage-orthogonal estimates converge under randomized independent stage interventions.  
**Affected:** gwt, claygraph, frank, claycode.

## H-E1-04 — Interventional synergy outranks observational nonadditivity

**Counterprediction:** positive interaction/nonadditivity on passive traces is sufficient.

**Affected:** emergenz, hyperposition, clay.

**Falsifier:** preregistered scalar alone predicts all held-out causal intervention outcomes across architectures with no profile information.  
**Affected:** emergenz, frank.
