## Digital Retina — recovered measurement lineage

Digital Retina is now grounded by multiple historical measurement generations rather than one headline value.

### May-20 working-paper regime

A dictionary-growth/held-out protocol reports 82.4% phrase coverage across 47 held-out images / 1196 phrases at `tau=0.22`, with a reported realistic-FPR-corrected lower bound of 68.8%. The final round reached 99.1% on only five images. The paper itself warns about narrow distribution and lack of compositional-binding evaluation.

### May-26 Tier-3 strict-text audit

A later honest audit reports much lower strict text coverage: 35.3% on a 12-image hard set and 47.5% on a 10-image easy set. It documents concrete failure families: YOLO class ceiling/false detections on art and AI imagery, CLIP vocabulary gaps, stylized-text OCR failures, missing composition and color/detail gaps. Region-BLIP helped some cases but also worsened at least one image with a hallucinated interpretation.

### June-9 post-optimization regime

After targeted phrase-emission changes, the hard-set strict score reached 87.1%, while an independent 32-image / 681-phrase Freshset reached 70.0% strict text. CLIP-lenient reached 94.6% on hard and 92.5% on Freshset, but the threshold sweep shows this measure is highly threshold-sensitive: hard-set coverage ranged from 1.4% at 0.30 to 99.3% at 0.18, with 66.3% at 0.22 and 94.6% at 0.20.
