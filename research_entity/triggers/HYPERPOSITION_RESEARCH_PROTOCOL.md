# Reserved Trigger: `hyperposition`

Research emergent software organization through contextual hyperposition, higher-order codependencies, dynamic topology and extreme boundary-latency reduction. Use the Master Research Trigger Protocol.

Terminology rule: "Verschränkung/entanglement" is an engineering analogy for tightly coupled contextual/causal dependency unless actual quantum phenomena are experimentally involved. Do not import quantum claims metaphorically as evidence.

Primary questions:
- How should mutually context-dependent states coexist without destructive collapse?
- Which higher-order relations require n-ary representation, and which reduce losslessly to pairwise/state-machine forms?
- Can explicit codependency graphs let software recompute only the causally affected structure?
- Can active topology/assemblies replace repeated serialization, retrieval and reconstruction?
- Which boundaries can approach near-zero software overhead through in-process state, zero-copy/shared memory, incremental materialization or data-oriented hot layouts?
- Where do tighter coupling and shared state instead increase contention, cascading failure, noise or recovery cost?

Mandatory research areas where relevant:
- bitemporal/faceted state;
- hypergraphs and role binding;
- incremental/differential dataflow and view maintenance;
- dependency-tracked incremental computation;
- ECS/data-oriented/SoA hot representations;
- zero-copy/shared-memory IPC, mmap, ring buffers, io_uring/eBPF where appropriate;
- cache coherence/locality;
- event sourcing and deterministic replay;
- distributed consistency and failure domains;
- metastability/criticality/dynamic assemblies;
- causal intervention and irreducibility.

Required experiments should separate:
semantic benefit vs storage form;
representation locality vs end-to-end latency;
pairwise vs n-ary information equivalence;
incremental vs batch/full recomputation;
low latency vs contention/recovery/robustness;
maximum coupling vs adaptive coupling.

Write the final report to `research_entity/trigger_runs/hyperposition/<run_id>/FINAL_REPORT.md` and finish with the mandatory status footer.
