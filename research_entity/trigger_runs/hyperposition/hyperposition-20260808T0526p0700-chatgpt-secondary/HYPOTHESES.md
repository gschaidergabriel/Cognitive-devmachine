# Hyperposition H1 Hypothesis Matrix

All hypotheses are OPEN research candidates unless explicitly marked as a bounded representation result. No live-FRANK claim is promoted.

1. **H-HYP-001 — Semantic invariant over backend.** Contextual hyperposition is a state/query invariant: conflict, branch, unknown, revocation, provenance and context remain recoverable until projection. Falsifier: equal-semantics backends cannot reproduce the same contextual answers.
2. **H-HYP-002 — Role-aware relation-instance ABI.** A relation instance with explicit role→entity bindings is a portable lossless ABI for n-ary facts across ordinary graph, relational, event-log and native-hypergraph backends. Falsifier: a tested n-ary fact cannot round-trip through role-aware relation-node reification.
3. **H-HYP-003 — Pairwise projections are not generally sufficient.** Pairwise role projections can erase higher-order grouping/synergy even when each pairwise marginal is preserved. Falsifier: every finite n-ary relation is uniquely reconstructible from its pairwise projections.
4. **H-HYP-004 — Native hyperedges are an optimization, not semantic authority.** Native hypergraph storage should win only when its locality/query/update trade-offs beat role-aware reification under equal semantics. Falsifier: reification necessarily loses tested semantics or cannot meet required complexity.
5. **H-HYP-005 — Monotonicity predicts coordination need.** Additive/monotone derived state can often be merged and incrementally maintained without global coordination. Falsifier: representative monotone transitions require total ordering for correctness.
6. **H-HYP-006 — Non-monotone transitions define closure boundaries.** Revocation, uniqueness, destructive conflict resolution, negative constraints and external effects are the primary candidates for stronger causal/authority coordination. Falsifier: those operations remain safe under arbitrary reorder/partition without added metadata or coordination.
7. **H-HYP-007 — Bitemporality is required for durable contextual truth.** Valid-time and transaction-time must be distinct when claims can be learned late, corrected, revoked or replayed. Falsifier: a single time axis reconstructs all tested histories without ambiguity.
8. **H-HYP-008 — Incremental maintenance has a workload-specific crossover.** Delta propagation beats full rebuild only while change amplification and touched-state fraction stay below a workload-dependent threshold. Falsifier: one strategy dominates across edit rates and topologies.
9. **H-HYP-009 — Tail cascade risk matters more than mean fanout.** Overcoupling control needs tail metrics (e.g. q95/q99 affected fraction) in addition to mean `R_delta`. Falsifier: tail cascade metrics add no predictive value for latency/recovery failures.
10. **H-HYP-010 — Active assemblies should be disposable projections.** Dynamic topology should be compiled from canonical events/relations/context rather than mutated as irreplaceable truth. Falsifier: projection loss destroys information that canonical history cannot reconstruct.
11. **H-HYP-011 — Selective closure outperforms universal closure.** Local/speculative cognitive state plus synchronized effect/conflict boundaries can retain safety with lower latency and better partition tolerance than ordering every transition. Falsifier: selective closure produces unsafe external effects or irrecoverable causal errors.
12. **H-HYP-012 — Zero-copy shifts, rather than deletes, boundary cost.** Shared-memory/zero-copy removes serialization/copy work but exposes cache-coherence, atomic, ownership, reclamation and wakeup costs that rise with contention. Falsifier: those costs remain negligible across representative multicore loads.
13. **H-HYP-013 — Hot representation can be SoA/ECS-like without changing semantics.** The active projection plane may use data-oriented layouts while canonical semantics remain backend-neutral. Falsifier: semantic invariants require canonical objects to share the same hot layout.
14. **H-HYP-014 — Negative deltas are first-class computation.** Retraction/revocation should propagate through incremental projections as explicit negative deltas/tombstones. Falsifier: derived state remains correct under deletions without explicit dependency invalidation.
15. **H-HYP-015 — Replay must separate cognition from effects.** Pure/derived cognition may replay deterministically, while historical external effects must be reauthorized against current roots/capabilities. Falsifier: blind effect replay is safe under changed authority/state.
16. **H-HYP-016 — Coupling should minimize a cost vector, not maximize connectivity.** Control should jointly track semantic benefit, affected-state fraction, copy/serialization cost, cache-line transfers, synchronization, recovery and failure-domain exposure. Falsifier: maximum coupling monotonically improves objective performance without robustness/cost penalty.

## Transformed second-generation hypotheses

- **H-HYP-2A — Monotonicity-Gated Hyperposition:** compile transitions into monotone-local versus non-monotone-closure classes.
- **H-HYP-2B — N-ary Semantic ABI:** canonical relation instances are `(relation_id, roles, entities, context, valid_time, tx_time, provenance, branch/conflict, revocation)`; storage is replaceable.
- **H-HYP-2C — Cascade Tail Controller:** replace a single coupling coefficient with mean + tail change-amplification observability and rebuild crossover.
- **H-HYP-2D — Boundary Cost Vector:** define near-zero-latency as minimizing copies, serialization, syscalls, wakeups, atomics/cache transfers and reclamation while preserving isolation/recovery.
- **H-HYP-2E — Projection Compiler:** canonical event/state plane → incremental contextual projections → bounded active assemblies → external effect closure.
- **H-HYP-2F — Revocation Is the Stress Test:** deletion/retraction, not append-only throughput, is the discriminator for hyperposition backends.
