## 4. Mandatory cycle

Every Trigger-2 cycle performs, in order:

1. **Authority refresh** — exact current `main`, newest owner target, supervisor, program state, current target ABI/census and relevant execution/evidence receipts.
2. **Overlap refresh** — inspect current Trigger-2 PRs/branches/workers and converge instead of duplicating an active lane.
3. **Research refresh** — ingest new repository measurements/negative evidence and current external primary research that can alter a labeler, generator, shortcut test, curriculum order, evaluation or interpretation.
4. **Artifact inventory** — rediscover exact paths/hashes/versions/generators/labelers/splits/provenance/model/tokenizer bindings and predecessor/successor edges.
5. **Staleness propagation** — changed dependencies mark only dependent artifacts `REVIEW_REQUIRED`; unrelated frozen evidence is not churned.
6. **Shortcut/leakage audit** — model-visible nuisance signatures, conditional MI/decoder tests, semantic ablation, ancestry/family leakage, prompt/metadata integrity and holdout contamination fail closed before training promotion.
7. **Labelability gate** — positive targets require deterministic or explicitly adjudicated label sources. Do not invent D/E labels.
8. **Candidate generation/revision** — prioritize retrofit of high-coverage stale families over raw duplicate volume.
9. **Structured swarm review** — target coverage, evidence, labeler, leakage, provenance, Hyperposition/GRF, DB boundary, variable-N, Claude bridge, authority, retention, versioning.
10. **Checkpoint evaluation design** — separate capability curves; aggregate score may summarize but cannot hide a required-channel failure.
11. **Emergence audit** — only on frozen independent families; apparent emergence stays `EMERGENT_CANDIDATE` until explicit-training overlap, ancestry and shortcut contamination are excluded.
12. **Durable write + re-entry** — versioned research delta, registry delta, row/generator delta, supersession links, blockers and next discriminator; then refresh `main` again.

## 8. Current scientific corrections

### 8.1 Binding shortcut law

Supervisor 8.78 rejects Binding-Router Factorial Generator v3 for direct training because higher-order model-visible nuisance signatures remain strongly target-predictive despite marginal balance. Randomized order and zero/low marginal MI are insufficient.

Before binder promotion require at least:

- all target classes and target slots possible inside each relevant model-visible nuisance signature;
- all six display orders where applicable;
- nuisance-only decoder / lookup at prior;
- conditional dependence audit;
- prompt-derived semantic ablation audit rather than hidden-metadata-only proof;
- split/family/provenance isolation on final rendered bytes.

### 8.2 Variable binding curriculum

Current external primary research supports treating binding as a learnable mechanism with a possible shallow-heuristic phase before systematic routing. Therefore checkpoint curves must distinguish shallow shortcut acquisition from systematic multi-hop/variable-binding generalization.

### 8.3 Input-label mapping

Current ICL research shows models can under-use episode-local input-label mappings and fall back to pretrained semantic priors. Opaque, permuted, counterbalanced episode-local codebooks therefore remain a core training/evaluation family, but must be embedded inside the Hyperposition-native operating frame.

### 8.4 Probe interpretation

Representation probes are diagnostic, not causal proof. C1 movement/non-movement must be paired with behavioral/OOD controls; `NO_MOVEMENT != MODEL_SIZE_PROVEN_WRONG`.

### 8.5 Incomplete SFT learning

Recent ACL 2026 evidence shows converged SFT can leave heterogeneous subsets unlearned because of prerequisite gaps, supervision/pretraining conflict, internal inconsistency, forgetting or insufficient optimization on rare/complex patterns. Trigger-2 therefore requires capability/family-level checkpoint curves and diagnostic routing rather than one aggregate train/eval metric.
