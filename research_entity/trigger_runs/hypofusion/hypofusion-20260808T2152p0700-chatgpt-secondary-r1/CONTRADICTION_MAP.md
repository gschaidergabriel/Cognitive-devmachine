# Hypofusion H1 — Contradiction Map

## C1 — Persistence vs context overload

**Clay-side:** persistent state should continuously shape future cognition.

**External challenge:** Lost in the Middle and Context Rot show that simply exposing more retained information can reduce effective use of relevant information.

**Resolution:** persistence and presentation are separate. Keep rich persistent state; expose only a bounded, task-conditioned Assembly/rail. `persistent memory != maximal prompt context`.

**Status:** REVISE, not refute.

## C2 — Graph activation vs retrieval

**Clay-side:** sparse activation can make relevant associations proactively available.

**External challenge:** strong RAG/coding-agent baselines and graph maintenance costs mean graph activation may not dominate every query.

**Resolution:** retain dual-path memory: proactive activation for availability; explicit retrieval for exact lookup, validation, OOD and broad search.

**Status:** SUPPORT WITH BOUNDARY.

## C3 — Information-rich latent rail vs useful communication

**Positive evidence:** Interlat demonstrates technically useful latent communication in some settings.

**Counterevidence:** arXiv 2607.14103 finds much higher probe retention without task-level superiority; arXiv 2607.26773 shows aggregate accuracy can hide opposing message effects.

**Resolution:** P3 is an optimization rail, never a truth source. Gate adoption on causal task utility after alignment/transfer cost and message-substitution audit.

**Status:** REVISE STRONGLY.

## C4 — System GWT vs model-internal workspace

**Old risk:** treating FRANK WorkspaceEpisode selection as proof that the model internally used the selected content in a workspace-like mechanism.

**Fresh external evidence:** Anthropic's J-space work shows a causally load-bearing privileged verbalizable representation in Claude and releases Jacobian-lens tooling adaptable to Qwen/HuggingFace decoders.

**Fresh worker evidence:** GWT R5 compiled the Jacobian-lens source in E2B after a dependency-install path hit MemoryError; no target hidden-state intervention has yet run.

**Resolution:** add an explicit `internal_workspace_uptake` causal stage between projection and proposal. System and internal workspace are separately testable.

**Status:** NEW MECHANISTIC SPLIT; target-Qwen test open.

## C5 — More broadcast vs bounded workspace

**Positive evidence:** shared-workspace neural-module research gives a rationale for bandwidth limits promoting specialization/synchronization.

**Counter:** long-context models can benefit from more information when selection/position is favorable.

**Resolution:** do not assume an inverted-U or monotonic curve. Measure workspace capacity directly under pinned candidate/projection semantics.

**Status:** OPEN.

## C6 — Unified semantic organism vs physical monolith

**Clay/FRANK:** one Clay semantic organism.

**External support/challenge:** polystore work supports global semantic schemas across specialized stores but documents processing overhead; event sourcing supports replay/audit while adding eventual-consistency/idempotency complexity.

**Resolution:** semantic unity is retained; physical topology remains evidence-selected and reversible.

**Status:** SUPPORT WITH COST COUNTERARGUMENT.

## C7 — Typed/generative safety vs external authority

**Positive prompt-layer evidence:** Task Shield shows meaningful attack reduction.

**Stronger boundary evidence:** tracked-capability work and 2026 runtime MCP policy enforcement support complete mediation at effect/tool boundaries; prompt-only defenses leave classes of attacks unclosed in the reported studies.

**Resolution:** keep prompt/task alignment as defense-in-depth; authority root remains deterministic broker/PEP outside generative interpretation.

**Status:** STRENGTHEN.

## C8 — Higher-order relations vs overcoupling

**Positive evidence:** higher-order-interaction physics and HyperGraphRAG support explicit group/n-ary relations.

**Counterevidence:** hypergraph/GNN literature shows oversmoothing and representation collapse can worsen under strong multi-way propagation.

**Resolution:** distinguish representational arity from propagation magnitude. Keep n-ary semantics; bound activation depth/gain/budget and measure diversity.

**Status:** REVISE.

## C9 — Effective information vs causal emergence

**Positive:** PEID offers intervention-grounded synergistic causal decomposition.

**Counter:** Dewhurst argues effective-information causal emergence may be epistemic rather than strong causal emergence; different frameworks need not agree.

**Resolution:** no single scalar can promote emergence. E-CERT2 remains multimetric and intervention-first.

**Status:** COUNTERARGUMENT ADOPTED AS TEST CONSTRAINT.

## C10 — Full-model Clay adaptation vs forgetting

**Project fixed target:** fully adapt the target model to Clay.

**External challenge:** ACL 2026 Source-Shielded Updates reports substantial source-task degradation under ordinary full fine-tuning in tested models/languages.

**Resolution:** do not reopen the owner-fixed target, but promote retained-capability/OOD/authority shadow metrics to hard checkpoint gates. Tokenizer strategy remains factorial.

**Status:** TARGET RETAINED, METHOD CONSTRAINED.

## C11 — Utility adaptation vs self-reinforcing memory

**Clay G12:** utility may update salience but not truth.

**New Hypofusion challenge:** even if truth is immutable, salience can self-amplify, suppress novelty and form an internally coherent but wrong attractor.

**Resolution:** add explicit anti-attractor controls: bounded gain, inhibition, novelty/diversity reserve, role/temporal-shuffled holdouts and adversarial utility poisoning tests.

**Status:** NEW FAILURE MODE / NEW EXPERIMENT.

## C12 — Consciousness inference

**Functional evidence:** GWT-like internal representations and recurrent memory can be mechanistically real.

**Counterboundary:** biological GNWT remains contested; popular-science discussion such as Anil Seth's Nautilus essay emphasizes that intelligence/humanlike behavior does not establish consciousness.

**Resolution:** preserve current RAE rule: functional GWT, continuity, self-model or emergence measures do not establish phenomenal consciousness.

**Status:** NO PROMOTION.
