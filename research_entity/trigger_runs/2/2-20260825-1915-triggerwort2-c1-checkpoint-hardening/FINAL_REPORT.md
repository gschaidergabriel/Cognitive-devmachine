### 1. Preregistered normalized checkpoint cadence

Before the first optimizer update, freeze checkpoint evaluation cadence in normalized optimizer-progress coordinates. Default research candidates are:

`0.00, 0.01, 0.03, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00`

A resource-constrained pilot may use a smaller frozen subset only if it retains baseline, at least two interior early/mid checkpoints and final checkpoint.

Forbidden after holdout observation:

- selecting checkpoint times after final holdout behavior is known;
- reporting only the best checkpoint;
- deleting heuristic-dominant intermediate checkpoints from provenance;
- changing cadence because an opened holdout failed.

This operationalizes current-main's requirement that aggregate convergence cannot hide persistent family-specific non-learning or shortcut phases.

### 2. Generalized nuisance-only challenger

The original C1 protocol already required model-visible nuisance prediction on final rendered bytes. This revision adds a separate stronger diagnostic:

- semantically ablate only the task-relevant payload while preserving model-visible nuisance surface;
- train a simple preregistered nuisance-only challenger only on TRAIN nuisance projections;
- evaluate it on disjoint VALIDATION nuisance families;
- freeze challenger architecture/features/training procedure/tolerance before the pilot.

Reason: exact-signature lookup at chance is necessary but not sufficient if a learnable higher-order nuisance function generalizes across signatures/families.

C1 `GO` now requires both exact and learned nuisance-only controls to remain at/near their preregistered null/tolerance.
