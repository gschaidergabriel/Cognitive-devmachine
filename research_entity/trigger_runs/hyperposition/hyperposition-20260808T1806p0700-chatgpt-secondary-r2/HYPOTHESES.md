# Hyperposition H4 Hypothesis Matrix

Boundary: all hypotheses below are OPEN unless explicitly marked negative/transformed. The project-owner decision that target FRANK is Clay-native end-to-end except Needle is fixed and is not a hypothesis.

## First generation — 32 falsifiable hypotheses

1. **H-HYP4-001 — One language ≠ one failure domain.** A Clay-native system can preserve one semantic language while enforcing multiple non-interchangeable semantic classes. Falsifier: cross-class unrestricted substitution is equally safe under adversarial multi-stage tests.
2. **H-HYP4-002 — Semantic-class identity is part of meaning.** `FACT(x)`, `ACTIVATION(x)`, `PROPOSAL(x)` and `CAPABILITY(x)` with identical surface payloads are not substitutable. Falsifier: class identity never changes valid transitions/effects.
3. **H-HYP4-003 — Raw symbol equality is not authority identity.** Surface-token equality must never establish capability/resource identity. Falsifier: adversarial aliasing cannot redirect effect-bound references when class/identity is omitted.
4. **H-HYP4-004 — Activation cannot cast to truth.** Activation may change accessibility/salience but needs exact-anchor validation to become an asserted fact. Falsifier: direct activation→fact casting remains safe under stale/revoked activation attacks.
5. **H-HYP4-005 — Proposal cannot cast to authority.** Model/Needle/memory proposals require an external trusted capability transition. Falsifier: proposal-derived authority is equally safe under prompt/memory corruption.
6. **H-HYP4-006 — Form/code materialization is an effect.** Homoiconic Clay data representing executable forms must remain inert until an explicit typed materialization transition. Falsifier: ungoverned data→code interpretation cannot create new authority/effects.
7. **H-HYP4-007 — Origin precedes selection.** Write-time origin/authority and retrieval-time selector integrity are separate witnesses. Falsifier: one gate dominates both poisoning and structural redirection with equal utility/cost.
8. **H-HYP4-008 — Selector provenance belongs in effect-bound basis.** Reallocatable query-plan/selector semantics must be bound into the operation-specific basis. Falsifier: selector mutation changes effect-bound membership while basis remains safely valid without selector witness.
9. **H-HYP4-009 — Revocation is ancestry propagation.** Revocation should invalidate dependent activation, Assemblies, candidate sets, workspace episodes and pending re-entry/effect intents through dependency ancestry. Falsifier: record-only revalidation reliably blocks all stale descendants at lower cost.
10. **H-HYP4-010 — Typed firebreaks reduce privilege cascades.** Cross-domain semantic typing reduces authority/effect contamination even when cognitive errors continue propagating. Falsifier: matched hypergraph/live faults show no reduction in privilege/effect spread.
11. **H-HYP4-011 — Coverage controls firebreak economics.** Narrow typed fast paths are economical only when witness/closure coverage is very high. Falsifier: low-coverage scopes retain low fallback and zero unsafe accepts without broader validation.
12. **H-HYP4-012 — Compact typed handles beat verbose objects on hot paths.** Interned/packed `{class,id,epoch}` handles preserve semantics with lower runtime/cache cost than verbose maps/strings. Falsifier: target Rust/C/Android benchmarks show no benefit or worse recovery/debug cost.
13. **H-HYP4-013 — Projection remains causal in a Clay-only system.** Clay→Clay projection/order/schema can alter reader behavior even without language translation. Falsifier: target matched counterbalancing shows invariant behavior across semantically equivalent projections.
14. **H-HYP4-014 — Compiler diversity can reduce common-mode errors without a second semantic architecture.** Independent verifier implementations can check the same Clay contract. Falsifier: verifier diversity adds no fault-detection value or creates semantic divergence.
15. **H-HYP4-015 — Homoiconic safety needs type/effect separation, not textual conventions alone.** Falsifier: adversarial multi-turn data/code confusion remains equally contained without runtime type/effect enforcement.
16. **H-HYP4-016 — Needle needs an explicit foreign-body bridge.** Needle output enters Clay as typed observation/proposal plus provenance, never authority. Falsifier: implicit/raw routing is equally auditable and safe.
17. **H-HYP4-017 — GWT tests must orthogonalize semantic-class transitions.** Candidate generation, selection, projection, uptake and re-entry should not be bundled with class-casts. Falsifier: bundled tests remain unbiased under planted neighboring effects.
18. **H-HYP4-018 — Pairwise flattening remains a within-domain association hazard.** Semantic classes do not repair n-ary role loss. Falsifier: all relevant ClayGraph queries admit proven pairwise equivalence.
19. **H-HYP4-019 — Coordination follows semantic transition requirements, not shared language.** Clay-native modules need synchronization only where invariants/effects require it. Falsifier: global coordination Pareto-dominates scoped closure on real traces.
20. **H-HYP4-020 — Effect plane deserves the strongest validation tier.** Reversible cognition can tolerate broader uncertain inputs than irreversible effects. Falsifier: one integrity tier gives equal utility/safety at lower complexity.
21. **H-HYP4-021 — Unknown semantic casts fail broad.** Unknown/reflective/plugin cross-class transitions should widen/isolate/reject rather than infer authority. Falsifier: permissive inference shows no additional unsafe paths.
22. **H-HYP4-022 — Semantic tags need provenance/version, not names alone.** Class labels without schema/version/origin can be stale or forged. Falsifier: name-only labels survive schema mutation and laundering tests.
23. **H-HYP4-023 — A semantic-domain lattice can encode monotone authority attenuation.** Derived values may retain or lose rights but not silently gain them. Falsifier: useful legal workflows require implicit authority amplification that cannot be expressed as explicit grant.
24. **H-HYP4-024 — Candidate-set receipts bridge safety and science.** The same membership receipt can detect selector tampering and support causal replay without conflating conclusions. Falsifier: it fails either use under complete declared coverage.
25. **H-HYP4-025 — Cross-domain cascade tails matter more than mean spread.** p95/p99 privilege/effect contamination should be monitored separately from mean cognitive spread. Falsifier: tail measures add no predictive value for recovery/effect risk.
26. **H-HYP4-026 — Firebreak failures should be observable as cast faults.** Invalid semantic transitions should emit explicit receipts rather than silently coerce. Falsifier: coercion is equally debuggable/recoverable.
27. **H-HYP4-027 — ClayVector/tensor crossings need typed basis/unit/axis semantics.** Physical numeric tensors remain non-textual but semantic crossings require Clay-typed meaning. Falsifier: basis/unit/axis omissions never cause detectable semantic faults.
28. **H-HYP4-028 — Exact truth and derived Clay views require different mutation rights.** Derived projections may be discarded/rebuilt; canonical exact/event state cannot be mutated through the same path. Falsifier: shared mutation path is equally replay-safe.
29. **H-HYP4-029 — Firebreak placement should be operation-specific.** Not every transition should pay authority-grade validation. Falsifier: universal strong validation has lower total cost without harmless rejects.
30. **H-HYP4-030 — Common-mode semantic/compiler faults become more important as translation boundaries disappear.** Falsifier: full Clay-native integration reduces both translation and shared semantic error sensitivity in target tests.
31. **H-HYP4-031 — Emergent assemblies require role/class-preserving identity.** Recurrence based only on member overlap or shared Clay tokens is insufficient. Falsifier: role/class permutation does not affect function under lesion/rescue.
32. **H-HYP4-032 — Near-zero latency is compatible with semantic firebreaks only after lowering them to compact verified handles.** Falsifier: target implementation cannot meet latency/cache budgets without erasing semantic distinctions.

## Fusion 2 — transformed hypotheses

### H4-T1 — HYP-RC4: Clay-Native Typed Semantic Firebreak Hyperposition
One Clay semantic language; explicit non-interchangeable semantic domains; operation-specific legal casts; effect authority external and fail-closed.

### H4-T2 — Semantic Domain Lattice
Candidate classes: `FACT/EXACT`, `OBSERVATION`, `ACTIVATION_PRIOR`, `PROPOSAL`, `FORM`, `AUTHORITY/CAPABILITY`, `EFFECT_INTENT`, with version/origin/basis facets. The exact lattice remains OPEN and must be minimized experimentally.

### H4-T3 — Origin–Selector–Basis Capsule
Extend HYP-RC3 `TopologyBasis`/FRANK `CognitiveEpochCapsule` for effect-bound operations with origin authority, selector/query-plan identity, candidate-set receipt and required semantic-domain witnesses only.

### H4-T4 — Ancestry Revocation Chain
A signed revocation traverses reverse semantic dependencies through activation → Assembly → candidate set → WorkspaceEpisode → pending re-entry/effect intent; unaffected branches remain valid.

### H4-T5 — Compact Typed Handle ABI
Compile Clay semantic objects to interned/packed hot-path handles such as `{class_id, semantic_id, epoch_or_basis_ref, integrity_ref}` while preserving reconstructibility to canonical Clay semantics.

### H4-T6 — Firebreak Coverage Controller
Measure coverage and route transitions among `typed-fast-path | broadened-validation | isolated-evaluation | reject`. Coverage uncertainty buys validation rather than optimistic coercion.

### H4-T7 — Homoiconic Materialization Gate
Clay forms are data by default. `FORM -> EXECUTABLE/EFFECT` is an explicit deterministic checked operation with compositional closure and capability validation.

### H4-T8 — Semantic Cascade Certificate
Track cascade distribution by semantic domain and authority tier: mean/q95/q99 spread, privilege-crossing count, effect contamination, invalid-cast rate, recovery/revocation depth. Do not collapse to one scalar fitness objective.
