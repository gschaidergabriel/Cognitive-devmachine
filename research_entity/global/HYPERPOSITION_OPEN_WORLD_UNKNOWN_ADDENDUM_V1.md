# Hyperposition open-world / UNKNOWN addendum v1

Status: RESEARCH_CANDIDATE / NOT_VALIDATED
Parent: `HYPERPOSITION_GWT_INFORMATION_GEOMETRY_V1.md`

## 1. Failure in ordinary normalization

A categorical posterior over K hypotheses satisfies:

`sum_(i=1..K) p_i = 1`.

That is safe only if the modeled hypothesis set is admitted as exhaustive at the relevant scope.

If the true explanation may lie outside `{H_1,...,H_K}`, normalizing known candidates to 1 can create false certainty.

Clay invariant:

`KNOWN-CANDIDATE NORMALIZATION != WORLD EXHAUSTIVENESS`.

## 2. Three different leftovers

Do not conflate:

1. **known omitted tail** — admitted known candidates compressed out of the active view;
2. **set ignorance** — evidence supports a set/disjunction without selecting a member;
3. **open-world unknown** — current candidate vocabulary may be incomplete.

The H1 `~` pseudo-state is reserved for (1): omitted known mass/value-relevant residue.

It must not silently stand for (2) or (3).

## 3. Minimal Clay open-world law

The simplest architecture does not require adopting a full Dempster-Shafer or credal-set engine.

Instead canonical state records an explicit exhaustiveness/unknown status for each Hyperposition relation:

`scope_exhaustive: TRUE | FALSE | UNKNOWN`

and, when an admitted model supplies it, optional bounded unknown/other mass or interval:

`p_unknown` or `[p_unknown_low,p_unknown_high]`.

If exhaustiveness is not TRUE, the model/runtime must not interpret `max_i p_i -> 1` among known candidates as proof that one known candidate is true.

## 4. Conditional known posterior

When open-world uncertainty exists, use notation:

`p_i^K = P(H_i | H in known_set, E)`

for normalized probability **conditional on the known set**.

This avoids the false identity:

`P(H_i|E) = p_i^K`.

If an admitted unknown mass `u=P(H outside known_set|E)` exists, then:

`P(H_i|E) = (1-u) p_i^K`.

Without an admitted u, the absolute probability remains unresolved.

## 5. Credal fallback for source/model imprecision

When source reliability or model specification cannot justify one precise posterior, a research alternative is a credal set:

`Pset = {p^(1),...,p^(m)}`

or lower/upper probabilities:

`p_i in [p_i^-,p_i^+]`.

Decision robustness can be tested using lower expected utility / minimax regret rather than inventing a point probability.

This is optional research machinery. It should be admitted only where measured benefit exceeds complexity.

## 6. Decision rule under imprecision

For precise posterior:

`a* = argmax_a E_p U(a,H)`.

For credal set Pset, candidate robust rule:

`a* = argmax_a min_(p in Pset) E_p U(a,H)`

or minimax regret, depending on task contract.

No robust rule itself creates truth.

## 7. Compact transport consequence

Preferred H0 handle:

`h(c,7)`

needs no extra token syntax; the canonical bundle can carry exhaustiveness/unknown state.

If H1 self-contained transport is ever needed, distinguish known omitted tail from open-world UNKNOWN. Candidate conceptual form only:

`h(c,"a-3|b|~-8#e7")`

where `~` means known compressed tail. Open-world status should preferably remain in `#e7` rather than adding repeated generated syntax.

Reason: UNKNOWN/exhaustiveness is canonical epistemic metadata and should not be regenerated by the model.

## 8. Research implication

A high posterior leader can coexist with high model-set uncertainty.

Therefore future evaluation must separately score:
- within-known calibration;
- open-set/OOD abstention;
- unsupported known-candidate collapse;
- candidate-set expansion after discriminator evidence;
- false certainty caused by renormalization.

## 9. Literature motivation, not authority

Imprecise-probability/credal-set work explicitly represents uncertainty using sets of precise distributions rather than a single precise distribution, including near-ignorance/model-imprecision cases. Dempster-Shafer-style frameworks can assign support to hypothesis sets and distinguish ignorance from a precise split among singletons. Open-set recognition likewise studies settings where unseen classes can occur.

Clay does not inherit any one framework wholesale. The product-level requirement extracted here is narrower:

`UNKNOWN MUST SURVIVE NORMALIZATION`.

## 10. Promotion boundary

Do not add unknown-mass numeric machinery unless a task-valid estimator/calibration contract exists. A fabricated `p_unknown` is worse than explicit UNKNOWN.

Until measured otherwise:
- explicit exhaustiveness status is preferred;
- H0 keeps it canonical and token-free at the generated-code layer;
- H1 transports only derived known-state coordinates;
- R6/provenance remains the fact-collapse boundary.
