## 1. Executive synthesis

The strongest synthesis is that Clay/FRANK should treat GWT and emergence as two orthogonal but coupled scientific questions.

1. **GWT question:** did a capacity-limited competition select an episode that became globally addressable and was actually delivered, reconstructed, taken up, causally used, converted into an outcome and re-entered?
2. **Emergence question:** does the joint organization produce intervention-relevant structure that cannot be adequately explained by isolated/additive/pairwise components or by an arbitrary micro description?
3. **Control question:** can the measured coupling regime be safely steered toward useful task outcomes without optimizing a decorative emergence proxy?

The resulting architecture is therefore not `one emergence score -> control`. It is:

`candidate state -> competition -> selection -> global addressability -> delivery -> reconstruction -> uptake -> causal use -> outcome -> provenance-bound re-entry`

plus a parallel measurement plane:

`interaction + directed flow + higher-order irreducibility + macro causality + dynamics + generalization + cost + controls/nulls`.

This is fully aligned with the current repository's GWT/Emergence contracts and with the existing negative evidence that stage labels alone are a poor validator and that one fixed coupling strength does not transfer universally.

The external literature strengthens five design directions:

- a bandwidth-limited workspace is functionally plausible and can promote specialization/robustness;
- GWT's computational core should be separated from a fixed biological/anatomical implementation;
- reactivation/activity-silent persistence makes repeated selection/broadcast/re-entry more plausible than continuous global activation;
- aggregate measures such as transfer entropy or a single integration score conflate distinct information phenomena;
- higher-order and macro-level claims require explicit reduction/intervention tests because apparent hypergraph or macro structure can be reducible or causally ill-defined.

No source reviewed justifies inferring phenomenal consciousness from these mechanisms. This report concerns functional/causal emergence only.

## 3. Fusion 1 — pre-external-search model

Before the external hypothesis-guided search, the internally grounded model contained seven mechanism families.

### F1.1 Workspace causal chain

An emergent candidate cannot be inferred from a selected winner alone. A successful episode must preserve distinct identities for selection, broadcast, delivery, reconstruction, uptake, causal use, outcome and re-entry. This creates natural lesion surfaces and prevents 'broadcast happened' from being confused with 'broadcast mattered'.

### F1.2 Re-entry as causal closure

Re-entry is not a boolean feature. It is a typed information path carrying outcome and provenance into future competition. Strong evidence requires truthful vs shuffled/replaced/no-reentry conditions and a predicted downstream state difference.

### F1.3 Higher-order coupling

Hyperposition and ClayGraph allow n-ary relations, but representation does not establish irreducibility. Every higher-order candidate needs a best pairwise/state-machine reduction under matched inputs and interventions.

### F1.4 Multi-timescale coupling

Fast competition can be modulated by slower memory, goals, personality/body state, consolidation and restart history. Emergence may therefore appear as path dependence or hysteresis and disappear if slow variables are averaged away.

### F1.5 Macro causality

A WorkspaceEpisode or Assembly may be a useful macro variable, but only if interventions on the macro have a coherent micro realization and preserve causal meaning. Better prediction under compression alone is insufficient.

### F1.6 Metastability / adaptive coupling

The internal fixed-coupling negative result suggests that a useful regime is task/state dependent. The natural hypothesis is a bounded operating region, not a universal gain constant.

### F1.7 Observability-to-control boundary

Emergence readouts must remain measurements. They do not become truth or execution authority and they should not be optimized directly without task, failure, resource, authority and recovery shadows.

## 4. External research round 1 — what survives

### 4.1 GWT in artificial systems

VanRullen & Kanai's deep-learning GWT roadmap frames a global latent workspace as a communication/translation surface among specialized latent spaces. Goyal et al.'s shared global workspace demonstrates a bandwidth-limited communication channel where specialists compete for access; the reported capacity constraint encourages specialization/compositionality and coordination. Dossa et al. implement an embodied audiovisual GWT agent and report stronger robustness at smaller working-memory capacities relative to a recurrent comparator, with task/regularization dependence. These sources jointly support treating limited capacity as a functional hypothesis rather than an inconvenience.

A 2026 preprint by Bertin-Johannet et al. further reports improved robustness and cross-task/cross-modality generalization from top-down modality selection inside a frozen global-workspace system. Because this is current preprint evidence, it supports testing but not canonical promotion.

### 4.2 Biological GNWT constraints do not force software anatomy

The preregistered 2025 Cogitate adversarial collaboration tested biological implementation predictions of GNWT and IIT in 256 participants using fMRI, MEG and intracranial EEG. It found some GNWT-compatible fronto-sensory coupling but challenged important anatomical/temporal predictions, including a general lack of the predicted offset ignition pattern and limited PFC representation for some content dimensions. Critically for software architecture, the paper explicitly separates theoretical computational cores from particular biological implementations.

Therefore Clay should not encode 'PFC-like service location = ignition'. A software ignition should be an operational state transition in global availability and use, with implementation location left falsifiable.

### 4.3 Activity-silent persistence and reactivation

Primate work by Watanabe et al. found cycles of goal silencing and reactivation rather than unbroken sustained goal codes during complex problem solving. This does not prove GWT re-entry in Clay, but it makes a computational design with latent persistence + context-triggered reactivation more biologically plausible than requiring continuous active broadcast.

### 4.4 Selection-broadcast cycles

Nakanishi et al. explicitly analyze a Selection-Broadcast Cycle as a GWT-inspired mechanism for dynamic, experience-based and real-time adaptation. This is a hypothesis/theory contribution rather than decisive empirical evidence, but it aligns with the project's requirement that outcome/re-entry can change future competition.

### 4.5 Information dynamics need decomposition

Mediano et al.'s 2025 PNAS work on Integrated Information Decomposition (PhiID) shows that commonly used information-dynamic quantities can aggregate qualitatively different information atoms. In particular, transfer entropy can mix different unique/synergistic transitions. This strongly supports Clay's decision not to collapse information flow or integration into one number.

PEID (2026 preprint) is especially interesting for the project because it defines an intervention-oriented decomposition of multivariate causal influence into unique and synergistic components under specified interventions, potentially yielding causal hyperedges. It is promising but currently too new/preprint-level to be an authoritative estimator.

### 4.6 Higher-order causality has a real anti-reduction criterion

A 2025 Physical Review E method generalizes information-theoretic causality to multivariate causal sets and can identify purely synergistic inputs that pairwise TE/Granger-style reasoning misses. Conversely, a 2026 Physical Review Research result shows that an entire class of higher-order hypergraph update models can be mapped exactly to weighted pairwise interactions while preserving microscopic transition probabilities.

This pair of findings is decisive for methodology: **higher-order representation is neither sufficient nor necessary evidence for higher-order causal mechanism**. Clay must ask whether the best reduction reproduces prediction and intervention behavior.

### 4.7 Macro causal variables need intervention semantics

Causal-emergence/effective-information research provides tools for comparing causal structure across scales, and recent machine-learning work learns macro-dynamics by maximizing effective information. But Zhu et al. show that interventions on aggregated variables can become ill-defined because different micro-realizations of one macro intervention cause different downstream effects. Targeted Causal Reduction offers an alternative: find a concise causal representation for a specific target phenomenon using interventional data.

The practical consequence for Clay is to reject a universal 'macro emergence variable'. Candidate Assemblies should be **target-conditioned causal reductions** with explicit micro realization and intervention equivalence error.

### 4.8 Integration/segregation and metastability

A 2025 network-model study found richest dynamic behavior around intermediate integration/segregation regimes, while highly integrated states became dynamically flatter. This is not direct evidence about FRANK, but it is mechanistically congruent with the existing internal fixed-coupling failure and justifies explicit overcoupling tests.

## 5. Hypothesis-guided external search — contradiction map

The second search deliberately targeted the strongest and wildest hypotheses.

### C1. 'Hyperedges are intrinsically emergent' — rejected

Contradicted by exact pairwise reducibility results for some higher-order models. Keep higher-order ClayGraph primitives as representational tools, but promote an emergent hyperedge only if reduction fails on a relevant intervention/prediction criterion.

### C2. 'Transfer entropy proves directed causality' — rejected

Information directionality can be useful, but observational directed-flow estimators do not replace interventions. PhiID further shows that TE can aggregate qualitatively different information transitions.

### C3. 'Macro effective information automatically defines the right causal level' — weakened

Macro variables can be powerful, but aggregation can make interventions ill-defined. Macro candidates need target, micro-realization distribution and intervention contract.

### C4. 'More integration/broadcast is better' — rejected as default

Artificial GWT results and network-dynamics results instead motivate constrained capacity and an integration/segregation balance. The optimization target must be useful causal coordination, not maximal reach/connectivity.

### C5. 'Ignition requires one persistent active state' — weakened

GNWT biological predictions themselves include activity-silent maintenance between ignition events, and primate working-memory work supports reactivation cycles. For software, an episode may be latent between re-entry and reactivation if its identity and causal lineage are preserved.

### C6. 'One physical module must own the workspace' — unsupported

No reviewed evidence requires a particular software deployment boundary. The project's H1/H2/H3 program can therefore test whether a causal workspace motif survives physical consolidation.

## 6. Fusion 2 — resulting model

The second fusion yields a stronger operational definition of functional/causal emergence in a GWT system:

> A candidate emergent mechanism is a reproducible, intervention-sensitive organization of workspace-linked state transitions whose useful outcome dependence cannot be adequately reproduced by a specified simpler/additive/pairwise/micro baseline, whose causal role is supported by predicted lesion and rescue, and whose effect survives relevant context/implementation transfer within a declared scope.

This definition has six independent evidence axes.

1. **GWT completion:** competition -> selected episode -> addressability -> delivery -> reconstruction -> uptake -> causal use -> outcome -> re-entry.
2. **Interaction:** factorial nonadditivity and joint-vs-isolated effects.
3. **Higher-order information:** PID/PhiID/PEID-style synergy with estimator/null sensitivity.
4. **Causal intervention:** lesion, rescue, delay/gain/topology/re-entry perturbation.
5. **Macro explanatory gain:** target-specific causal reduction/effective-information gain with explicit intervention semantics.
6. **Transfer/robustness:** OOD recurrence, cross-task/topology/implementation transfer plus latency/memory/instability/false-binding costs.

No one axis can stand in for the others.

## 7. Hypothesis generation 2

The run emits 36 falsifiable hypotheses in `HYPOTHESIS_MATRIX.json`, divided into conservative, aggressive and wild-but-GWT-consistent tiers. All preserve a capacity-limited global selection/broadcast layer; local, shadow, hierarchical or hyperposition processes remain candidates or pre/post-processing and do not create a second unrestricted authority plane.

Highest-value hypotheses are:

### H-P0 Functional ignition is location-independent

Treat ignition as a functional transition whose certificate includes selected episode identity, global addressability and downstream causal-use evidence. H1/H2/H3 should preserve this motif despite different physical boundaries.

### H-P1 Truthful provenance-bound re-entry beats content-only repetition

This is the cleanest live extension of current synthetic evidence. Use truthful, temporally shuffled, receiver-shuffled, provenance-broken, delayed and absent re-entry arms.

### H-P2 Workspace capacity follows an inverted-U

Sweep candidate/workspace capacity under distractors and OOD conditions. Measure specialization, task success, causal-use fraction, synergy atoms, latency and false binding.

### H-P3 Dynamic topology beats static topology only in some regimes

Test static pairwise, dynamic pairwise and dynamic higher-order topology under a fixed edge/resource budget. Do not assume the hypergraph arm wins.

### H-P4 True higher-order relations survive pairwise reduction

Every proposed hyperedge enters a reduction tournament. Promotion requires a residual in intervention response, prediction, selection or replay behavior that the best pairwise/state-machine model cannot reproduce economically.

### H-P5 Slow state creates GWT hysteresis

Identical present input can yield different workspace winners after different validated histories if goal/memory/personality state modulates competition. Clamp and rescue those slow states.

### H-P6 There is a useful re-entry delay band

Too-fast re-entry may create echo/self-reinforcement; too-slow re-entry loses relevance. Sweep delay and measure utility, recurrence, instability and causal contribution.

### H-P7 Global availability can use sparse physical delivery

The workspace can be semantically addressable to all modules while actual delivery/uptake is selective. This could preserve the GWT global-availability property while reducing compute and harmful uptake.

### H-P8 Workspace macrostate as causal compression

Test whether a WorkspaceEpisode can serve as a compact intervention variable whose effects approximate full micro interventions with bounded error. Reject if compression merely predicts but does not preserve interventions.

### H-P9 Model self-readout is itself a causal intervention

A verified ReadoutProjector shown to the 4B can change future competition. Compare blind, truthful and falsified readout conditions; never allow model introspection to certify the measurement.

### H-P10 Ephemeral higher-order topology pulses

Wild but GWT-consistent: temporary hyperedges may form only during binding/ignition and dissolve after re-entry, reducing long-lived overbinding. Compare ephemeral and persistent relation lifetimes.

### H-P11 Counterfactual shadow workspaces

Wild but GWT-consistent: generate multiple mutually exclusive candidate future workspace episodes in a no-effect shadow environment, then submit them to the next real global competition. Only the selected real episode may broadcast/effect state.

## 8. Minimum decisive experimental program

### E1 — Causal episode instrumentation first

Before measuring emergence, H1 must emit immutable receipts for:

- BroadcastEnvelope;

- next-episode influence;
- model-output hash;
- intervention id;
- topology hash;
- estimator/version;
- result hash.

If this is absent, most higher-order statistics are observational decoration.

### E2 — Re-entry causal ladder

Conditions: truthful, temporal-shuffle, receiver-shuffle, provenance-broken payload-preserving, delayed, absent. Use matched current-turn model output where technically possible. Predetermine which later candidate scores, winners, memory states and outcomes should change.

Promotion path: observational -> predictive -> lesion-supported -> rescue-supported -> counterfactual-reentry-supported -> OOD/implementation-transfer-supported.

### E3 — Capacity/gain phase diagram

Sweep workspace capacity K, competition gain and ignition threshold in forward and reverse directions. Record task success, receiver causal-use fraction, recurrence, false binding, latency, memory and instability. Search for hysteresis and under/overcoupling regimes rather than maximizing one score.

### E4 — Pairwise vs higher-order tournament

For every candidate hyperedge/Assembly:

1. full n-ary model;
2. best weighted pairwise model;
3. pairwise + latent state-machine reduction;
4. shuffled hyperedge null;
5. joint lesion;
6. constituent lesions;
7. rescue.

Compare prediction, intervention response, workspace selection, outcome and cost.

### E5 — Macro causal reduction

Choose a declared target, e.g. successful recovery after conflicting sensory evidence. Compare micro trace, WorkspaceEpisode, Assembly and learned targeted causal reduction. Define the micro realization distribution for each macro intervention and estimate intervention-equivalence error.

### E6 — Information-dynamics decomposition

Use TE/directed prediction only as one layer. Add PID/PhiID for selected small variable sets and PEID experimentally where intervention assumptions are met. Bootstrap/null/shuffle estimator results. Do not require expensive decomposition on every live episode; use stratified/offline analysis.

### E7 — Metastability / control

Only after E1-E6 identify causal readouts, compare:

- static coupling;
- fixed high coupling;
- adaptive bounded controller;
- naive emergence-readout maximizer.

Objective: task utility + transfer + recovery under false-binding, effect-error, authority, latency, memory and rollback constraints.

## 10. Cross-cutting packages

Verify invariants: exactly one authoritative selected episode per workspace epoch where required; no effect from shadow candidates; UNKNOWN never coerced to zero; measurement cannot mutate authority; re-entry lineage cannot silently cross epochs; replay intervention hashes are stable.

### Performance

Every emergence readout carries cost shadows. Use cheap live counters continuously and expensive PID/PhiID/PEID/EI estimators offline or adaptively sampled. Optimize useful task behavior under resource constraints, never estimator magnitude.

Test whether compact causal readouts improve the local model's routing/calibration without feeding raw telemetry overload. Keep an external validator for readout truth. Small-model proxy results are not target-Qwen-4B proof.

Create contrastive ClayCode examples where: broadcast without causal use is negative; shuffled re-entry is negative; a hyperedge reducible to pairwise is negative; macro intervention without micro semantics is invalid; truthful lesion/rescue chain is positive. Do not train 'high synergy = consciousness' or 'high emergence = good'.

## 13. Final synthesis

The most important conclusion is not a new 'theory of emergence'. It is a stronger experimental architecture.

Clay/FRANK can remain rigorously GWT-consistent while allowing very unconventional mechanisms: dynamic topologies, temporary hyperedges, activity-silent episode persistence, target-specific causal macrostates, nested local pre-workspaces, counterfactual shadow candidates and adaptive coupling controllers. None of those violate GWT provided the system still exposes a real capacity-limited competition, a selected globally addressable episode, receiver-specific use, outcome and re-entry, and provided shadow/local structures do not acquire independent effect authority.

The wild ideas therefore become scientifically useful because each receives a simple death test. If a pairwise reduction reproduces a hyperedge, delete the claim. If shuffled re-entry works equally well, delete the causal-closure claim. If a macro intervention changes meaning across micro realizations, reject the macro variable. If larger workspace capacity always wins, discard the bottleneck optimum. If a controller only raises synergy while harming task success or stability, rollback it. If physical consolidation changes causal lesion signatures, H3 is not semantically equivalent.

That combination — GWT invariants plus aggressive falsification — is the best path found in this run toward measurable functional emergence without mysticism, anatomy lock-in, scalar Goodharting or consciousness overclaim.
