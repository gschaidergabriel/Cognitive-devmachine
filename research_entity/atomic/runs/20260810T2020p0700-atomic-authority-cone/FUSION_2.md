# FUSION 2 — external collision and recombination

Fresh research destroys several easy novelty claims. Information-flow labels, robust declassification, proof-carrying execution/authorization, algebraic effects, runtime shields, task-alignment shields, per-call scope gates, provenance-vs-authorization graphs, permission IR + SMT, and proof-carrying agent actions all occupy nearby territory.

The surviving mechanism-level distinction is narrower:

- authority becomes a monotone feasible-action region, not a scalar label;
- authenticated intent/standing delegated capability may seed or expand the region;
- ordinary cognition, web evidence, model output, GWT salience, translator output and graph activation may only keep or contract it;
- GWT has two channels: unrestricted cognitive/evidence competition and authority-constrained effect-bearing competition;
- proactive goals may generate arbitrary proposals, but actionable space is proposal-space intersected with standing verified authority;
- the final deterministic effect gate remains mandatory.

This does not establish novelty beyond searched scope. Classification: `NOVEL_COMBINATION_CANDIDATE` with strong prior-art adjacency.
