# ATOMIC — CARF-001 Causal Algebraic Re-entry Fabric

## Final verdict

**CARF-001 is refuted as a distinct runtime architecture/data structure.**

The strongest conventional baseline — a typed factorized dependency DAG plus an explicit responsibility evaluator — matches CARF's exact synthetic credit result and has exactly the same factor-node count on the structured factorization benchmark. No separate CARF runtime organ is justified.

A narrower result survives: **CARF is retained as a ClayCode causal-credit contract candidate.** The useful question is whether the currently underspecified `causal_credit(outcome, ws)` step should become a typed, current-epoch, outcome-verified responsibility witness mandatory before material slow plasticity.

## Redundant-cause discriminator

For `(a AND b) OR (c AND d)` with all atoms true, broad/ancestor credit gives all four full binary credit; first-path credits only `a,b`; exact responsibility assigns all four score 1/2 because each becomes critical under a one-atom contingency. The useful distinction is internal dependency responsibility, not metaphysical causation.

## Complexity

Naive exact responsibility becomes rapidly more expensive as active atoms grow. Local Python p50/p95 were approximately 0.032/0.151 ms at cap 6, 0.119/0.389 ms at 8, 0.289/1.694 ms at 10, 1.274/5.585 ms at 12 and 2.528/15.193 ms at 14. Therefore exact responsibility cannot be assumed to be a universal per-cycle operation. A production contract requires a bounded causal cone and explicit `ABSTAIN` on budget exhaustion.

## Factorization and architecture kill

For a family with four alternatives per conjunctive group, explicit derivation count rises from 256 paths at four groups to 65,536 paths at eight groups while the factor representation grows from 21 to 41 nodes. But a strong conventional factorized DAG uses exactly the same 21, 26, 31, 36 and 41 nodes as CARF. That kills the claim of a distinct compression geometry.

## ClayCode candidate

The semantic candidate defines typed provenance atoms, `AnyOf`/`AllOf` derivations, episode/semantic/state epoch binding, exact/bounded/abstaining responsibility witnesses, a hard separation of broadcast/delivery/reconstruction from causal credit, and a requirement for verified outcome plus current witness before material slow plasticity.

It also explicitly states that responsibility is not probability, truth, self identity, user intent or effect authority. UnifiedDB may persist a normal factorized DAG. ClayGraph remains derived diagnostic topology. No current WP04 type/effect/compiler/runtime acceptance is claimed.

## Strongest counterhypothesis

The null hypothesis currently wins at runtime level: existing typed receipts/factorized dependency DAG plus responsibility evaluator and epoch checks can satisfy the semantic requirement with less architecture.

The remaining unresolved question is compiler integration. A ClayCode witness could still be useful if it rejects non-bypass mutants that ordinary declarations, lints and runtime assertions miss.
