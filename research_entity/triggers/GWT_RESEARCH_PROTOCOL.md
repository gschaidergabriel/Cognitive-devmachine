## ClayGraph — mandatory workspace substrate input

Every GWT run MUST deeply inspect the newest ClayGraph accepted/candidate architecture, activation/assembly semantics, self-use benchmarks, graph evolution decisions, dynamic Harness ClayGraphs and negative results.

GWT research must explicitly distinguish:

- ordinary graph spreading activation from workspace candidate competition;
- graph salience/edge weights from selective ignition;
- graph reachability from actual global addressability/broadcast;
- addressability from delivery;
- delivery from reconstruction;
- reconstruction from uptake;
- uptake from local causal use;
- local causal use from joint outcome;
- outcome from provenance-bound re-entry;
- graph persistence from causal outcome/re-entry;
- retrieval success from workspace-mediated task effect.

Current compact causal law:

`global_addressability != delivery != reconstruction != uptake != causal_use != outcome != reentry`

ClayGraph may become a substrate for candidate generation, associative context, audience routing, coupling analysis or re-entry, but those roles must be causally tested rather than assumed.
