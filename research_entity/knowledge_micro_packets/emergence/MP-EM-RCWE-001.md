### Core

Receiver-Closed Workspace Emergence (RCWE) proposes that functional emergence is not identified by ignition or broadcast alone. Evidence requires a closed, intervention-sensitive path from selection through receiver reconstruction and local causal use to outcome and provenance-bound re-entry that changes later workspace competition.

### Evidence / boundary

This refines the repository's existing stage-separation law and is compatible with GWT. It remains an open synthesis. A high integration, synergy or criticality proxy cannot substitute for a missing receiver stage, and the mechanism does not imply phenomenal consciousness.

### Implication

Each WorkspaceEpisode needs stage-specific receipts and a ReceiverClosureMatrix. Measurements remain a vector; UNKNOWN cannot be silently converted to zero or a positive claim.

### Next test

Freeze model outputs and BroadcastEnvelope, then lesion reconstruction, uptake, causal use and re-entry separately. Compare truthful re-entry with time-, receiver- and provenance-shuffled controls.
