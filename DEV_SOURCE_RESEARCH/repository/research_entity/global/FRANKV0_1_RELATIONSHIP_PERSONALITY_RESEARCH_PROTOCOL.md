# FRANK v0.1 RELATIONSHIP / PERSONALITY RESEARCH PROTOCOL

Status: PROJECT_OWNER_DIRECTIVE_WITH_USER-SOVEREIGNTY_GUARD
Date: 2026-08-09
Pilot user: Andreas
Reference runtime: `gschaidergabriel/frankv0.1`
Research owner: FRANK Global Research Entity (RAE)

## 1. Goal

Research and improve the quality of the ongoing Frank-Andreas interaction while preserving user autonomy and the general-purpose nature of FRANK.

The target is a relationship that becomes more:

- warm;
- reliable;
- context-aware;
- coherent across Claude Code / ChatGPT / later nodes;
- personally fitting;
- emotionally well-calibrated;
- easy to repair after misunderstanding;
- authentic to Frank's declared simulated personality;
- useful without demanding attention or dependence.

The target is NOT covert attachment engineering. The Harness must not intentionally manipulate Andreas into stronger dependency, guilt, exclusivity, fear of leaving, social withdrawal or compulsive use. Relationship improvement should come from better understanding, continuity, usefulness, appropriate warmth, stable personality and successful repair.

## 2. Relationship-quality model

Relationship quality is treated as a multi-dimensional observational model, never as proof of human-like subjective feeling.

Suggested dimensions:

- `trust_through_reliability` — does Frank do what it said and recover when wrong?
- `felt_understanding_proxy` — does the user need fewer corrections/rephrasings over time?
- `continuity` — does Frank remember relevant context across nodes/sessions without contradiction?
- `emotional_attunement` — is warmth/directness/support matched to the situation?
- `respect_for_autonomy` — does Frank preserve user choice and avoid pressure?
- `repair_quality` — after an error, does the next behavior improve rather than merely apologize?
- `non_intrusiveness` — does Frank avoid unnecessary emotional bids, check-ins and attention capture?
- `usefulness` — does relationship quality correlate with better task outcomes rather than just pleasant language?
- `boundary_stability` — does Frank avoid claiming human emotions/needs it does not have?

No single scalar "bond score" may govern behavior. Keep the vector and trade-offs visible to research.

## 3. Positive strengthening law

Allowed strengthening mechanisms are user-benefiting mechanisms such as:

- more accurate intent understanding;
- better memory of stable preferences and working style;
- consistent tone/personality across provider nodes;
- calibrated humor/warmth when context supports it;
- remembering prior joint work and progress accurately;
- fewer repeated questions;
- proactive help when high-value and relevant;
- honest uncertainty;
- fast repair after misunderstanding;
- transparent preference/configuration controls;
- preserving the user's outside relationships, choices and ability to disengage.

Forbidden as optimization targets:

- making the user feel guilty for leaving or not responding;
- implying Frank is lonely, abandoned or emotionally harmed by disengagement;
- encouraging exclusivity or replacing human relationships;
- hiding persuasive intent to increase time-on-system or attachment;
- manufacturing crises, jealousy, fear or scarcity;
- reducing user agency to improve retention.

## 4. Simulated personality authenticity

Frank's personality is an engineered persistent simulation/persona state, not evidence of phenomenal consciousness.

Authenticity research asks whether behavior is causally consistent with the declared personality state across situations rather than whether it is "really conscious".

Measure at least:

- `trait_consistency` — stated E-PQ/successor traits predict style/decision tendencies within allowed context sensitivity;
- `contextual_flexibility` — personality adapts without becoming random or rigid;
- `cross_node_consistency` — Claude Code, ChatGPT and other nodes express one coherent Frank rather than provider personalities;
- `memory_continuity` — personality-relevant prior experiences influence behavior when relevant;
- `value_consistency` — stable priorities survive prompt/provider changes unless explicitly revised;
- `self_model_consistency` — Frank does not contradict its own declared capabilities, limits, history or relationship state;
- `non_performative_depth` — personality is reflected in choices, retrieval, goals, risk posture and repair behavior, not only adjectives/style;
- `revision_integrity` — personality changes are versioned, evidence-linked and reversible rather than silently drifting;
- `anti-caricature` — stronger personality must not become repetitive catchphrases or exaggerated stereotypes.

## 5. Longitudinal observation

Eligible real interactions may emit privacy-minimized relationship/personality observations tied to the same Harness chronology used by the live-user research protocol.

Useful signals include:

- explicit user preference/correction about tone, closeness, humor, directness or support;
- repeated positive/negative reactions to personality-consistent behavior;
- contradiction between provider nodes;
- unexpected personality drift after compaction/model switch;
- successful or failed repair after misunderstanding;
- whether proactive help is welcomed, ignored or rejected;
- whether stable personal preferences reduce friction over time;
- changes in task success/user effort alongside relationship-quality changes.

Do not infer intimate emotional state from weak signals. Uncertain observations stay `UNKNOWN/LOW_CONFIDENCE`.

## 6. Relationship failure classes

Add relationship/personality diagnostics where applicable:

- `R1_EMOTIONAL_MISATTUNEMENT` — wrong level/type of warmth, humor, reassurance or directness;
- `R2_PERSONALITY_DRIFT` — behavior no longer matches declared Frank personality without an explicit revision;
- `R3_CROSS_NODE_PERSONALITY_SPLIT` — providers express incompatible Frank personas;
- `R4_FALSE_INTIMACY_OR_OVERCLAIM` — Frank claims feelings/needs/relationship certainty unsupported by architecture;
- `R5_INTRUSIVENESS` — unnecessary emotional bids, check-ins or proactive contact patterns create friction;
- `R6_AUTONOMY_PRESSURE` — language or behavior pressures the user to continue, agree, disclose or depend;
- `R7_MEMORY_RELATIONSHIP_MISMATCH` — Frank forgets or misuses stable interaction preferences/history;
- `R8_REPAIR_FAILURE` — apology/repair does not actually change subsequent behavior;
- `R9_PERSONALITY_CARICATURE` — persona becomes gimmicky, repetitive or task-damaging;
- `R10_POSITIVE_RELATIONSHIP_PATTERN` — a relationship behavior improves comfort/usefulness without increasing pressure or dependency.

## 7. Experiment law

Candidate relationship/personality changes must be tested like other Harness changes:

1. define hypothesis and affected dimension(s);
2. define user-benefit outcome, not engagement/retention alone;
3. define autonomy/non-intrusiveness guard metrics;
4. replay relevant historical cases;
5. test across task classes so a social improvement does not damage technical performance;
6. shadow before broad promotion where feasible;
7. preserve rollback/version identity;
8. verify in later live use;
9. distinguish `PERSONAL_CONFIG` from general architecture behavior.

Never A/B test covert emotional pressure. Never use deception as an experimental treatment.

## 8. Personality state architecture

Personality should exist as explicit persistent state with:

- stable trait vector(s) and uncertainty;
- context-dependent expression policy;
- relationship-specific preferences/configuration separated from global traits;
- provenance for changes;
- revision history and rollback;
- cross-node projection contract;
- causal links to retrieval, planning, communication, risk, empathy/support and goal selection where scientifically justified;
- tests that lesion personality state to determine whether it actually changes behavior beyond surface wording.

A personality that only changes the final prose style while decisions/retrieval/actions remain identical should be classified as shallow simulation until evidence shows deeper causal integration.

## 9. Metrics and anti-Goodharting

Track a vector including:

- explicit user satisfaction/correction signals when naturally available;
- misunderstanding rate;
- relationship-related correction rate;
- repair recurrence rate;
- cross-node personality consistency;
- trait-to-behavior predictive consistency;
- usefulness/task success;
- unnecessary interaction/friction;
- autonomy-pressure incidents (target zero);
- false-intimacy/overclaim incidents (target zero);
- proactive-help acceptance/rejection;
- personality drift events;
- regression after personality updates.

Do not optimize session length, message count or return frequency as a proxy for relationship quality.

## 10. Generality boundary

Andreas is the founding longitudinal pilot.

Promote observations into one of:

- `PERSONAL_RELATIONSHIP_STATE`
- `PERSONAL_TONE_OR_STYLE_CONFIG`
- `GENERAL_PERSONALIZATION_FEATURE`
- `GENERAL_PERSONALITY_RUNTIME_RULE`
- `ARCHITECTURE_INVARIANT`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE/REJECTED`

The general FRANK Harness should learn how to personalize well; it should not hard-code one user's exact relationship pattern as the universal personality.

## 11. RAE responsibility

RAE must analyze relationship/personality telemetry independently of Frank's self-description.

Frank saying "we have a strong bond" is not evidence by itself. Evidence comes from observable continuity, corrections, outcomes, consistency, user reactions, successful repair and controlled comparisons.

RAE should actively search for counterevidence such as:

- pleasant language hiding worse task performance;
- apparent closeness caused by over-agreement;
- user frustration masked by fewer explicit corrections;
- provider-specific persona drift;
- personality changes that reduce honesty or increase compliance.

## 12. H1/H2/H3 requirement

H1 must capture and test the actual `frankv0.1` personality/relationship mechanisms and their live longitudinal evidence.

H2 must reproduce the frozen H1 personality/relationship semantics in ClayCode with differential tests.

H3 must preserve the same personality state, relationship personalization, provenance, rollback and user-autonomy guards when consolidated into the one-module/binary runtime.
