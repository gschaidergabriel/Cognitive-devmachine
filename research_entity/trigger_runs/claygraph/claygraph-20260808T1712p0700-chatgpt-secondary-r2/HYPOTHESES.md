# ClayGraph Cycle 4 — G1d Hypotheses

All hypotheses are research candidates. Historical source recurrence is not independent support.

1. **H-CG4-01 Sparse latent field:** Event-driven lazy decay/local propagation can keep median activation-update cost below 10% of a matched full-field sweep while preserving the same bounded active roots on the current corpus. Falsifier: no material update saving or systematic missed relevant roots.
2. **H-CG4-02 Memory-through-thinking effect:** A pre-existing latent activation state changes a downstream 4B operation/answer in a beneficial way under matched visible information, token budget and order compared with query-only retrieval. Falsifier: no gain after controls or increased false association.
3. **H-CG4-03 Epoch consistency:** Epoch-pinned Assemblies eliminate mixed semantic-version reads under concurrent projector updates. Falsifier: any Assembly contains mutually impossible records caused solely by crossing graph epochs.
4. **H-CG4-04 Adaptive compaction:** A workload-aware delta/rebuild controller beats fixed always-incremental and always-rebuild policies on total update+query+memory cost without replay divergence. Falsifier: fixed policy Pareto-dominates across representative workloads.
5. **H-CG4-05 Overlay crossover depends on read/write ratio:** Change fraction alone is insufficient to choose compaction because overlay query tax can outweigh cheap publication under read-heavy workloads. Falsifier: one static change threshold generalizes across workload ratios.
6. **H-CG4-06 Activation cannot safely precede publish:** Allowing activation to reference an unvalidated/unpublished semantic delta increases stale/phantom association errors. Falsifier: pre-publish activation is provably equivalent under atomic source semantics.
7. **H-CG4-07 Assembly epoch tuple:** Pinning `(graph_epoch, activation_epoch, CognitiveFrame)` materially improves debugging/replay of memory-influenced decisions. Falsifier: tuple adds overhead without increasing causal reconstruction or stale detection.
8. **H-CG4-08 Explicit inhibition needed:** Decay alone cannot sufficiently suppress recurrent high-degree/noisy hubs under long-running event streams. Falsifier: bounded decay/saturation alone matches inhibition on false activation and recall.
9. **H-CG4-09 Negative-history routing:** Audit/contradiction tasks require a status-aware override that can activate REFUTED/SUPERSEDED/STALE material even when ordinary current-state routing suppresses it. Falsifier: same retrieval policy matches audit recall without current-task pollution.
10. **H-CG4-10 Fast/slow separation:** Fast local processes can maintain useful latent activation while expensive model/graph traversal remains selectively invoked. Falsifier: model-visible benefit requires continuous expensive inference/traversal.
11. **H-CG4-11 Physically plural logical world:** A unified semantic/activation plane can improve continuity without physical DB monolithization. Falsifier: required relational semantics or atomicity cannot be achieved without store merger after parity/recovery testing.
12. **H-CG4-12 Historical mechanism survival:** The older `incremental relations -> activation field -> Assembly` ordering remains architecture-useful after current G1c latency evidence. Falsifier: actual implementation shows latent field/epoch machinery adds complexity without retrieval/decision benefit.
13. **H-CG4-13 Small-model leverage:** A 4B model gains more from structured epoch-pinned Assembly than a larger model at matched facts/context because it spends fewer parameters reconstructing state. Falsifier: no interaction with model size.
14. **H-CG4-14 Field priming vs explicit retrieval:** State-driven preactivation improves reaction to events where the agent would otherwise fail to initiate a memory search. Falsifier: a cheap retrieval router triggers equivalently on all tested events.
15. **H-CG4-15 Bounded active fraction:** Useful latent activation remains sparse under realistic long-running streams rather than saturating the graph. Falsifier: active fraction grows monotonically/noisily without frequent expensive global resets.
16. **H-CG4-16 Outcome-conditioned plasticity:** Typed outcome receipts can safely modulate future activation weights better than unconditioned recency/frequency. Falsifier: no causal benefit or Goodhart/noise amplification under lesions and shuffled outcomes.
