## Atomic claim

The strongest current OPEN FRANK architecture separates four operations that were easy to conflate:

`presence -> admissibility -> selection -> authority`.

A value can be present/authentic/retrievable without being admissible for a consequential path; it can be admissible without winning the system workspace; a workspace winner or privileged model-internal representation never becomes execution authority merely through cognitive privilege.

For effects, the corresponding contract is:

`EffectIntent (model-constructible, non-authoritative)`
`-> opaque host-minted EffectPermit`
`-> release-time basis/source/selection/revocation/closure/recovery/resource revalidation`
`-> effect commit receipt`.
