# Clay G9 negative and limiting results

1. **Always-graph hot path remains rejected/HOLD.** Current runner-scale graph activation is much slower than deterministic FTS; G9 keeps graph use as escalation pending target evidence.
2. **Always-overlay and always-rebuild are both rejected as universal maintenance policies.** Fresh C4 and G9 economics show a workload/read-count crossover.
3. **One fixed 5%/10% compaction threshold is not universal.** In the seeded G9 workload both had materially more regret than read-aware selection.
4. **Lazy activation is not free memory.** It eliminates global decay work but materialized low-salience entries accumulate; active-set and stored-entry budgets differ.
5. **Maintenance-debt policy is not universally better.** In `EXP-G9-MAINTENANCE-DEBT-008`, debt-aware rebuilding reduced p95 (1.1125→0.8034 cost units) but worsened mean (2.4954→2.9380) and rebuilt more often (100→218).
6. **Cryptographic/metadata projection integrity is insufficient.** Hash/basis/reader-version checks cannot detect trusted deterministic miscompilation.
7. **Compiled small-model projection is not equivalent to deterministic execution.** Groq 8B improved raw 6/10→compiled 8/10 with fewer tokens, yet still made a wrong min/max choice and omitted a unique answer.
8. **Compact model-readable semantics cannot be safety authority.** Prior 8B OTCC/Gamma proxy produced unsafe ACCEPTs; G9 narrows rather than expands model authority.
9. **Record provenance is not selection integrity.** External selection-integrity work provides a concrete counterexample class where authenticated facts can be redirected by structural selection.
10. **Unlabeled pairwise role control from earlier reader work was leaky.** G9 randomized serialization; unlabeled exact role recovery fell to ~1/6 by construction, so earlier positional success must not be interpreted as preserved n-ary semantics.
11. **Freshness does not imply frame consistency.** A newly refreshed workspace can still be assembled from incompatible source/graph epochs.
12. **Graph activation does not equal GWT competition, broadcast or ignition.** It is an accessibility prior unless causal workspace mechanisms are separately instrumented.
13. **Transport does not equal semantic reconstruction/uptake.** Prior 8B projection lesion remains a separate evidence family.
14. **Persistent activation/recurrence does not equal emergence.** G9 matched-persistence proxy had similar autocorrelation but functional lesion delta 0.16175 versus echo delta 0.
15. **Graph-memory self-evolution is not automatically beneficial.** Reader-writer feedback can also amplify poisoning/selection bias and must be audited.
16. **Physical graph-database consolidation remains unsupported.** Semantic graph layers can coexist with local SQLite/FTS/exact stores; no target evidence yet requires one graph DB.
17. **Server measurements do not set Android policy.** Speculation/KV/thermal/backend behavior remains target-specific.
18. **Cross-branch agreement is not independent replication.** G9, ClayGraph C4, GWT C4 and ClayCode CC9 are concurrent and share RAE ancestry.
19. **OpenAlex broad G9 query was low precision.** It is provider/discovery evidence only.
20. **Semantic Scholar failed with HTTP 429.** It is recorded as RATE_LIMITED, not silently represented as successful.
21. **Groq 8B does not close Qwen3-4B target questions.** Tokenizer, LoRA/internalization, hidden activation, local latency/RAM/energy/thermal and live FRANK remain open.
22. **No current result supports phenomenal-consciousness inference.** GWT, emergence, recurrence, self-report or graph activation are insufficient for that claim.
