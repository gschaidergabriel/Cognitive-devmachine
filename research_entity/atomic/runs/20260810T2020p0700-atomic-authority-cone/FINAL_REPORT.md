# ATOMIC — AUTHORITY CONE GWT V1

## Result

One radical ClayCode-native possibility was constructed and measured: `AUTHORITY-CONE-GWT-V1`.

The mechanism treats authenticated effect authority as a monotone feasible-action region. Authenticated direct intent, standing delegated capability, or verified recovery may seed that region. Ordinary cognition, model output, GWT salience, open-web evidence, translator output and ClayGraph activation may not expand it; they may only keep, contract or intersect it. Effect-bearing GWT competition is restricted to candidates inside the region, while actionless evidence/cognition remains unrestricted. Needle and the deterministic final effect-authority gate remain mandatory.

## Why it was selected

Current BASE has WP01/WP02 PASS and WP03/WP04 OPEN. WP03/WP04 must encode typed AgencyState, ingress-authority classes and deterministic effect separation. The selected problem was possible wasted GWT/recurrent work on action candidates that are destined to fail authority checks, especially when untrusted evidence is highly salient or proactive goals generate broad proposal spaces.

## Fresh external collision

The search found substantial near prior art: decentralized information-flow control, robust declassification, proof-carrying code/authorization, algebraic effect systems, runtime shielding, Task Shield, ScopeGate, AuthGraph, FAVA and proof-carrying agent actions. Therefore this packet makes no world-first claim. Classification is `NOVEL_COMBINATION_CANDIDATE`, with high equivalence risk.

## Proxy experiment

Synthetic setup: 5,000 episodes per attack condition, 8 authorized candidates per episode, unauthorized high-salience candidates in {0,5,20,100}, recurrence budget 3, deterministic seed 202608102020.

At 20 unauthorized high-salience candidates:

- late final gate: task success 2.38%; mean 80.271 GWT candidate evaluations;
- Authority Cone preselection: task success 100%; mean 8 evaluations;
- blanket web-taint baseline: task success 64.62%; mean 2.1046 evaluations.

At zero attack candidates, late gate and Authority Cone were identical: 100% success and 8 evaluations.

All strategies had zero unauthorized external effects by construction because the final deterministic effect gate was retained. Therefore the experiment supports only an upstream selection/work hypothesis, not improved final effect safety.

## Live-refresh correction during the run

While the ATOMIC run was active, current main advanced with a deterministic reference `EffectGate` and acceptance tests. Those tests explicitly preserve authenticated/scoped effect semantics and ensure model/GWT/ClayGraph salience cannot mint authority.

This materially strengthens the simplest counterhypothesis: for fully specified `EffectRequest`s, just apply the existing deterministic EffectGate before effect-bearing GWT ranking. That may produce the same candidate set as V1 Authority Cone with less architecture.

The current proxy does not defeat that counterhypothesis.

## Decision

`RETEST`, not promote.

What survived:

- late-only authority gating after unrestricted effect-bearing candidate competition can be wasteful in the synthetic salience-flood model;
- web evidence should not be blanket-blocked because useful authorized web-informed actions are lost;
- pre-GWT authority-aware pruning deserves a real differential test.

What did not survive as a supported claim:

- Authority Cone is not shown superior to the simpler early-EffectGate alternative;
- no target-VPS performance claim exists;
- no real GWT/Frank benefit is established;
- no world-level novelty claim exists;
- no final-effect safety gain is established.

## Strongest next falsifier

Use partially specified action synthesis where authority constrains verb/resource/parameter/provenance domains before a full `EffectRequest` exists. Compare under matched semantics and resources:

A. full candidate construction + early existing EffectGate;
B. ordinary set/CSP constraint prefilter;
C. AuthorityCone incremental constraint propagation.

Only retain Authority Cone as a distinct architecture mechanism if C produces measurable benefit not explained by B, while preserving zero authority violations and no false-deny regression.

Also require stale/revoked cone replay, shuffled cone-to-episode control, inside-cone malicious-data control, real structured-web ingress + GWT traces, full raw-row persistence and target-VPS benchmark.
