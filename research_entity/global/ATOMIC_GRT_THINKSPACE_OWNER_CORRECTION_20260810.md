## Correction

The phrase **"within GWT thinkable"** in the initial ATOMIC trigger was a semantic mistake.

The intended owner-fixed meaning is:

> **ATOMIC searches what is thinkable within the Generative Reality Theory (GRT).**

GRT is the outer generative possibility / reality-model space used by ATOMIC.

GWT is **not** the outer boundary of thinkability. GWT remains a researchable architecture/mechanism inside the GRT search space: a possible selection, competition, ignition, broadcast, reconstruction, causal-use and re-entry geometry among other possible mechanisms.

Canonical relation:

`GRT_CONSISTENT -> THINKABLE`

`THINKABLE != TRUE`

`THINKABLE != SUPPORTED`

`THINKABLE != AUTHORIZED`

`GWT_CONSISTENT` may be a property of a candidate that uses GWT, but it is neither necessary nor sufficient for global ATOMIC thinkability unless the candidate specifically claims a GWT mechanism.
