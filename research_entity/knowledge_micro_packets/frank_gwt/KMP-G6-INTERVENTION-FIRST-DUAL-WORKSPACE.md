### Core
Agreement between FRANK's receipt-defined workspace and a latent J-space/ignition-like model marker is not sufficient evidence of a shared causal mechanism. The useful test is intervention: independently perturb each channel and compare function-specific downstream effects, rescue and re-entry.

### Evidence / boundary
A G6 common-cause proxy retained strong observational predictivity (R² 0.678) while direct receipt lesion had zero structural effect; a causal-mediator proxy showed substantial lesion effect. Current J-space and Ignition Index work supplies distinct functional markers rather than one universal workspace score. None establishes phenomenal consciousness.

### Implication
Store workspace evidence as an intervention-signature vector: receipts/order/effects, ignition dynamics, latent reportability/mediation, lesion/rescue and re-entry. Cross-channel divergence is diagnostic rather than automatically an error.

### Next test
Instrument an open local model and perform matched dual cross-lesions plus shuffled/delayed re-entry controls.
