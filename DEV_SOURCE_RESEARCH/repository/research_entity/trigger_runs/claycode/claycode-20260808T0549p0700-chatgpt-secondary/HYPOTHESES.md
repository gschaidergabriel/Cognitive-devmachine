## Transformed synthesis

CC6 proposed a semantic IR separated from projection and authority. G6 added root/epoch-bound state-aware decoding. CC7 refines the latter: **the global root remains the identity of the basis snapshot, but commit-time freshness should be checked against a deterministic dependency/invariant closure derived by trusted operation semantics.** A global-root mismatch is therefore not automatically fatal; it is a signal to revalidate/rebase. If the relevant closure changed, semantics are unknown, or an invariant is not proven merge-safe, the proposal escalates or fails closed.

This is an OPEN architecture candidate, not a canonical promotion.
