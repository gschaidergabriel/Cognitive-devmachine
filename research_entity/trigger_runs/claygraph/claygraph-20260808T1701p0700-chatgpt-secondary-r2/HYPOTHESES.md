# ClayGraph Cycle 3 — G1c hypotheses

All hypotheses are OPEN unless explicitly marked. Imported Groq results are 8B proxy evidence, not Qwen-4B/live-FRANK proof.

1. **H-CG3-001 Resident index latency.** Moving node/edge/index structures resident and using inverted seed indexes will reduce current-corpus ClayGraph retrieval p95 by at least 5x versus per-query reload/scan. Falsifier: <2x p95 improvement or memory/update cost erases benefit.
2. **H-CG3-002 Adaptive escalation.** Routing factual/local queries to exact/packet/FTS and graph-escalating relational/scattered queries will preserve or improve answer quality while reducing median retrieval latency/context versus always-on graph. Falsifier: relational misses rise materially or cost savings vanish.
3. **H-CG3-003 Scatter threshold.** Graph traversal benefit increases with evidence scatter/multihop need and is negligible or harmful on concentrated factual queries. Falsifier: no interaction between scatter class and graph benefit on independent gold set.
4. **H-CG3-004 Graph as discovery, reranker as selector.** Graph expansion is most useful for candidate discovery; final evidence selection should be lexical/vector/learned reranking under fixed budget. Falsifier: traversal score alone matches/exceeds reranked answer quality and stale control.
5. **H-CG3-005 Role-explicit projection.** Explicit subject/predicate/object/role labels outperform aggressive compact relation projection for <=8B readers at matched semantics despite extra tokens. Falsifier: compact projection matches role-binding/safety accuracy at lower context cost.
6. **H-CG3-006 Packet baseline retention.** Runtime deterministic active-packet envelopes remain useful as safety/grounding baseline even after graph routing improves. Falsifier: G1c strictly dominates packet envelope across recall, negative/contradiction retention, latency and failure recovery.
7. **H-CG3-007 Cost-aware stop.** Marginal-evidence-gain stopping reduces context growth without sacrificing multihop recall. Falsifier: early stopping disproportionately removes necessary bridge evidence.
8. **H-CG3-008 Exact-anchor split.** Graph navigation can improve relational recall while exact-anchor validation prevents stale/current-state errors. Falsifier: exact validation adds no safety value or cannot detect injected stale assertions.
9. **H-CG3-009 Resident update crossover.** Incremental resident-index maintenance wins below a measured change-fanout threshold; deterministic rebuild wins above it. Falsifier: one policy dominates across real change regimes.
10. **H-CG3-010 Family-aware reranking.** Soft evidence-family penalties plus role diversity outperform hard one-per-family caps. Falsifier: hard caps match recall/decision quality with lower context on independent tests.
11. **H-CG3-011 Negative-state routing.** `STALE/REFUTED/SUPERSEDED/NEGATIVE_RESULT` should be suppressed for ordinary factual queries but actively surfaced for audit/contradiction queries. Falsifier: query-conditioned status routing loses necessary evidence or increases false conclusions.
12. **H-CG3-012 GWT separation.** ClayGraph assembly quality and GWT winner/ignition are causally distinct; relation-shuffling before workspace arbitration should alter memory evidence without being confounded with broadcast order. Falsifier: effects cannot be separated under matched projection/order controls.
13. **H-CG3-013 4B transfer boundary.** Groq 8B direction will not be assumed to transfer to Qwen-class 4B; explicit role labels and smaller assemblies may be even more important at 4B. Falsifier/test: pinned 4B matched-budget factorial.
14. **H-CG3-014 No migration from retrieval win.** Even a promoted retrieval architecture does not imply physical database merge; storage migration requires independent replay/consistency/authority evidence. Falsifier: a store-specific shadow migration proves semantic, transactional and recovery equivalence with lower total cost.
15. **H-CG3-015 Graph lexical vs activation.** If resident lexical graph selection matches spreading activation on most queries, activation should be reserved for explicit relational classes. Falsifier: activation yields robust independent answer-quality gains after latency/context matching.
16. **H-CG3-016 Assembly certificate utility.** Route/path/evidence-family/staleness receipts reduce debugging and false confidence enough to justify metadata overhead. Falsifier: no measurable diagnosis/recovery benefit.
