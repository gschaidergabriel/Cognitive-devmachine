# Level 0 Exhaustive Corpus Completion Law

**Status:** PROJECT_OWNER_FIXED
**Applies to:** exact `666` while `L0_ARCHIVE_ASSIMILATION` is open
**Authority:** project owner directive, 2026-08-09

## Core law

Level 0 means exhaustive assimilation of the complete frozen historical corpus. It is not a sampling exercise.

The two mandatory source universes are:

1. the complete frozen `clay` Google Drive epoch, recursively;
2. the complete frozen Project-Frankenstein Level-0 Git/history/tree scope.

`666_RELEASE_GATE = PASS` is forbidden until both universes and their cross-source reconciliation are completely accounted for.

## Every source unit must be accounted for

Every unit inside the frozen source universes receives a deterministic disposition. No file, commit, deleted/superseded historical unit, recovery branch, database/snapshot, model artifact, binary, asset, vendor tree, cache or generated artifact may disappear from accounting merely because it looks uninteresting.

Each unit must end in one of these evidence-backed classes:

- `SEMANTICALLY_ANALYZED`
- `CODE_ANALYZED`
- `DATA_OR_EXPERIMENT_ANALYZED`
- `MODEL_OR_BINARY_STRUCTURALLY_ANALYZED`
- `DUPLICATE_OR_ALIAS_PROVEN`
- `SUPERSEDED_LINEAGE_ANALYZED`
- `SUPPORT_VENDOR_CACHE_CLASSIFIED_ZERO_SEMANTIC_CREDIT`
- `CORRUPT_OR_UNREADABLE_WITH_EXPLICIT_BLOCKER`
- `NO_RESEARCH_DELTA_AFTER_REVIEW`

Classification is not permission to skip source inspection. The evidence needed for the classification must be recorded.

## Sampling law

Sampling, representative-file reading and heuristic search may be used only to:

- prioritize work;
- discover families;
- choose shard boundaries;
- design tests;
- estimate throughput.

They may never be used to claim whole-corpus completion.

A family can be closed without semantically rereading every byte only when an exact deterministic rule proves the remaining members are duplicates, generated/support/vendor material, or another explicitly zero-semantic class. The proof and denominator must be persisted.

## Drive law

The frozen Drive root epoch is traversed recursively. Every direct root child must have a recursively closed denominator or an explicit external-access blocker. Large archives/models that cannot be downloaded through a connector remain visible obligations; extracted mirrors may satisfy semantic coverage only when their lineage/identity relationship is proven.

## Project-Frankenstein law

The frozen Project-Frankenstein Level-0 scope is history-aware. It includes the defined commit interval/scope law, frozen-head tree, deleted/superseded mechanisms visible in history, and relevant recovery-only material reconciled from Drive.

Head-only analysis is insufficient. Commit history must be processed for material changes, failures, reversions, deletions, migrations and supersession. Exact duplicate/no-op/history-family clustering is allowed when proven.

## Reconciliation law

After source-local closure, Drive and Project-Frankenstein are reconciled for:

- exact duplicates/aliases;
- version ancestry;
- later uncommitted recovery branches;
- superseded implementations;
- contradictions;
- negative results and fixes;
- current architecture inheritance;
- historical earned-work mapping.

No duplicate receives duplicate semantic or earned-work credit.

## Completion law

Level 0 PASS requires all of the following:

- frozen Drive source universe fully accounted for;
- frozen Project-Frankenstein source universe fully accounted for;
- every atomic obligation completed or explicitly blocker-classified under the protocol;
- cross-source reconciliation complete;
- branch feedback/NO_CHANGE complete;
- historical earned-work row-level dedup/audit complete;
- release-manifest counts/hashes/receipts validate;
- exact coverage = 100% for all accessible obligations, with any genuinely inaccessible external objects explicitly enumerated and owner-visible rather than silently ignored.

The 3-hour target changes parallelism and tooling, never the completeness requirement.
