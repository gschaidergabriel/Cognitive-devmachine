# ATOMIC SWC-001 — Fusion 1

## Current problem graph

Frank v0.1 requires causally observable GWT delivery/reconstruction/use/outcome/re-entry while Hyperposition preserves unresolved alternatives and ClayGraph exposes the relevant topology. Current contracts correctly forbid treating broadcast exposure, activation, recurrence or salience as truth. A remaining structural risk is subtler: several receivers can each hold locally valid and pairwise-compatible projections while the family has no single globally coherent semantic assignment. On cyclic overlap topology, local agreement alone can therefore be an unsafe proxy for coherent global re-entry.

This is not merely a database issue. A WorkspaceEpisode may combine self/world/memory/goal/Needle/authority/provenance slices produced on different scopes and timescales. If coherence is checked only at individual receiver or pairwise-overlap level, an impossible global combination can survive long enough to be re-entered, credited or compressed. Conversely, solving every episode as a full global constraint problem can impose avoidable latency and state amplification.

## Accepted invariants

- coherence is not truth;
- coherence is not identity;
- coherence is not effect authority;
- broadcast/addressability is not delivery/reconstruction/use/outcome/re-entry;
- multiple unresolved but globally coherent alternatives must remain representable;
- zero coherent global alternatives must fail closed for coherent re-entry;
- stale epoch/provenance cannot be repaired by mathematical consistency;
- self.root may not authorize its own Harness mutation;
- final training remains owner-gated.

## High-leverage bottleneck

The selected bottleneck is **adaptive local-to-global semantic coherence**: determine the cheapest sufficient consistency procedure from the actual ClayGraph receiver-scope topology, preserving Hyperposition rather than forcing premature collapse.

## Strongest baseline set

1. current architecture semantics with receiver-local/pairwise checks only;
2. always-global conventional CSP/SAT/SMT validation;
3. conventional arc consistency followed by global solving;
4. topology-decomposed join-tree/hypertree validation;
5. no-coherence candidate ablation;
6. shuffled/provenance-broken/stale-state controls.

## Explicit non-targets

SWC-001 does not solve self.root identity lineage, binary attestation, semantic truth, effect authorization, provider reconciliation, or model-latent workspace identification. It is intentionally orthogonal to the concurrently queued LCOP native-model-workspace x ClayGWT factorial.
