# H6 Negative Results

1. **Destruction-free topology changes are not harmless.** CC13 and H6 both preserve the counterexample that a new recoverable version can be bad when made live.
2. **Atomic switching is not cognitive validation.** EXP-HYP-H6-ATOMIC-LIVE-REF-003 prevents partial live state only in an idealized model; a fully consistent wrong topology remains possible.
3. **Path-scoped credit is not automatically enough.** In EXP-HYP-H6-CREDIT-TOPOLOGY-001, chosen-path credit still concentrated on the frequent context after distribution shift.
4. **Canarying is not free.** The constructed H6 canary rejected ~29.8% of beneficial candidates while reducing bad-update exposure.
5. **Receipt-gated credit depends on a causal-credit estimator.** H6 uses a constructed support signal; target causal attribution quality is unknown.
6. **Broadcast outcome reward is unsafe as a default in the constructed family**, but the numeric rate is not a FRANK prevalence estimate.
7. **Query-adaptive graph memory evidence does not validate FRANK Hyperposition.** External systems use different data, objectives, models and update rules.
8. **Recommender feedback-loop evidence is an analogy**, not direct ClayGraph proof.
9. **Engineering canary/blue-green precedent does not establish cognitive-topology correctness.** It supports blast-radius control only.
10. **Android R1 is not an H6 mechanism result.** It proves the experimental lane and selected semantic invariants, not topology switching/IPC/rematerialization.
11. **No target Qwen, live FRANK, production capability broker, durable filesystem crash, or real distributed topology promotion was tested.**
12. **No emergence or phenomenal-consciousness conclusion follows from adaptive topology, credit assignment, recurrence, multistability or nested workspaces.**
