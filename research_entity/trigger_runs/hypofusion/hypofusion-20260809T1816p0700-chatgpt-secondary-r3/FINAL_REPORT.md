## Main conclusion

The new evidence does not justify a new canonical architecture generation. It **narrows** several active hypotheses and creates sharper discriminating tests.

### 1. Recurrence: keep the loop-of-loops, widen the controller hypothesis

A hosted synthetic GWT proxy found that pressure+trend matched fixed-12 success while using about 47.1% fewer recurrent steps and substantially outperformed delta-only halting. This is useful mechanism evidence, not live FRANK evidence.

Independent recurrent-depth research supports adaptive computation but also supplies strong countermechanisms: overthinking can degrade performance, and memory/state capacity can change the value and learnability of recurrent depth. Therefore pressure+trend is not promoted as the controller.

**H-HF3-01:** recurrence continuation should discriminate among unresolved pressure, trend, memory/state capacity, stability/overthinking, GWT/reentry and hard budget signals.

Highest-information test: matched-budget frozen H1 Ubuntu episodes, factorially ablating those signals and varying state/memory capacity.

### 2. Effectful compiler safety is a triple closure problem

Internal source-chain negative evidence already showed that provenance/hash consistency can coexist with wrong lowering. A new effect-authority proxy independently demonstrated that effect typing alone does not prevent ambient-host bypass or structurally forged capability records. External verified-compiler, WIT-resource and capability-system precedents point in the same mechanistic direction without validating ClayCode itself.

**H-HF3-02:** an effectful ClayCode path must separately close:

1. semantic preservation;
2. resource lifetime/current identity;
3. effect authority/provenance at the final gateway.

Counterhypothesis: a sufficiently small runtime/OS capability substrate may make some language-level lifetime machinery unnecessary. Test that directly on Ubuntu rather than expanding surface syntax now.

### 3. MVC state expressibility is supported; continual online learning is not yet justified

The MVC semantic proxy replayed 100,000 typed/versioned transitions and preserved open-set/rollback/budget invariants after an initially over-strong test oracle was corrected. That supports ordinary typed-state expressibility, not classifier accuracy or personalized benefit.

External open-set and prototype-learning work makes explicit UNKNOWN/rejection and prototype state plausible, but does not validate Clay's GOLD/SILVER/BRONZE policy or need for continual adaptation.

**H-HF3-03:** treat MVC as a versioned open-set/prototype control plane; compare bounded online adaptation against a frozen calibrated prototype/open-set baseline before claiming learning benefit.

### 4. Async replay: deterministic where commutative, recorded choice where causal

The async proxy observed zero schedule divergence for 43,823 disjoint pairs, divergence for all 6,177 conflicting pairs, 66,583/100,000 replay mismatches when intentional race choices were not recorded, and zero when the choice receipt was present. This rejects the strong proxy claim that one arbitrary global schedule is the sole production semantics.

**H-HF3-04:** disjoint operations can canonicalize; semantically conflicting choices belong to execution/replay identity.

Counterhypothesis: a serialized authoritative H1 may be simpler and adequate. Compare it against recorded-choice concurrency under matched utility/latency/fault coverage.

### 5. Adaptive systems make lesion causality time-dependent

The adaptive-lesion proxy showed a frozen lesion remained impaired while online adaptation moved an alternate weight toward full compensation and nearly erased delayed error. Independent neural-plasticity research provides a conceptual analogue: acute perturbation and later compensation can diverge.

**H-HF3-05:** lesion effects in adaptive FRANK are indexed by intervention time, learner-state lineage and compensation trajectory. Report immediate and delayed effects separately; include frozen-learning and state-rollback rescue arms.

### 6. H1/H2/H3 equivalence must commute under interventions

A late Emergenz experiment produced observationally equivalent H1/H3 trajectories, but a naive low-level lesion in the transformed representation changed the causal effect. Translating the semantic intervention through the representation mapping restored effect equivalence to numerical precision. Independent causal-abstraction theory likewise treats intervention translation as part of cross-level causal equivalence.

**H-HF3-06:** H1/H2/H3 equivalence requires a semantic intervention map whose counterfactual effects commute across implementations. State/output parity alone is insufficient.

This is a material refinement to the Harness differential program.

### 7. ClayGraph anti-echo should be ancestry-aware but complexity must earn its cost

The RAB synthetic benchmark dramatically reduced false reversal under correlated source echo, while ordinary context-aware scalar state already solved normal context bifurcation nearly as well or better. Higher provenance-diversity thresholds also increased adaptation lag. Independent social-learning work on data incest shows the same general failure class: reused dependent information creates overconfidence when treated as independent.

**H-HF3-07:** durable reversal evidence should carry source-family ancestry/dependence; compare RAB against a simpler source-correlation-weighted scalar before selecting architecture.

### 8. New late counterexample: capability-safe can still be epistemically unsafe

The visual-action closure proxy enumerated 720 modeled states and exposed three high-risk cases where familiarity alone could lead to a wrong-but-authorized action. A risk-sensitive evidence floor closed all modeled cases while preserving the small modeled legitimate fast-path set. Selective-classification research independently supports abstention/risk-coverage as a safety mechanism when mistakes are costly.

**H-HF3-08:** Safe-Effect geometry must distinguish **authority closure** from **epistemic-action closure**. A valid capability does not prove that the evidence basis for the chosen action is sufficient. For high-consequence visually conditioned effects, familiarity should be routing/support evidence unless an explicit calibrated risk guarantee satisfies the action evidence requirement; otherwise obtain fresh action-specific evidence/deeper perception or abstain.

### 9. Measurement can perturb the mechanism being measured

A late synthetic observer-effect timing model showed that illustrative probe delays can change deadline success and asymmetric instrumentation can change competition order. These are not measured FRANK overheads. Linux ftrace documentation independently warns that tracing overhead can extend latency and that some tracer overhead remains even when output writing is disabled.

**H-HF3-09:** latency-sensitive causal readouts need probe-mode/overhead receipts and decisive findings should survive lower-intrusion or symmetric instrumentation controls.

Counterhypothesis: chosen low-overhead target probes are negligible relative to FRANK loop slack. Measure this on Andreas' Ubuntu target.

### 10. Unicode identity needs a semantic domain boundary

A late ClayCode proxy tested 12,665 canonical-equivalence pairs and 2,411 compatibility-only pairs. It preserved a candidate split between exact scalar-sequence strings and explicit NFC-normalized text, while finding concrete counterexamples to assuming global implicit NFC or NFKC is semantics-neutral for exact-sequence operations. Unicode UAX #15 independently defines canonical vs compatibility normalization and explicitly treats normalization strategy as a system-design tradeoff.

**H-HF3-10:** distinguish exact scalar-sequence semantics from explicitly normalized text semantics and include normalization policy/version in semantic identity where observable. The proxy used Unicode data 15.1 while current normative UAX #15 is Unicode 17.0, so the next normative corpus must pin current data/version.

No semantic redesign is justified by this result.
