### Core
Authenticated facts can still be selected through a structurally manipulated/noisy graph. Therefore record provenance answers a different question from selection provenance. When selected memory can influence an irreversible effect, the selector/query plan and structural trust path are part of the trusted causal boundary.

### Architecture consequence
Exploratory cognition may use broad associative cues, but effect-bound decisions require an authorized/authenticated selection view or an equivalent integrity check/fallback before the normal capability/freshness/OTCC effect gateway.

### Falsifier
Adversarial target-graph tests must show that record-only provenance prevents all structurally induced selection redirection; otherwise the boundary remains required.
