### Core
A sharp sigmoid or threshold transition can be generated by a history-free external gate over a continuously varying latent variable. Therefore response/probe steepness is not sufficient evidence of an endogenous recurrent ignition mechanism.

### R6 calibration
The local configured-threshold control produced a near-perfect sigmoid fit (`R²≈0.99984`) while up/down history difference was near zero. Independent E2B execution reproduced the basic pattern: `p(-3)=0.001`, `p(0)=0.50125`, `p(+3)=0.99875`, with mean up/down difference about `0.00429`.

### Consequence
An `IgnitionReceipt` should include at least a configured-threshold control and one mechanism-sensitive discriminator such as recurrence/history, hysteresis/persistence, targeted lesion, or mechanism-specific rescue. Gate opening and sharp competition remain separate from ignition certification.

### Boundary
This does not invalidate the 2026 Ignition Index as a descriptive statistic. It limits the mechanistic interpretation that may be attached to sigmoid steepness alone.
