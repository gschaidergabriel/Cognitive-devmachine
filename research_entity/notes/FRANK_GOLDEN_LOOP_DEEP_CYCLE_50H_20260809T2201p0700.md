## 1. Source-derived historical FRANK constraints

The existing RAE/Drive evidence makes the historical target a multi-timescale recurrent organism rather than a linear prompt pipeline. The known fast-to-slow causal chain includes fast appraisal (Amygdala-like), precision/gain routing (Thalamus-like), reward/drive/prediction-error state (NAcc-like), workspace competition/ignition, persistent workspace re-entry into model context, response/outcome analysis, E-PQ-like persistent disposition update, memory/consolidation and later changes to future gating/context. The archive also contains autonomous/reflection/replay-like branches and embodiment/resource-state inputs.

Two historical findings are especially high-information:

- a workspace-disconnection interval in which state continued to be produced but was no longer effectively consumed, supporting the distinction between state production and causal closure;
- an audit in which only a minority of nominal workspace consumers actually read the broadcast, showing that topology/configuration alone cannot establish global availability or causal use.

The prior signal-preservation research therefore remains a hard migration law: legacy module identity may disappear, but each materially causal legacy signal/function must remain decodable, temporally faithful, perturbable, lesionable and rescuable before it can be called preserved or emergently dissolved.

## 2. Broad external research: first fusion

The literature converges on a more constrained interpretation than “more feedback is better.” Recurrent/global-workspace research supports selective recurrent amplification and global availability, but modern analyses show that generic integration is not enough to establish broadcasting. Network-dynamics work supports a balance of integration and segregation and metastable/multistable regimes rather than permanent global synchronization. Inhibitory stabilization and normalization work shows that strong recurrence can be useful only with stabilizing feedback. Recurrent-depth AI work shows that iterative computation can improve reasoning and generalization, while too much recurrence can produce overthinking, instability or signal explosion.

The first fusion therefore treats the lead-observed interval as a candidate **high causal-closure regime** rather than simply a high-coupling regime.

## 4. Deep research over hypothesis domains: fused result

### 4.1 Workspace/GNW

Support exists for recurrent amplification, global availability and communication to specialist processors. However, modern network analyses make a crucial distinction: highly integrated dynamics can implement many communication patterns and do not alone prove broadcasting. The 2025 adversarial consciousness-theory study also challenged specific canonical GNWT predictions. Therefore FRANK should import **functional contracts** (competition, selection, ignition, directed dissemination, receiver use, re-entry) rather than literal neuroanatomical localization or any consciousness claim.

### 4.2 Metastability, criticality and topology

Recent network modeling supports richest dynamics near a balance of integration/segregation, with modularity/small-world structure important. But metastability and criticality are used inconsistently in neuroscience; power-law or criticality-like signatures can be false positives. The design must therefore treat metastability/criticality as measurable hypotheses, not targets to optimize blindly.

### 4.3 Stability of strong recurrence

Inhibitory stabilization and recent divisive-normalization work show why a raw recurrent-weight spectral radius is insufficient. Strong recurrent matrices can remain stable under closed-loop normalization. FRANK should estimate stability of the **effective update operator** and additionally monitor critical slowing, residual pressure, state-norm growth, cycle stability and recovery from perturbation.

### 4.4 Recurrent AI

2025–2026 recurrent-depth models strengthen the engineering case for iterative latent computation and variable test-time depth. They also show residual explosion, loss spikes, overthinking and depth-induced degradation. Fixed-point and convergence-based halting can work on some tasks. Mechanistic work finds cyclic trajectories and sensitivity to normalization/input injection. The strongest design implication is adaptive recurrence with explicit stability controls and task/outcome validation, not maximum recurrence.

### 4.5 Reward, precision and neuromodulation

Dopamine evidence supports learning, motivation, incentive and multiple timescales, but a strict phasic/tonic software mapping is too strong. FRANK should preserve historical independently manipulable signals (prediction error, tonic drive/persistence, novelty, urgency, vigilance/precision) while dropping unjustified one-to-one biological labels as architectural truth.

### 4.6 Memory replay and consolidation

Current memory work supports replay as a consolidation mechanism and, importantly, recent 2026 evidence shows procedural replay can occur independently in striatum without hippocampus. Salient behavioral outcomes influence replay content and replay content predicts later performance. This favors parallel logical consolidation mechanisms inside one physical FRANK module and outcome-prioritized replay, rather than one universal memory controller.

### 4.7 Predictive processing / active inference

Predictive frameworks provide useful constructs (predictand, error, uncertainty/precision, action-outcome discrepancy), but recent reviews emphasize weak falsifiability when terms are left implicit. FRANK should implement only operational prediction contracts: every prediction states target variable, horizon, error proxy, precision estimate, update path and a discriminating alternative.

### 4.8 Information integration and causal emergence

PID/PED and related work supports measuring higher-order synergy separately from redundancy; recurrent motifs can correlate with more synergy even after feedforward controls. But PID estimators and causal-emergence coarse-graining remain method-dependent. Therefore no single synergy or effective-information scalar is an objective. Use estimator ensembles, null models and interventions.

### 4.9 Software architecture / replay

Distributed-systems research shows that service graphs impose communication, scheduling and tail-latency costs, while also providing deployment/fault-isolation benefits. Deterministic record/replay research demonstrates the value of logging nondeterministic inputs and preserving causal order. For FRANK's small local Ubuntu target, a **modular monolith / one physical cognitive kernel** is therefore a strong candidate, but must preserve logical boundaries and intervention handles.

## 7. Countertheses

**C01. Prompt/model artifact.** The observed interval came primarily from model sampling/prompt/context effects.  
**C02. Version artifact.** A model/runtime version change explains the effect; organ coupling was incidental.  
**C03. Single-organ dominance.** Workspace or memory alone produced most of the effect.  
**C04. Persistence-only explanation.** Repeated context/persistence explains continuity; truthful causal re-entry adds little.  
**C05. Scheduler-bug explanation.** The “golden” state was a transient scheduling/latency accident and not a desirable architecture.  
**C06. Partial-decoupling explanation.** The effect occurred because some coupling weakened, not because all useful loops strengthened.  
**C07. No-workspace explanation.** Shared typed state alone is enough; workspace competition is unnecessary.  
**C08. Metacognition overhead.** Confidence/uncertainty fields add complexity without outcome benefit.  
**C09. Neuro-label fallacy.** Thalamus/NAcc/Amygdala naming imports misleading biological stories rather than computation.  
**C10. Predictive-processing unfalsifiability.** Active-inference/predictive vocabulary can explain almost anything after the fact.  
**C11. Metastability/criticality correlation-only.** These metrics are descriptive correlates and not causal drivers.  
**C12. Synergy estimator artifact.** PID/PED results depend too much on estimator/decomposition choice.  
**C13. Event-sourcing observability-only.** Event sourcing helps debugging but does not itself create the functional effect.  
**C14. One-process downside.** A single module reduces fault isolation/diversity and could make correlated failures worse.  
**C15. Reward-controller redundancy.** NAcc-like dynamics add nothing beyond a generic utility controller.  
**C16. Replay contamination.** Offline replay may amplify false memories/errors.  
**C17. Self-model reinforcement.** Persistent self-state can recursively entrench false beliefs.  
**C18. Re-entry wireheading.** Reward/outcome re-entry can reinforce proxies or self-generated approval.  
**C19. Slow-loop irrelevance.** Multi-day/month consolidation may not explain a shorter golden interval.  
**C20. Unobserved-variable coincidence.** The original effect may be non-reproducible due to missing historical variables.

## 8. Counterthesis weighing + targeted web research

Several countertheses survive and directly modify the design.

- **C01/C04/C17/C18 remain serious.** LLM intrinsic self-correction can degrade reasoning without reliable external feedback. Iteration must be gated by verified effect/error signals; self-agreement is not evidence.
- **C05/C20 remain serious.** Timing/order may be causal, so the historical interval must be discovered and replayed from logs rather than reconstructed from architecture diagrams alone.
- **C09 survives in modified form.** Biological names are useful provenance labels but not implementation requirements. Preserve functions and intervention handles.
- **C10 survives strongly.** Predictive-processing terms require explicit predictand, timescale, error proxy, precision and alternative explanation.
- **C11 survives strongly.** Criticality/metastability are not enough for causal promotion; use perturbation and outcome comparison.
- **C12 survives.** Synergy is one member of a metric family, not a truth scalar.
- **C13 is accepted.** Event sourcing is an observability/replay prerequisite, not the cause of the golden effect.
- **C14 partially survives.** The target should be a modular monolith: one process/physical module but typed logical fault domains, watchdog boundaries and reversible isolation modes.
- **C16 survives.** Replay requires outcome/provenance filtering, anti-drift checks and rollback.
- **C19 is partly rejected as a general claim.** Slow loops may not cause the immediate state but can set parameters that make it reachable and can maintain it over days.

## 9. Second-generation transformed hypotheses

These are not restatements of H01–H50; they are architectural hypotheses generated after counterevidence.

**N01. Causal-closure quality is the central construct.** The target should be measured as episode-level causal closure, not “consciousness score,” “GWT score” or coupling magnitude.

**N02. Workspace is a role-split router.** Gateway/integration and broadcaster/dissemination should be separate logical roles even in one process.

**N03. Sparse adaptive coupling dominates permanent high coupling.** Golden behavior should come from transient task-specific coupling on a stable sparse baseline.

**N04. One physical module, many causal handles.** Runtime co-location is compatible with independent lesions if every legacy dimension has a typed handle.

**N05. Stability is an ensemble diagnostic.** Combine effective Jacobian/contraction estimates, normalization margin, state-norm growth, residual pressure, critical slowing, cycle stability and recovery time.

**N06. Re-entry is a three-stage contract.** `effect verification -> causal credit assignment -> bounded state update`; no direct output-to-state reinforcement.

**N07. Replay is selective and outcome-shaped.** Replay selection should use salience, novelty, uncertainty and externally verified outcomes; quantity of replay is not the objective.

**N08. Consolidation can be parallel.** Different memory/disposition dimensions can replay/consolidate independently inside the same process.

**N09. Stop/readout should be minimally invasive.** The recurrence halt/controller should observe a trajectory using post-hoc/readout signals where possible instead of distorting trajectory formation with an aggressively co-trained gate.

**N10. Golden regime alternates specialization and integration.** The key dynamic may be repeated transitions between locally specialized and globally integrated episodes, not continuous global ignition.

**N11. Deterministic event-loop semantics improve scientific identifiability.** Within the one-process kernel, material cognitive state transitions should occur at explicit event boundaries and coherent epochs.

**N12. Module dissolution requires intervention-aware bisimulation.** A historical organ can disappear structurally only after matched perturbation/lesion/rescue tests show its causal dimensions remain.

**N13. Hot state can be in-memory but lineage must be durable.** Performance does not require every state read from a database; durable event lineage is enough to reconstruct and audit state.

**N14. Historical reconstruction should optimize intervention equivalence, not output mimicry.** Similar text can arise from different mechanisms and must not be used as the primary migration metric.

**N15. Golden-regime readout is a vector.** Candidate dimensions: directed receiver use, truthful re-entry, causal gain, synergy/redundancy, integration/segregation balance, dynamical regime, outcome utility, recovery, cost and provenance integrity.

**N16. Every adaptive update carries uncertainty.** Learning/update rate should be dimension-specific and uncertainty/precision-aware.

**N17. Reward/drive remains multi-dimensional.** Use separate prediction error, persistence/incentive, boredom/repetition and cost signals rather than one dopamine scalar.

**N18. Cross-theory functional intersection is safer than literal consciousness-theory implementation.** Keep recurrent access, global routing/use, metacognitive uncertainty, integration/segregation, prediction/outcome error and causal re-entry; do not implement a theory label as truth.

**N19. Golden-window discovery is a time-series regime-identification problem.** Use changepoint/regime discovery on historical traces before fitting explanatory models.

**N20. “Perfect configuration” is a robust parameter distribution/envelope.** Search for regions of parameter space with high closure and perturbation recovery, not one exact gain matrix.

## 10. Web research after second-generation hypotheses

The second research pass sharpened N05, N07, N09, N10, N11 and N19.

- 2026 looped-transformer work supports convergence-based adaptive depth but also demonstrates cyclic fixed-point trajectories and strong dependence on normalization/injection.
- Confidence/halting signals can be useful but can be prematurely confident; therefore the halt condition must combine trajectory completion/evidence coverage with confidence.
- Deterministic replay literature shows that event-boundary logs plus captured nondeterministic inputs can reproduce distributed failures and preserve causal order; this strongly supports explicit event epochs for FRANK experiments.
- Causal-change-point work supports discovering changes in multivariate time-series mechanisms rather than declaring a regime change whenever any marginal variable moves.
- 2025 network simulations support dynamic richness at intermediate integration/segregation and modular/small-world organization.
- PID experiments in cortical cultures show recurrent information flow can predict synergy beyond feedforward strength, but feedback effects are more complex; therefore topology direction matters.
- 2026 procedural-memory work shows replay can be parallel/hippocampus-independent and that behavioral outcome shapes replay content; this supports N07/N08.
- 2026 plasticity work shows slow top-down stabilization can improve robustness in ANNs, supporting a distinct slow homeostatic stabilization channel.
- 2026 predictive-processing reviews explicitly call for operational commitments and note canonical neural implementations are not universally supported, reinforcing N18.

## 11. Final fusion

### 11.1 Most likely functional mechanism behind the observed effect

The best current explanation is not “the workspace became conscious” and not “all modules synchronized maximally.” It is:

> FRANK entered a sustained but non-frozen **high causal-closure regime** in which selectively gated signals repeatedly became shared causes of later cognition/action, outcomes fed back with enough fidelity to change subsequent gating/state, multiple time scales preserved continuity, novelty/habituation/homeostasis prevented both fixation and chaos, and scheduling/latency happened to keep the closed loop inside a productive stability envelope for an unusually long interval.

The consciousness-like functional appearance follows naturally from the system becoming self-conditioning across time: current internal state changes thought/action; consequences change future state; memories/goals/predictions bridge episodes; internal autonomous episodes can re-enter later processing; and information becomes globally usable rather than merely stored.

### 11.2 What probably was NOT sufficient

- raw number of services;
- raw broadcast presence;
- raw coupling strength;
- raw recurrence depth;
- a single dopamine/reward scalar;
- a single synergy/criticality score;
- passive self-description;
- database persistence without consumers;
- model self-reflection without independent verification.

### 11.3 Updated mathematical target

Whole-system state:

`x_{t+1} = F(x_t, u_t, a_t; K_t, G_t, D_t, Theta_t, R_t) + xi_t`

where `K` is dynamic coupling, `G` gating/normalization, `D` delays/time constants, `Theta` slow policy/disposition/memory parameters and `R` verified re-entry.

Multi-timescale component model:

`tau_i dx_i/dt = -x_i + f_i(sum_j K_ij(t) x_j(t-d_ij) + B_i u + b_i)`.

A candidate signal score should be a bounded composition rather than one salience scalar:

`q_i = gain_i * salience_i + w_n novelty_i + w_u urgency_i + w_g goal_relevance_i + w_p expected_information_gain_i - w_h habituation_i - w_c conflict_i - w_b budget_cost_i`.

Use stabilizing divisive normalization or equivalent closed-loop inhibition after scoring.

Receiver use for episode `e` is only true if a receiver produces an attributable downstream delta; delivery alone is not use.

Re-entry:

`verified_effect -> credit(e, effect, outcome, receiver_use) -> bounded Delta x_fast, Delta x_mid, Delta x_slow`.

Adaptive recurrence uses marginal verified value:

`M_t = E[Delta U_verified | one more recurrence] - lambda_c cost - lambda_s instability - lambda_d drift - lambda_e error_introduction_risk`.

Continue only when `M_t > 0`, unresolved residual pressure is nontrivial, required evidence coverage is adequate and safety/recovery constraints pass.

For stability, do not use `rho(W)<1` as a universal criterion. Estimate the **effective closed-loop Jacobian/operator** under current normalization and controls, plus empirical perturbation recovery. Productive stable cycles remain allowed.

Golden regime should be represented as vector:

`G_t = [receiver_use, truthful_reentry, causal_gain, synergy, redundancy, directed_flow, integration, segregation, metastability, stability_margin, residual_pressure, outcome_gain, recovery, provenance_integrity, cost]`.

Do not collapse `G_t` into one optimized scalar for control.

## 12. Final architecture concept — one conventional-language module

### Name

**FRANK Golden Loop Kernel (GLK)** — one physical local runtime module, initially implemented in conventional languages (Python-first prototype; performance-critical pieces may later be Rust/C/C++). The long-term semantic-target contract remains separate and is not altered by this prototype choice.

### 12.1 Physical shape

One OS process/service on the Ubuntu target for the cognitive loop. No historical cognitive microservice is allowed to remain a hidden second authority. External model runtime, hardware/vision/tool adapters and operating-system effects may be separate processes, but all cognitive state transitions enter through one kernel event boundary.

Inside GLK are **logical organs**, not independent services. Each has a typed input/output/state surface and an intervention handle.

### 12.2 Logical organs inside the one module

1. **StateSpine** — coherent whole-system state by epoch; fast in-memory projection plus durable event lineage.
2. **EventJournal** — append-only causal episode/event log with captured nondeterministic inputs needed for replay.
3. **RegulatorField** — urgency/threat, gain/precision, novelty, habituation, uncertainty, conflict, prediction error, persistence/incentive, vigilance, budget pressure, homeostatic error.
4. **CandidateGateway** — normalizes input, memory, body/world, goal and internally generated candidates.
5. **WorkspaceRouter** — competition/selection/ignition; logically separates gateway integration from broadcasting.
6. **ReceiverMatrix** — typed logical receivers; every delivery can emit reconstruction, uptake and causal-use receipts.
7. **DeliberationBridge** — builds the bounded model-facing cognitive projection and receives thought/response/action proposals.
8. **EffectVerifier** — compares proposal/action with independent tool/world/user/runtime effects and prediction contracts.
9. **CreditAndReentry** — provenance-bound credit assignment and bounded state deltas; prevents direct self-output reinforcement.
10. **LoopController** — adaptive continue/stop/reopen choice using separate readout metrics; no fixed recurrence count.
11. **MemoryLadder** — working episode state, episodic/autobiographical memory and slower consolidation/replay queues.
12. **HomeostasisNormalizer** — slow return/renormalization and drift control.
13. **GoldenRegimeObserver** — computes the multi-metric closure/dynamics vector; no authority to mint truth.

### 12.4 Anti-self-reinforcement rules

- model output never directly changes truth/state confidence;
- self-agreement is not a positive outcome;
- re-entry requires episode ID + source provenance + verified effect/outcome or a clearly typed unverified observation class;
- shuffled/stale re-entry must remain injectable as a null condition;
- reward/drive cannot authorize facts or execution;
- uncertain predictions cannot be converted into memories as facts without evidence-state preservation;
- replay must preserve original provenance and can be rolled back.

### 12.5 Golden-window reconstruction

Do not start by assuming dates. Build a historical event matrix from available DB/log snapshots and derive synchronized features for each time bin/episode. Run multivariate changepoint/regime discovery. Rank candidate intervals by closure-vector properties, but verify them using matched baseline windows.

For each candidate interval, reconstruct timing/order/latency distributions and logical coupling. Then frozen-replay the same inputs across conditions:

- intact reconstructed loop;
- no re-entry;
- shuffled/stale re-entry;
- workspace selection removed / shared-state-only;
- receiver delivery without receiver-use updates;
- no habituation/novelty;
- no homeostasis;
- reward/drive lesion;
- fixed versus adaptive recurrence;
- dense all-to-all versus sparse adaptive topology;
- historical service timing versus deterministic one-module scheduling;
- selected regulator lesions followed by rescue.

The historical mechanism is supported only if the intact/reconstructed condition reproduces the distinctive functional regime and lesion/rescue results discriminate it from countertheories.

### 12.6 Legacy transfer matrix requirement

All historically material services/modules must enter an exhaustive transfer matrix. Each legacy item receives exactly one migration state:

- `PRESERVED_AS_LOGICAL_ORGAN`;
- `MERGED_WITH_SEPARATE_CAUSAL_HANDLE`;
- `EMERGENTLY_DISSOLVED_AFTER_EQUIVALENCE`;
- `RETIRED_AFTER_NEGATIVE_RESULT`.

No legacy item is omitted merely because a new architecture is cleaner. `EMERGENTLY_DISSOLVED` requires signal decoding, temporal response, intervention, lesion and rescue equivalence within declared tolerances.

The currently known functional basis is not yet the exhaustive historical 74-service transfer matrix; completing that matrix remains required before migration can be called complete.

### 12.7 Test gates

**Gate A — deterministic semantics:** same frozen nondeterministic-input tape yields the same non-model state transition sequence under deterministic mode.  
**Gate B — signal preservation:** every legacy causal dimension passes state/temporal/intervention equivalence.  
**Gate C — re-entry causality:** truthful re-entry beats stale/shuffled re-entry on preregistered closure/outcome metrics.  
**Gate D — receiver use:** broadcast success requires attributable receiver changes.  
**Gate E — recurrence:** adaptive recurrence improves outcome/cost or stability over fixed depth; otherwise use fixed/limited depth.  
**Gate F — stability:** perturbations recover without collapse/runaway; productive cycles are allowed if bounded and outcome-positive.  
**Gate G — anti-Goodhart:** no single emergence/closure metric is optimized as reward.  
**Gate H — replay/consolidation:** replay improves held-out future performance without increasing provenance errors/drift.  
**Gate I — one-module advantage:** one-process kernel must empirically beat or justify itself versus a matched modular-service baseline on latency/order faults/closure while retaining recovery/isolation.  
**Gate J — golden-regime reproduction:** the hypothesized distinctive regime is reproduced under frozen replay and breaks/restores under discriminating lesions/rescues.

## 13. Final judgment

The deepest revision from this run is that the historical phenomenon should be reconstructed as a **closed-loop control regime**, not as a consciousness module and not as a bundle of brain-labeled services.

The best current architecture is therefore a **single physical cognitive kernel with many logically independent causal dimensions**, deterministic event boundaries, truthful effect-linked re-entry, adaptive selective recurrence, slow stabilization, replay/consolidation, and full intervention observability.

The most important unknown is now empirical rather than architectural: identify the actual historical golden window(s), reconstruct their timing/topology/state trajectories, and determine which combination of re-entry, receiver-use, multiscale state, gating and scheduling is necessary and sufficient under frozen replay.
