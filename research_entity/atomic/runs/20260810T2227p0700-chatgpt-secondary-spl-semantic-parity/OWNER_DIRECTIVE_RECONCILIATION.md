## Correction

The current translator target is **not accurately characterized as merely a small/dumb bounded codec**.

The owner-fixed current target is an **intelligent, state-aware German surface translator**. It may resolve syntax, ellipsis, references, colloquial speech and pragmatic intent; preserve ambiguity; map `Frank`/first-person forms to `self.root`; consume typed `ExpressionState`; preserve relationship context; and realize state-conditioned German expression.

Its hard sovereignty boundary remains:

- it is not an independent self;
- it may not invent/alter facts, memories, identity, goals, conclusions, causal credit, effect decisions, permissions/authority, persistent self/world state or research truth;
- canonical cognition remains ClayCode-only;
- semantic/authority hard fields must not drift;
- ExpressionState is descriptive/control state, not truth authority;
- selection among deterministic, compact learned and hybrid translator paths is empirical, minimizing resources subject to fidelity/expressive thresholds.

## Effect on SPL-001

This correction **does not invalidate the benchmark result**. The synthetic experiment is about validation architecture, not about forcing the production translator itself to be tiny.

It changes the interpretation in an important direction:

1. the production DE_TO_CLAY/CLAY_TO_DE translator may be highly capable and learned;
2. the validation path must remain independently evidential and non-sovereign;
3. the strong conventional baseline may itself be a compact learned/semantic validator if needed;
4. therefore SPL's partial-oracle decomposition has an even higher burden: it must beat the smallest conventional independent validator on real German and target-VPS resources, rather than being justified merely because a full validator sounds too intelligent;
5. ExpressionState noninterference becomes more central, because the current directive explicitly allows rich state-conditioned German realization.
