# ATOMIC — IAC-001 Invariant Action Cone

## Final verdict

**IAC-001 is refuted as a distinct runtime organ/data structure.** The physical core reduces to an ordinary projection of every current live Hyperposition branch onto a complete `ActionSemanticKey` followed by `cardinality == 1`. The explicit conventional action-set baseline was predicate-equivalent on every reachable prefix in the local corpus. Fresh prior-art research also shows that rational metareasoning, value-directed belief approximation and action-preserving POMDP simplification already occupy the broader conceptual space.

A narrower Frank-specific result survives and is worth keeping as a **typed sufficient recurrence-stop law**:

> If and only if the current Hyperposition coverage is certified, every live branch is current under the same state/semantic/decision/effect-policy epochs, and all branches project to the same complete action-semantic key, branch-discriminating recurrence may halt and route that shared action without collapsing the unresolved epistemic alternatives.

The witness is not truth, not user intent, not effect authorization and not a replacement for the normal effect gate.

## Why this problem was selected

The owner-fixed Loop-of-Loops directive already requires adaptive recurrence and says to continue only while expected marginal utility of another recurrence exceeds compute/resource/instability cost and unresolved pressure remains material. But it also warns that low state delta with high residual error is stagnation, not convergence. The concrete stopping geometry remains open.

Meanwhile the live swarm is dense elsewhere: V17 support binding, execution lineage, UnifiedDB pair/WAL/semantic-root work, token TLS, translator hard-fields, WP04 symbol resolution and provider retry/reconciliation are occupied. Recent ATOMIC candidates CCW, CARF and CQC also cover terminal closure, causal credit and architectural quotienting. During this run DCA arrived for context completeness and TRSE opened for transverse/orbital recurrence stability. IAC therefore targets a distinct middle question: can another recurrence still change the **immediate action semantics**?

## Core separation

The candidate deliberately splits two quantities that are often conflated:

- epistemic uncertainty: how many world/hypothesis alternatives remain unresolved;
- action uncertainty: how many materially distinct immediate action semantics those alternatives induce.

A system can have high epistemic uncertainty and zero immediate action uncertainty. Requiring full state certainty in that regime is computational waste. Conversely, a high-probability majority action is not exact action invariance: a low-mass branch may require a different target, effect class, confirmation posture or action entirely.

## Hypothesis field and collision

Fourteen mechanisms from deletion, state geometry, dynamics, information, causal sensitivity, cross-domain control and null-baseline families were generated. The selected candidate was the exact action-projection cone because it is easy to falsify and directly couples Hyperposition to adaptive recurrence without changing truth or effect authority.

External research then reduced novelty sharply. Rational metareasoning frames computation itself as an action chosen by value and cost. Value-directed POMDP work evaluates belief approximation by decision/utility error instead of state error. Adaptive POMDP simplification can tighten bounds until it identifies the same action as the unsimplified planner. Current LLM research explicitly studies when to stop seeking or reasoning. Thus IAC is not a new general theory of stopping.

## ClayCode semantics

The candidate defines `ActionSemanticKey`, `BranchActionProjection`, `HyperpositionCoverageWitness` and `InvariantActionWitness`.

The action key must include every effect-policy-material dimension: action kind, target, exact scope, effect class, destructive class, confirmation requirement and intended payload identity. Two branches are not action-equivalent merely because their natural-language verb looks similar.

The coverage witness is essential. Branch omission cannot be interpreted as branch refutation. A witness is rejected when coverage is partial/unknown/stale, or when state, semantic, decision or effect-policy epochs differ from current.

The invariant witness is ephemeral/revalidatable evidence. `HALT_AND_ROUTE` only says that the represented current alternatives agree on the route. The routed action still passes through Frank's deterministic effect authority. `DENY` still denies; `REQUIRE_CONFIRMATION` still requires confirmation.

## Executable discriminator

A deterministic local reference model used 32 states, five binary information features and up to four actions. Twenty seeds were used for the randomized diffuse mapping. Five regimes were tested:

1. `COMPRESSED`: many epistemic states, action depends on one bit;
2. `MODERATE`: action depends on two bits;
3. `PARITY_SYNERGY`: action depends on five-bit parity, creating zero one-step decision gain until multi-step information is accumulated;
4. `RARE_BRANCH`: 30/32 states share one action while two states require another;
5. `DIFFUSE`: randomized branch-to-action maps.

Baselines were IAC, myopic one-step value of computation, 0.90 action confidence, full state certainty, fixed budget, a resource-matched randomized fixed budget and the strongest simple conventional action-set-cardinality implementation.

All results are synthetic reference-model evidence only.

## Main results

### Action-compressed uncertainty

In `COMPRESSED`, IAC reached 100% decision accuracy after one query. Full state certainty required five. Myopic VoC and action confidence also stopped after one and were exact in this easy regime. This demonstrates the decision/state separation but not IAC uniqueness.

In `MODERATE`, IAC, myopic VoC and action confidence all needed two queries and were exact; state certainty still needed five. Again the candidate is useful but not unique.

### Multi-query synergy

`PARITY_SYNERGY` is the strongest attack on myopic VoC. No single query improves immediate majority-action accuracy, so the one-step VoC controller stopped at zero queries and achieved 0.5 accuracy. IAC did not claim zero value while multiple actions remained in support; it used all five queries and achieved 1.0.

This does **not** prove IAC dominates nonmyopic rational metareasoning. It only falsifies one-step VoC as a universal sufficient stop rule.

### Rare material branch

In `RARE_BRANCH`, the majority action already had 30/32 = 0.9375 support. Therefore the 0.90 confidence controller stopped immediately and achieved 0.9375 accuracy. Myopic VoC under the chosen query cost did the same.

IAC preserved the two disagreeing branches, required 1.875 queries on average and achieved 1.0. A resource-matched randomized fixed budget with the same expected query count still achieved only 0.9375 because it could not allocate extra work specifically to ambiguous branch paths.

This is the most Frank-relevant local result: confidence can be high while action-semantic disagreement is still material.

### Diffuse mapping

Across twenty randomized diffuse action maps, IAC achieved 1.0 mean and minimum accuracy with 4.475 mean queries. Full state certainty used five. The resource-matched randomized fixed strategy at the same expected query count achieved about 0.854 expected accuracy. Myopic VoC averaged about 0.641 and fixed-3 about 0.614.

The result demonstrates adaptive allocation in this constructed world. It is not evidence about real Frank task accuracy.

## Safety lesions

### Stale decision mapping

A witness was created under decision epoch D1 where all represented branches agreed. The decision map then changed under D2. A naive persisted witness would still say invariant. Exact decision-epoch binding rejected it. Therefore an action-invariance witness cannot be a timeless receipt.

### Broken Hyperposition coverage

The two rare action branches were removed from the represented support. The remaining branches were perfectly action-homogeneous, so an unguarded invariance test would stop incorrectly. Marking coverage non-current prevented the witness. Therefore IAC safety depends on a real completeness/coverage contract; it cannot manufacture closed-world semantics from omission.

These controls link directly to the newly arrived DCA result: absence/incompleteness semantics matter. DCA is upstream context selection; IAC still needs its own branch-set coverage/currentness input.

## Micro-cost

A Python microbenchmark of the tiny exact all-same-action predicate was around a few hundred nanoseconds per check in this synthetic representation and happened to be substantially cheaper than the Counter-based confidence calculation. This number has almost no production significance; Python object layout and a real branch/action projection dominate. It only shows the final singleton check itself is not intrinsically expensive.

## Strongest counterhypothesis wins

The decisive architecture result is not a benchmark number. The candidate predicate and a plain conventional `Set<ActionSemanticKey>` cardinality predicate were equivalent on every reachable prefix across the tested corpus.

Therefore no `Invariant Action Cone` runtime organ is justified. The correct architectural compression is:

- existing Hyperposition supplies current alternatives;
- existing/current decision logic projects each alternative to a complete action semantic key;
- existing loop controller may consume `projected_action_cardinality == 1` as one sufficient halt reason;
- a typed witness is useful only if it materially improves non-bypassability/currentness/coverage checking;
- otherwise ordinary metadata is preferable.

## Prior-art verdict

No world-first claim survives. The closest conceptual families include rational metareasoning/value of computation, value-directed belief approximation/sampling, policy/action-preserving POMDP simplification and modern LLM early-stopping/termination work.

Classification:

`KNOWN_NEAR_ANALOGUE / PROJECT_SPECIFIC_TYPED_COMBINATION`

The Frank-specific combination is the exact binding of action equivalence to typed Hyperposition coverage and deterministic effect-policy semantics while preserving unresolved branches and granting zero authority.

## Relationship to DCA and TRSE

DCA asks what context/evidence must be assembled so material decision distinctions are not erased. IAC consumes an already represented branch set and asks whether any material action distinction remains. DCA can therefore reduce the risk that IAC receives an incomplete decision basis, but DCA does not prove IAC coverage automatically.

TRSE asks whether a recurrent orbit/limit-cycle is transversely stable and useful under perturbation. IAC can halt action-discriminating recurrence even in a stable recurrent regime if every current branch already agrees on the action. Conversely, action disagreement can require continued/reseeded cognition even when dynamics are stable. Neither subsumes the other.

## Durable result

The surviving architecture law is:

**Epistemic uncertainty is not by itself a reason to keep thinking. Action disagreement is.**

More precisely:

`certified current branch coverage + complete current branch->action projection + singleton action-semantic support -> branch-discriminating recurrence may halt`

while:

`unknown/partial coverage OR stale decision/effect epoch OR >1 action semantic key -> no IAC halt witness`.

This is only a sufficient stop condition. Nonmyopic value-of-computation, deadlines, resource limits, exploration, learning goals or future information-gathering value can still govern other recurrence decisions.
