## A. Global workspace / broadcast

### Mashour et al. / Changeux-Dehaene review
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8770991/
Use: GNW describes nonlinear recurrent ignition that makes a representation globally accessible to local processors.
Supports: engineering abstraction of bounded global access/broadcast.
Does not support: Clay consciousness, exact GWT implementation equivalence, or Hyperposition synergy.

### Fahrenfort et al. / broadcasting-network clarification
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11117084/
Use: broadcasting is proposed as a main GNW function; graph properties alone can be consistent with other communication regimes.
Supports: keep `broadcast/access` operationally defined rather than naming any integration mechanism `GWT`.

### Cogitate Consortium 2025 adversarial collaboration
URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1
Use: large preregistered adversarial test substantially challenged key predictions of both GNWT and IIT.
Supports counterhypothesis: biological GNWT is not settled; computational workspace research must be falsifiable and must not inherit consciousness claims.

## B. Multimodal probabilistic representations

### Ma et al. 2006 probabilistic population codes
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17115060/
Use: population activity can represent probability distributions and support Bayesian inference.
Supports: representing uncertainty as a distribution is computationally legitimate; does not prescribe Clay encoding.

### Bishop 1994 Mixture Density Networks
URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/bishop-ncrg-94-004.pdf
Use: in multi-valued problems, the conditional average of correct targets can itself be incorrect; model full conditional distribution.
Supports: preserve modes instead of blindly averaging candidate states.

## C. Attention / associative metastability

### Ramsauer et al. 2020 Modern Hopfield Networks
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02217
Use: continuous Hopfield network has global-mean, metastable subset-average, and single-pattern fixed points; update rule equivalent to transformer attention.
Supports: attention dynamics can move between distributed/subset/single-mode regimes.
Does not support: attention weights as posterior belief or truth.

## D. Set structure / permutation invariance

### Zaheer et al. 2017 Deep Sets
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06114
Use: set functions should be permutation invariant; characterization motivates symmetric aggregation.
Supports: reordering Hyperposition alternatives must not change semantics.

### Lee et al. 2019 Set Transformer
URL: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/lee19d/lee19d.pdf
Use: attention-based permutation-invariant set modeling; inducing-point variant reduces self-attention complexity from quadratic to linear in set size.
Supports: interaction-aware Hyperposition processing need not require sequence semantics or unbounded O(K^2) cost.

## E. Information seeking / active discrimination

### Friston et al. active inference and learning
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5167251/
Use: epistemic value is expected information gain; extrinsic value is related to expected utility/preferred outcomes.
Supports: discriminator choice can combine information value and task value.
Does not make active inference mandatory; the decomposition is used as a mathematical anchor.

### Bayesian optimal experimental design
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.00053
Use: expected information gain is a principled design criterion when observations are costly.
Supports: R4 discriminator can be chosen by expected reduction of uncertainty, subject to cost.

## F. Resource limits / compression

URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10353860/

Supports: token/memory budget belongs inside the cognition objective, not as an afterthought.

### Poupart & Boutilier — Value-directed Compression of POMDPs
URL: https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~cebly/Papers/vdib.pdf
Use: compress belief/state representation in ways that minimize loss of decision quality rather than generic reconstruction error.
Supports: preserve low-probability high-decision-impact hypotheses; reject naive posterior top-k as universal compression law.

### Poupart & Boutilier — Value-directed Belief State Approximation
URL: https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~cebly/Papers/vda-abs.html

## J. Research synthesis

Independent literatures converge on a narrower engineering claim:

1. multiple possible states can legitimately be represented probabilistically;
2. averaging modes can destroy valid solutions;
3. a workspace/broadcast mechanism can make selected information broadly accessible;
4. belief and attention/access are different quantities;
5. useful cognition can actively acquire information when information value exceeds cost;
6. bounded cognition should compress according to downstream value/distortion, not raw posterior alone;
7. unordered alternative sets require permutation-invariant semantics;
8. explicit alternatives may still be redundant or harmful if the base network already carries a sufficient latent belief state;
9. biological GNWT remains contested and cannot supply a consciousness shortcut.

Therefore the current research target is not `GWT + Hyperposition = consciousness`.

It is the falsifiable systems hypothesis:

`explicit multimodal evidence-bound belief + bounded global access + value-directed discrimination/compression`

may yield a positive resource-normalized interaction on tasks requiring unresolved alternatives.

Primary promotion statistic remains matched-budget task interaction:

`Delta_syn = J_GH - J_G - J_H + J_0`.

External sources motivate mechanisms; only exact Clay experiments can establish Clay-specific benefit.
