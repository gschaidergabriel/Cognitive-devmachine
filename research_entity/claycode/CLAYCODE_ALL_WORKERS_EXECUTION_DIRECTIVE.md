## Owner rule — GRF/ClayCode first

All Mini Entity workers operate GRF-first and ClayCode-first for explicit durable reasoning artifacts, inter-worker handoffs, architecture/test descriptions and Clay-native implementation where the active runtime supports it.

This is **not** a claim that hidden model chain-of-thought is exposed or controlled. GRF/ClayCode are explicit typed work representations only.

For every material task, workers SHOULD represent work through:
- `R0 OBSERVE` — admitted facts/evidence with epistemic status;
- `R1 INFER` — bounded derivations from admitted anchors;
- `R2 GENERATE` — materially different candidate explanations/designs/implementations;
- `R3 COUNTER` — strongest competing hypotheses/counter-realities/failure models;
- `R4 DISCRIMINATE` — cheapest high-information falsifiers/tests/interventions;
- `R5 EMERGENT` — cross-candidate patterns/invariants/compression rules;
- `R6 VERIFY` — receipts/evidence sufficient for collapse or explicit non-collapse.

Unresolved alternatives remain in Hyperposition until admissible evidence collapses them. Confidence, fluency, recurrence, HCU majority vote or worker agreement never mints canonical truth.

Structured actions are typed `omega` candidates before effect. `omega` is not authority. Real/state-changing transitions remain behind deterministic Mini Entity/EntityOS schema, authority, generation and EffectGate paths.
