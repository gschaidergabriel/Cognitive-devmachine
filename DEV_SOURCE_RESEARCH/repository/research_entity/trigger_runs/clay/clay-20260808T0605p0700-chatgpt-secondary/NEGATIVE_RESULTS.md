# Clay G8 Negative Results / Non-Promotions

All numeric results below are synthetic/analytic proxies unless an external-source boundary is stated. They do not promote live FRANK claims.

1. **N-G8-001 — Spectral radius alone is not a sufficient operational stability certificate.** In `EXP-G8-NONNORMAL-001`, every 2x2 system had `rho=0.88`, while maximum finite-horizon gain ranged from 1.0 to 26.16 as non-normal coupling increased. This refutes the universal use of `rho(M_delta)` alone, not its value as one asymptotic diagnostic.
2. **N-G8-002 — A hierarchical/threshold governor without switching control can chatter severely.** In `EXP-G8-GOV-STABILITY-002`, raw threshold switching occurred on ~69.4% of steps; hysteresis reduced this to ~5.76% at similar loss, and the slow+fast design reduced loss further in the constructed workload. The numeric ranking is not transferable to FRANK.
3. **N-G8-003 — Selective closure without dependency/invariant coverage evidence is unsafe.** In `EXP-G8-COVERAGE-003`, narrow observed scopes produced nonzero unsafe rates at all tested coverage levels. Coverage-aware fail-closed escalation removed unsafe cases by construction but paid more coordination.
4. **N-G8-004 — Immediate dirty refresh is not universally resource-optimal.** `EXP-G8-VOI-FRESHNESS-004` gave zero constructed query error for dirty-immediate but at send rate ~0.0726; a coalesced query/EVI proxy held query error ~0.00248 at send rate ~0.0312. Periodic refresh had much larger query error. This does not establish a real FRANK threshold.
5. **N-G8-005 — Scoped TCC rebase without coverage protection can admit stale conflicts.** `EXP-G8-TCC-REBASE-005` raised acceptance from ~35.0% under global-root rejection to ~97.8% under scoped validation, but admitted ~0.0674% unsafe cases in the hidden-dependency proxy. Coverage escalation reduced the designed unsafe rate to ~0.0009% while rejecting more proposals. A real sound coverage checker remains open.
6. **N-G8-006 — Repeated approximate semantic summaries are not automatically stable memory.** `EXP-G8-DRIFT-006` accumulated mean error ~1.56 without rebase; exact/error-triggered rebase bounded the constructed error far more tightly. This supports exact backing as a candidate, not the proxy threshold.
7. **N-G8-007 — Recurrence is not emergence.** `EXP-G8-EMERGENCE-007` constructed a useful assembly and a self-sustaining oscillator with the same recurrence (~0.222). The useful assembly showed lesion delta MSE ~0.142; the oscillator was ~0.000023. Recurrence/amplification must remain candidate signals only.
8. **N-G8-008 — Neither always-incremental nor always-rebuild is universally optimal.** In `EXP-G8-REBUILD-008`, average modeled costs were ~28.89 always-incremental, ~23.82 always-rebuild and ~21.31 adaptive including selector overhead; selector error remained ~8.1%. The target crossover must be measured live.
9. **N-G8-009 — Fixed sparse broadcast is not safety-equivalent to full broadcast under router drift.** `EXP-G8-BROADCAST-009` reduced mean fanout to ~6.74 but missed ~1.84% of causal audiences. Confidence fallback reduced misses to ~0.378% but increased fanout to ~15.26. Full broadcast remained the zero-miss baseline in the proxy.
10. **N-G8-010 — Cross-branch agreement is not independent replication.** Clay G7, FRANK Cycle 3, GWT R2, CC7 and H2 share substantial ancestry, literature and synthetic mechanisms. Mesh convergence raises coherence, not evidence independence.
11. **N-G8-011 — Learned invariant/dependency proposals are not authority.** Current formal-verification work supports LLM-assisted synthesis only when soundness is independently enforced by verifiers/contracts/abstract interpretation. G8 therefore rejects `LLM says invariant -> fast path` as an architecture rule.
12. **N-G8-012 — Multi-resolution memory does not justify destructive summary-only truth.** SeKV-style hierarchy is implementation evidence for resolution adaptation; FRANK/Clay still requires exact replay/provenance backing for canonical state.
13. **N-G8-013 — Semantic Scholar provider success cannot be claimed in this run.** The repository bridge returned HTTP 429. Scholarly discovery continued through successful OpenAlex/arXiv plus direct primary/native web fallback.
14. **N-G8-014 — No Qwen3-4B reader result.** Hugging Face inference remains cached quota-exhausted until 2026-09-01 according to `TOOL_QUOTA_STATE.json`; no model accuracy/token claim is generated here.
15. **N-G8-015 — No consciousness promotion.** Functional GWT, recurrence, non-normal transitions, self-description, workspace structure or emergent-language behavior do not establish phenomenal consciousness.
