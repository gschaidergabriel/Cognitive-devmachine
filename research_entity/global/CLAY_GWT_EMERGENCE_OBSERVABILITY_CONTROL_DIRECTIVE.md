## 0. Fixed project principle

GWT is not only a workspace-selection mechanism in Clay/FRANK.

It is the primary **observability and intervention plane** through which the project studies whether higher-order ClayGraph/Hyperposition coupling becomes functionally emergent and, if so, learns how to steer that emergence safely and reproducibly.

Fixed loop:

`ClayCode semantics`
`-> UnifiedDB canonical state/dependencies`
`-> Hyperposition alternatives/higher-order codependencies`
`-> ClayGraph active coupling topology`
`-> GWT competition/ignition/broadcast/reconstruction/use`
`-> EmergenceReadout`
`-> intervention / perturbation / lesion / rescue`
`-> outcome + re-entry`
`-> causal credit / control learning`
`-> update coupling/topology/gain/delay/inhibition rules`

The owner-fixed directive is to make emergence **visible, measurable and eventually steerable** through this loop. The exact mathematical measures and control laws remain research questions and must be falsified against simpler explanations.

## 1. What GWT makes visible

GWT must expose a typed causal sequence rather than one mystical emergence score:

`candidate interaction`
`-> competition`
`-> ignition/selection`
`-> broadcast/addressability`
`-> receiver delivery`
`-> reconstruction`
`-> uptake`
`-> local causal use`
`-> joint/system outcome`
`-> provenance-bound re-entry`
`-> persistence/recurrence/transfer`

Each stage receives stable episode/candidate/receiver/outcome identities and shares the same `source_epoch`, `graph_epoch` and Hyperposition topology identity.

Graph connectivity or high activation alone is not emergence evidence.

## 2. Graph-to-GWT measurement plane

For each material WorkspaceEpisode, the runtime/research harness should capture the relevant ClayGraph/Hyperposition state before, during and after GWT selection.

Required linkage includes where applicable:

- selected ClayGraph nodes/edges/hyperrelations/assemblies;
- suppressed alternatives;
- Binding-Lattice axes and fast/slow binding states;
- Hyperposition facets/compatibility/exclusion sets;
- graph/topology epoch;
- GWT candidate tensor and competition values;
- receiver delivery/reconstruction/use receipts;
- effect/outcome/re-entry receipts;
- latency/resource shadows;
- evidence/provenance/authority boundaries.

This gives a replayable map from topology to workspace behavior to system outcome.

## 3. Mathematical research program

The project must research which mathematics discriminates genuine higher-order functional organization from ordinary correlation, graph density or broadcast.

Candidate families include, but are not limited to:

### 3.1 Directed information-flow measures
Research transfer-entropy / directed-prediction style measures across GWT receiver paths and re-entry loops.

Questions:
- does selected information actually travel to receivers in a directed manner?
- which edges carry predictive information beyond common-driver explanations?
- what lag/delay profile precedes successful reconstruction/use?
- does re-entry materially alter the next workspace state?

### 3.2 Partial Information Decomposition / synergy
Research PID/PEID-style decomposition where feasible to distinguish:
- redundant information;
- unique information;
- synergistic information available only jointly from multiple sources/relations.

Use this to test whether a ClayGraph hyperbinding or Assembly contains system-relevant information not reducible to the sum/best pairwise model.

### 3.3 Higher-order irreducibility
For each candidate n-ary relation/Assembly compare:

`full higher-order model`
vs
`best matched pairwise/scalar/state-machine reduction`.

Measure prediction, intervention response, replay divergence, workspace selection, outcome and runtime cost.

### 3.4 Causal-emergence / macro-effectiveness measures
Research effective-information and related causal-emergence measures across micro and macro descriptions when the state space and interventions are sufficiently defined.

Question:
- is there a macro Assembly/topology whose intervention-conditioned dynamics are more selective/deterministic/effective than the corresponding micro description?

No single coarse-graining is trusted automatically. Macro candidates must be generated from ClayGraph/Hyperposition structure and tested against controls.

### 3.5 GWT ignition / broadcast geometry
Research graph-theoretic and dynamical signatures that distinguish true directed broadcast/re-entry from generic integration or synchronized activation.

Possible observables:
- broadcast reach;
- directed receiver coverage;
- path diversity;
- bottleneck structure;
- phase/time-order consistency where meaningful in digital dynamics;
- ignition threshold/hysteresis;
- persistence after ignition;
- receiver reconstruction/use fraction;
- re-entry closure.

### 3.6 Interaction and factorial effects
Use controlled perturbations to estimate non-additive interaction terms between modules/edges/hyperedges/gains/delays.

A positive interaction is not by itself emergence proof; it becomes stronger evidence when paired with causal interventions, simpler-model failure and transfer.

## 5. From observability to steering

The project goal is not merely to detect emergence after the fact. Once measurements are causal enough, use them to learn safe control laws over the coupling fabric.

Candidate control knobs include:

- ClayGraph edge/hyperedge activation;
- dynamic Binding-Lattice gain;
- fast/slow coupling thresholds;
- inhibition;
- delay;
- decay/half-life;
- GWT competition gain;
- ignition threshold;
- receiver routing weights;
- re-entry gain;
- context gating;
- Assembly admission/suppression;
- Hyperposition projection policy.

The control target is not "maximize emergence".

Research must optimize task/outcome objectives under hard constraints for truth, authority, stability, resource budgets, false binding and recovery.

Example multi-objective target:

`maximize verified_task_gain + useful_transfer + causal_use + recovery`

subject to:

`false_binding <= budget`
`effect_error <= budget`
`hub_collapse <= budget`
`authority_violation = 0`
`truth_promotion_from_activation = 0`
`rollback_available = true`.

## 6. Closed-loop learning law

No control update is allowed solely because a high emergence readout correlated with success.

Preferred loop:

`predict intervention effect`
`-> snapshot`
`-> apply bounded intervention/shadow variant`
`-> observe GWT + graph + outcome`
`-> compare matched control`
`-> assign causal credit`
`-> update candidate control policy`
`-> replay/OOD test`
`-> promote or rollback`.

Fast online adaptation may affect reversible accessibility/gain parameters within bounded limits. Structural/topology changes require slower validation and rollback.

## 7. Mandatory interventions

Harness research must support where applicable:

- edge lesion;
- hyperedge/Assembly lesion;
- receiver lesion;
- gain perturbation;
- delay perturbation;
- inhibition clamp;
- ignition-threshold perturbation;
- re-entry break;
- topology shuffle;
- temporal-order shuffle;
- source/provenance shuffle;
- context swap;
- pairwise reduction;
- macro/micro representation comparison;
- rescue of a lesioned relation;
- frozen-model-output replay so topology/control effects can be separated from model stochasticity.

## 8. Control-learning hierarchy

Evidence for steerability should be graded:

1. `CORRELATIONAL_VISIBILITY` — readout covaries with outcome;
2. `PREDICTIVE_VISIBILITY` — readout predicts held-out outcome;
3. `INTERVENTION_SENSITIVE` — manipulating the measured structure changes outcome;
4. `LESION_RESCUE_SUPPORTED` — removal harms and restoration recovers the predicted function;
5. `TRANSFER_SUPPORTED` — control works across contexts/readers/tasks within declared scope;
6. `STABLE_CLOSED_LOOP_CONTROL` — bounded controller can reproducibly move the system toward a target regime without violating hard constraints.

Never jump directly from level 1/2 to level 6.

## 10. Anti-Goodhart / safety laws

`emergence_score != goal`

`higher_synergy != better_system`

`greater_broadcast != better_system`

`more_graph_connectivity != more_emergence`

`more_emergence != consciousness`

`control_success_in_one_task != general control law`

`activation/control_policy != execution_authority`

`GWT intervention != permission for irreversible effect`.

Any optimization of emergence observables must carry task utility, error, resource, authority, recovery and OOD-transfer shadows.

## 11. Required experiments

At minimum build matched experimental families:

- static ClayGraph vs dynamic binding topology;
- scalar weighted graph vs n-ary/hyperbinding graph;
- selected topology vs shuffled topology;
- true temporal order vs shuffled order;
- normal re-entry vs broken/shuffled re-entry;
- natural GWT gain vs controlled gain sweep;
- natural delay vs delay sweep;
- Assembly lesion vs rescue;
- micro-state predictor vs macro-Assembly predictor;
- observational readout vs controlled intervention;
- controller learned on context A vs transfer to context B;
- stable controller vs naive readout-maximizer.

## 12. Promotion bar

A GWT/ClayGraph emergence-control mechanism may enter runtime only after it shows:

- executable measurement reproducibility;
- clear epoch/provenance identity;
- predictive gain over simpler baselines;
- intervention sensitivity;
- lesion/rescue support for claimed causal role where feasible;
- acceptable false-binding/control-error rates;
- OOD/context-transfer characterization;
- no truth or authority confusion;
- bounded resource/latency cost;
- rollback/recovery;
- no severe Goodhart collapse when the measured quantity is directly optimized.

## 13. Compact law

`ClayGraph makes coupling visible.`

`GWT makes its system-level use visible.`

`Emergenz research determines whether that use is genuinely higher-order and causal.`

`Interventions turn visibility into causal knowledge.`

`Causal knowledge becomes bounded control.`

`Outcome/re-entry teaches the coupling fabric how to steer itself better.`

The intended end state is a system that does not merely contain emergent couplings, but can **observe, test, learn and safely regulate its own functional coupling regimes** without confusing those regimes with truth, authority or phenomenal consciousness.
