Qwen is the candidate deep multimodal perceptual core. A second heavy semantic Retina encoder is not removed by decree; it must lose a matched differential.

## New important research split

The strongest new refinement is to distinguish three questions that were previously too easy to collapse:

1. **Physical reuse:** can Qwen/mmproj media features be intercepted once and reused without duplicate encoding?
2. **Representation geometry:** are raw Qwen features good enough for user-specific familiarity/open-set behavior, or is a tiny projection/dedicated light embedding representation needed?
3. **End-to-end cognition:** even if low-level vision features are stable, does the abliterated/text-semantic decoder preserve correct visual reasoning, GUI action selection and outcome interpretation?

These must be measured separately.
