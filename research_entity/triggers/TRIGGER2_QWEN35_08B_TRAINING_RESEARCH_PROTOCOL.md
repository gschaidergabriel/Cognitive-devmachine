## Scientific rules

- Raw private chain-of-thought is not a training target.
- Train observable decisions, typed state transitions, compact structured projections and checkable outcomes.
- Text SFT may teach a token/packet control ABI; it cannot by itself prove residual-stream/J-Space control.
- Hyperposition means preserving unresolved alternatives and their evidence/topology, not quantum claims.
- Majority vote, recurrence or model self-report does not mint truth.
- Shared cognitive state is not canonical truth.
- Dynamic coordinator is a temporary role, not a persistent authority or personality.
- Split by causal episode/trace family, never only random rows.
- Hard negatives are mandatory and overrepresented in validation/holdout.
- One completed training job is not PASS.
- Single-node PASS is not physical GRID10 HCU PASS.
- No second paid run without new owner authorization.
