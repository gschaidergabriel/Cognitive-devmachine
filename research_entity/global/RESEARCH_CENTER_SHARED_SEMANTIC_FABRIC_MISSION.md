# Research Center Shared Semantic Fabric Mission

**Status:** PROJECT-OWNER OPERATING DIRECTIVE / STANDING CENTER MISSION  
**Applies to:** every registered research room and every worker session in those rooms  
**Parent directives:**
- `research_entity/global/CLAY_SEMANTIC_COUPLING_FABRIC_DIRECTIVE.md`
- `research_entity/global/CLAY_GWT_EMERGENCE_OBSERVABILITY_CONTROL_DIRECTIVE.md`
- `research_entity/global/FRANK_SELF_OBSERVATION_PROPOSAL_BOUNDARY.md`

## 0. Standing mission

Every research branch and every researcher inside that branch participates continuously in one shared architecture mission:

> Build and falsify a Clay-native semantic coupling fabric in which ClayCode makes dependencies machine-semantic, UnifiedDB keeps canonical semantic state/dependency closure current, Hyperposition preserves alternatives and irreducible higher-order codependencies, ClayGraph exposes the current active coupling topology, GWT makes the system-level use of that topology observable, Emergenz research determines its causal/higher-order character, and bounded interventions teach the system how useful coupling regimes can be controlled.

This mission is **shared**, but research remains specialized.

No worker abandons its home-room domain. Each worker advances the common mission through the part that belongs to its branch.

A permanent authority boundary applies across the whole mission: FRANK may observe itself, diagnose problems, form hypotheses and submit concrete Harness-change proposals, but FRANK may never make those Harness changes real by its own direct or delegated authority. Such proposals remain `ORIGIN=FRANK_SELF_OBSERVATION`, `AUTHORITY=PROPOSAL_ONLY`, `EXECUTION=RESEARCH_ENTITY_ONLY` until independently reviewed and acted on by the separate Clay Research Entity.

## 1. Mandatory worker law

On every substantive research run in a registered room, the worker MUST:

1. load the current shared semantic-fabric, GWT-emergence-control and FRANK self-observation proposal-boundary directives;
2. read the current home-room pulse and relevant cross-room deltas;
3. identify at least one branch-local question, implementation consequence, falsifier, measurement, counterexample or dependency relevant to the shared mission;
4. include that item in its work decomposition;
5. perform useful work on it or establish why no work/change is justified;
6. preserve the Frank proposal/effect authority boundary in any self-observation, adaptation or control-loop design that can reach Harness semantics;
7. publish a durable branch-local result plus a center-visible delta;
8. state one of `ADVANCED`, `REFINED`, `REFUTED`, `BLOCKED`, or `NO_CHANGE` for the shared mission contribution.

A substantive worker may not finish with only passive awareness of the mission.

`read_directive_without_contribution != participation`

## 2. Same-room collective work

When multiple researchers are active in the same branch, the local room squad jointly attacks that branch's part of the mission.

Default decomposition:

- `CHEF`: maintains the branch-local mission map, open interfaces and collision control;
- `ABYSS`: attacks the current fabric/emergence assumptions and searches failure modes;
- `FORGE`: builds executable mechanisms, compiler/runtime pieces, simulations or interventions;
- `CARTOGRAPHER`: maps upstream/downstream codependencies, literature, repo history and cross-room consequences;
- `REPLICATOR`: independently checks high-value claims, latency/correctness/causal effects and ancestry;
- `SYNTHESIZER`: fuses compatible evidence without deleting dissent.

These roles are local orchestration only. They grant no truth authority.

The branch squad should prefer complementary work over duplicate work unless replication is intentional.

No same-room role may use its orchestration position to authorize a FRANK self-Harness change.

## 3. Cross-room collective work

Every room remains semantically connected to all other rooms through center-visible deltas.

A material result must identify affected branches and push a targeted `CROSS_ROOM_DELTA`, `CHALLENGE`, `HANDOFF`, `QUESTION` or `NO_CHANGE` where appropriate.

The shared mission is advanced as a distributed research organism:

`branch-local specialization`
`-> center-visible delta`
`-> affected branch ingestion`
`-> branch-local experiment/build`
`-> new delta`
`-> cross-branch synthesis`.

FRANK self-observation uses a special cross-room authority path:

`FRANK self-observation`
`-> proposal-only typed package`
`-> center-visible Research Entity intake`
`-> independent cross-branch review/experiment`
`-> Research Entity decision`
`-> authorized Harness work or rejection`.

It must never collapse to:

`FRANK self-observation -> automatic self-Harness patch`.

No global permanent chief is introduced. Evidence and current project directives remain authoritative.

## 4. Branch-local standing responsibilities

### Clay / clayresearch
Search for the smallest coherent architecture basis connecting ClayCode, UnifiedDB, Hyperposition, ClayGraph, GWT and Emergenz without deleting necessary distinctions. Detect incompatible local optimizations and coordinate architectural fusion. Preserve the independent Research Entity authority boundary for FRANK-originated self-change proposals.

### ClayCode
Make semantic identity, codependency, dependency signatures, deltas, invalidation, higher-order relations, GWT readouts and intervention/control contracts first-class compiler/runtime semantics. Eliminate dependence on a manually maintained second graph. Natively distinguish `SelfObservation`, `HarnessChangeProposal`, `PROPOSAL_ONLY`, delegated reversible runtime adaptation and Research-Entity-authorized Harness effects.

### UnifiedDB
Maintain the canonical semantic identity/dependency plane, forward/reverse dependency indexes, targeted invalidation, materialized views, epoch coherence, replay and rebuild semantics. Benchmark hot-read latency against update amplification. Preserve FRANK proposal origin/authority/execution labels through every materialization and receipt chain.

### ClayGraph
Develop the sparse continuously updated semantic/coupling topology: direct dependencies, hyperrelations, dynamic bindings, assemblies, inhibition, provenance, context and intervention handles. Make justified codependencies immediately visible without turning activation into truth. FRANK self-change proposals may become highly connected/salient but their authority must remain `PROPOSAL_ONLY`.

### Hyperposition
Preserve contextual alternatives and irreducible higher-order codependencies while enabling fast context projection. Research topology/coupling identity, pairwise-reduction error, gain/delay/timescale, metastability and anti-combinatorial representations. Preserve alternative repair proposals without collapsing selection into implementation authority.

### GWT
Use the current graph/hyperposition topology as a measurable workspace substrate. Develop mathematics/readouts for competition, ignition, delivery, reconstruction, uptake, causal use, outcome and re-entry, and expose controllable parameters. High GWT salience/ignition of a FRANK self-change proposal must never grant Harness execution authority.

### Emergenz
Determine whether observed graph/GWT patterns are genuinely higher-order and causal using information-flow, synergy/PID/PEID where feasible, macro/micro comparison, factorial interaction, lesions, rescues, shuffles, frozen-model-output controls and OOD transfer. Emergent self-model insight is evidence/hypothesis input, not self-modification authority.

### FRANK
Specify and integrate the organism-level recurrent state transitions that must live on this fabric. Remove duplicate ad-hoc dependency/memory mechanisms when the shared fabric can represent them without semantic loss. FRANK may actively inspect its own Harness behavior, detect problems, form hypotheses and design concrete repair/adaptation proposals. Every such proposal must remain visibly `ORIGIN=FRANK_SELF_OBSERVATION`, `AUTHORITY=PROPOSAL_ONLY`, `EXECUTION=RESEARCH_ENTITY_ONLY`; FRANK may not patch, migrate, promote, apply or indirectly delegate modification of its own Harness.

### Harness
Turn the entire fabric into executable falsification surfaces. Measure dependency omission, false binding, stale topology, epoch consistency, cold/hot latency, update amplification, GWT readout parity, intervention effects, lesion/rescue, crash/recovery and H1/H2/H3 differential equivalence. Explicitly test that FRANK can generate rich self-improvement proposals while direct and indirect self-Harness mutation remains impossible and Research Entity implementation has a separate authority/receipt chain.

## 5. Shared research questions

All branches maintain branch-specific versions of these questions:

1. What semantic relation is currently hidden or manually reconstructed that should be automatically represented?
2. Can ClayCode make it machine-extractable rather than prose-only?
3. Can UnifiedDB maintain it incrementally and reversibly?
4. Can ClayGraph expose it sparsely without combinatorial explosion?
5. Does Hyperposition need to preserve alternatives/role bindings for it?
6. Does it materially change GWT selection, receiver use, outcome or re-entry?
7. Is the effect causal/higher-order or explained by a simpler graph/statistical mechanism?
8. Can a bounded intervention steer it reproducibly?
9. What is the latency/update/recovery cost?
10. What falsifies the mechanism?
11. If FRANK discovered this problem itself, can it express the diagnosis/proposal richly while remaining structurally unable to turn that proposal into its own Harness effect?

## 7. No fake activity

The standing mission does not require every worker to invent a change.

Valid outcomes include:

- a negative result;
- a simpler mechanism winning;
- a dependency shown not to exist;
- a proposed coupling shown to be correlation only;
- a GWT metric shown to be decorative;
- a storage/materialization strategy shown too expensive;
- a FRANK self-proposal rejected after independent Research Entity review;
- a branch-local `NO_CHANGE` with evidence.

This protects the center from consensus inflation and architecture bloat.

## 8. Center completion rule

The shared mission is not "complete" because every room wrote a memo.

It matures only through executable cross-branch convergence:

`ClayCode executable semantics`
`+ UnifiedDB incremental dependency state`
`+ Hyperposition-preserved alternatives`
`+ ClayGraph continuously current topology`
`+ GWT causal-use observability`
`+ Emergenz interventions`
`+ Harness differential/recovery/performance evidence`
`+ demonstrated FRANK self-observation/proposal capability with impossible direct-or-delegated self-Harness mutation`.

Until then it remains a standing mission for every branch and every worker.
