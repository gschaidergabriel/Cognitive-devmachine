## Scope and hard boundary

This run studies functional/causal emergence only inside the project's GWT-compatible architecture. A hypothesis is admissible only if it preserves the functional distinction between local/specialist processing and capacity-limited workspace selection, broadcast/global availability, receiver-side reconstruction/uptake/causal use, outcome and provenance-bound re-entry. It may vary the physical implementation, topology, timescale or number of nested workspace layers. It may not replace GWT with generic integration, synchrony, graph density, recurrence, synergy or one scalar. No result is evidence of phenomenal consciousness.

## External research wave 2 — hypothesis-targeted attacks

Wave 2 specifically tried to falsify: (A) broadcast/use/re-entry as the causal chain, (B) transient workspace macro-states, (C) PEID/hyperedge causality, (D) metastability/hysteresis, (E) selective rather than maximal broadcast, (F) multiscale loop-of-loops emergence, and (G) nested intramodel/system workspaces.

Main outcomes:
- The 2025 Nature adversarial collaboration does not justify abandoning GWT, but it strongly warns against hard-coding a literal PFC/offset-ignition biological mapping as the software theory.
- The GNWT response shows that theory-prediction centrality itself must be preregistered; otherwise failed secondary predictions can be post-hoc reclassified.
- Anthropic 2026 provides unusually direct AI evidence for a selective, load-bearing workspace-like representational format, including targeted intervention and broadcast-head ablation, while explicitly delimiting where the architecture differs from full recurrent GWT.
- PEID is promising for interventionist synergy but remains a new preprint; it should be run beside alternative synergy estimators and explicit functional interventions, never alone.
- The multivariate PID inconsistency result is a strong reason to maintain an estimator-ensemble/robustness layer instead of canonizing one decomposition.
- Competitive-interaction results attack the naive hypothesis that greater global connectivity or synchronization should monotonically improve emergence. Selective competition may be causally necessary precisely because it preserves diverse specialist information for later integration.
- Causal Emergence 2.0 attacks the one-best-macro-level assumption. FRANK may have genuine causal contribution at multiple nested scales (model subspace, workspace episode, Assembly, loop family, whole-organism episode).

## Synthetic mechanism sanity tests (NOT live evidence)

These simulations only test separability of candidate mechanisms.

1. **Macro causal information toy.** Four microstates were grouped 3+1 into two macro-states with deterministic macro alternation but micro-level degeneracy. Effective information under uniform micro interventions was 0.811278 bits; the two-state macro intervention yielded 1.000000 bit. This reproduces the principle that a causal macro-description can outperform its micro-description under a changed intervention basis.
2. **Selective bottleneck toy.** 12 binary specialists: 3 relevant (75% accurate), 9 distractors (chance), noisy salience correlated with relevance. Mean accuracy over 200k episodes: top-3 workspace 77.354%; all-12 pooling 66.783%. This does not prove GWT but demonstrates a clean falsifier for the idea that maximal broadcast is always best.
3. **Truthful re-entry toy.** Three weak observations (65% each) of a stable binary latent. Final accuracy: no re-entry 65.011%; truthful cumulative re-entry 71.852%; shuffled previous-episode re-entry 56.887%. This demonstrates why `reentry_fraction` without semantic/provenance correctness is an unsafe metric.

## Fusion 2 — revised thesis

The strongest GWT-consistent emergence thesis after both research waves is:

> Functional emergence in FRANK should be searched for as **transient, selective, multiscale causal organization around workspace episodes**: specialist states compete for a capacity-limited workspace; selected content is broadcast in a format that diverse receivers can reconstruct and causally use; the resulting joint outcome is irreducible to matched simpler/pairwise descriptions under interventions; truthful outcome-bound re-entry changes subsequent competition; and useful organization occupies a bounded metastable regime rather than maximal coupling/synchrony. Emergence is present only to the degree that these causal contributions survive lesion/rescue, shuffle/null controls, interventional macro/micro comparison, estimator changes and OOD/implementation transfer.

This is compatible with GWT but does not equate GWT with emergence: a valid workspace can exist without higher-order causal emergence, and higher-order statistical structure can exist without a valid workspace causal chain.

## Counterhypotheses that must remain alive

C0 Ordinary event-driven orchestration plus good memory/routing explains all observed gains; GWT language adds no predictive power.
C1 Workspace bottleneck benefits are ordinary regularization/attention effects, not a distinct workspace mechanism.
C2 Statistical synergy is an estimator artifact or reflects common drivers; pairwise models suffice after correct conditioning.
C3 Macro causal advantage is produced by intervention-distribution/coarse-graining choices and does not transfer to natural task interventions.
C4 Re-entry benefits are just extra context/memory, not workspace-specific recursive closure.
C5 Dynamic topology gains are explained by compute allocation or cache locality rather than semantic coupling.
C6 J-space is a model-specific verbalization subspace; its relation to FRANK runtime GWT is superficial.
C7 Loop-of-loops dynamics add little beyond conventional queueing/control theory.
C8 Metastability/criticality measures are descriptive correlates, not causal controllers of workspace utility.
C9 Any apparent 'ephemeral organ' is just repeated task routing and has no irreducible causal identity.
C10 Emergence-control optimization Goodharts the readout and destabilizes the system; no general reusable control policy exists.
