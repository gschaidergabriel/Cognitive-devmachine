## 1. Executive synthesis

The prior RAE Emergence Certificate correctly required prospective prediction, recurrence, lesion/rescue, OOD transfer, multiscale/synergistic causal contribution, independent evaluation and robustness/cost shadows. E1 does not weaken this standard. It finds that the current FRANK/Synthetic-Clay architecture introduces additional ways to generate **false emergence evidence** unless the certificate is made stage- and identity-aware.

The strongest result is methodological:

> **Emergence should not be certified by a single high score, recurrence peak or successful endpoint rescue. It should be represented as a causal evidence profile that separates multiscale causal contribution from persistent role-identifiable organization, while proving that the observed effect is not an artifact of basis tearing, selection, projection, common drivers, partition search or bundled stage interventions.**

E1 therefore proposes the OPEN method candidate **E-CERT2** with two axes:

### Axis A — multiscale causal contribution
Does a macro/assembly scale contribute intervention-grounded causal information or function beyond the tested lower-level decomposition under matched information/capacity?

Useful evidence can include:
- controlled factorial intervention/mediation;
- PEID or another intervention-grounded synergy decomposition where appropriate;
- effective-information / causal-emergence analysis with preregistered or held-out macro definition;
- alternative-method shadows such as SVD/dynamical reversibility where useful;
- macro-vs-micro predictive/counterfactual comparisons with matched access to intervention information.

### Axis B — persistent causal organization
Is there a role-identifiable organization that:
- was predicted before observation;
- recurs above matched nulls;
- is causally necessary under a stage-specific lesion;
- can be **mechanistically** rescued;
- restores future/re-entry dynamics rather than merely the current endpoint;
- transfers/recurs OOD while preserving role/function?

A macroscale can satisfy Axis A without forming a persistent organ. A recurring assembly can satisfy parts of Axis B while adding no irreducible macro causal contribution. **`emergent_organ` candidacy requires both axes plus the shared validity gates.**

Current conclusion: **no FRANK organ/assembly is promoted as an emergent organ in E1.** The available repository evidence does not yet contain one prospectively preregistered live/twin candidate that passes the full certificate.

## 3. Fusion 1 — strongest competing explanations

E1 generated 18 falsifiable hypotheses. The highest-information counterexplanations were:

1. **Echo/persistence null:** a recurrent process may be dynamically persistent but functionally irrelevant.
2. **Identity null:** the same members may recur with different causal roles, so bag overlap can manufacture recurrence.
3. **Endpoint-rescue null:** directly patching the output can look like rescue while the causal mechanism/re-entry remains absent.
4. **Common-driver null:** directed prediction/TE can appear without a direct causal edge.
5. **Partition-search null:** selecting the macro/assembly definition that maximizes a score on the same sample can create a winner from noise.
6. **Stage-bundle null:** graph prior, candidate set, workspace, projection and re-entry can move together, creating a large system effect with no direct effect at the stage being named.
7. **Architecture/scalar null:** a scalar causal-emergence/Phi-like measure can be driven by architecture/representation and need not track learned organization monotonically.
8. **Selection/projection null:** authentic facts can be selected by a corrupted/noisy structure or correctly selected semantics can be misprojected into the reader.

These are now explicit negatives in `NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md`.

## 4. External research — Fusion 2

### 4.1 PEID strengthens the intervention requirement
**Partial Effective Information Decomposition for Synergistic Causality** (`arXiv:2605.03267`, 2026) defines unique/synergistic causal contribution under maximum-entropy interventions and supports hyperedge/downward-causation analysis. This is closer to the evidence E1 needs than passive nonadditivity because the decomposition is explicitly intervention-grounded.

Transfer to E-CERT2: PEID can be one Axis-A instrument; it is not a standalone emergent-organ certificate.

### 4.2 Artificial-agent causal emergence exists as a measurable research programme — but scalar interpretation is contested
**The Causally Emergent Alignment Hypothesis** (`arXiv:2605.06746`, 2026) reports causal-emergence signatures in learned RL representations that relate to reward development across environments/architectures.

A newer counterboundary is especially important: **Perspective Latents as an Architectural Condition for Causal Emergence in Active Inference Agents** (`arXiv:2607.20708`, 2026) reports that aggregate `Phi_r` magnitude can be largely architectural and even decrease with training, while the meaningful learning change becomes visible at the atom/compositional level. This directly supports E1's rejection of a one-number certificate.

### 4.3 Multi-agent work separates temporal synergy from functional collective organization
**Emergent Coordination in Multi-Agent Language Models** (ICLR 2026) uses information decomposition plus randomized interventions and coordination-free baselines. Its control condition can show strong temporal synergy without stable goal-directed complementarity; identity-linked differentiation and coordination interventions are needed to distinguish a higher-order collective from temporal coupling alone.

Transfer: E-CERT2 requires performance-relevant intervention effects and role identity, not only temporal synergy.

### 4.4 Macro-discovery needs causal/counterfactual discipline
**CAMO** (Findings of ACL 2026) builds computable micro-to-macro causal factors and uses simulator-internal counterfactual probing to orient/revise causal hypotheses. This supports the strategy of turning an emergent target into a falsifiable causal subgraph rather than a descriptive cluster.

### 4.5 Coarse-graining dependence is a real method risk
The 2025 npj Complexity work **Dynamical reversibility and a new theory of causal emergence based on SVD** explicitly identifies dependence on coarse-graining as a major challenge for EI-based causal emergence and proposes an SVD/dynamical-reversibility formulation intended to reduce that dependence.

Transfer: a serious Axis-A macro claim should carry at least one differently biased/cross-method shadow where feasible, especially when the macro partition was discovered rather than preregistered.

### 4.6 PID/TE require method-specific caution
Current multivariate PID work reports consistency limitations for three-or-more-source lattice decompositions under common desiderata, so one PID number cannot be treated as estimator-free ground truth. Likewise, partial/conditional TE can control known confounders but grows difficult in high-dimensional settings. E1 therefore keeps information-theoretic measures as instruments within an intervention/profile framework rather than final labels.

## 5. Empirical null controls

All magnitudes below are constructed workload/sample results, not FRANK estimates.

### EXP-EM1-MECHANISTIC-RESCUE-001
A nonlinear mediator `M=A*B` contributed to current output and future re-entry.

- lesion current MSE: **0.819255**;
- true mechanism rescue current MSE: **0**;
- fake endpoint-only rescue current MSE: **0**;
- future/re-entry MSE after fake rescue: **0.277444**;
- future/re-entry MSE after true rescue: **0**.

**Result:** endpoint recovery is not mechanistic rescue. A strong rescue must restore the causal path or an independently justified functionally equivalent path and must repair downstream/re-entry trajectories.

### EXP-EM1-PARTITION-SEARCH-NULL-002
Current and next 16-state microvariables were independent, so every fixed partition has true causal MI zero. E1 searched 2,000 random macro partitions on finite training data.

- best selected training macro MI: **0.007737 bit**;
- same selected partition on 100k held-out samples: **0.000121 bit**;
- mean top-50 training: **0.006117 bit**;
- same top-50 held out: **0.000144 bit**.

**Result:** post-hoc macro search can create an emergence-looking maximum under a null. The entire search procedure needs a held-out/null correction.

### EXP-EM1-ROLE-RECURRENCE-003
The same four members either retained their causal roles or were permuted across roles.

- true same-role cases: **25,134 / 50,000**;
- bag-of-members false recurrence: **24,866 / 50,000 = 49.732%**;
- role-aware false recurrence: **0**;
- role-aware true recurrence recall: **1.0**.

**Result:** recurrent assembly identity must include causal roles/relation identity, not only member overlap or embedding similarity.

### EXP-EM1-TE-COMMON-DRIVER-004
`Z_t` caused noisy proxy `X_t` and `Y_(t+1)`; there was no direct `X -> Y` edge.

- bivariate TE proxy `I(X_t;Y_(t+1)|Y_t)`: **0.253767 bit**;
- after conditioning on the true driver: **0.0000116 bit**.

**Result:** directed prediction gain is a discovery surface, not direct-causality proof. Confounder control or intervention is required.

### EXP-EM1-STAGE-ORTHOGONAL-SYNERGY-005
Graph prior `G`, workspace selection `W` and re-entry `R` were independently controlled.

- additive null three-way `Omega`: **-0.00144**;
- configured true three-way synergy `0.35`, estimated `Omega`: **0.35331**;
- bundled all-on/off contrast under additive null: **0.65023**;
- bundled contrast with true synergy: **1.00101**.

**Result:** a large whole-system contrast can exist under an interaction null. Stage-orthogonal factorial analysis is necessary before calling a higher-order interaction emergent.

### EXP-EM1-JUDGE0-REPLICATION-006
Independent Judge0/Python 3.8.1 code with new seeds/sample sizes reproduced all four applicable qualitative directions:

- role-insensitive false recurrence: **0.4989**; role-aware: **0**;
- bivariate TE: **0.25050 bit**; conditioned TE: **0.0000305 bit**;
- selected partition train MI: **0.01458 bit**; held-out: **0.000576 bit**;
- null `Omega`: **-0.00902**; configured synergy: **0.34446**.

This independently replicates the **failure mechanisms**, not live FRANK incidence or effect sizes.

## 6. E-CERT2 — method contract

A candidate profile should persist at least:

```text
candidate_identity / causal roles
causal_basis_receipt
formation_prediction
role_aware_recurrence
matched recurrence/persistence nulls
stage factorial / mediation design
causal lesion
mechanistic rescue
future/re-entry trajectory
OOD transfer
conditional directed-information profile
multiscale causal profile
macro-partition search/null provenance
selection integrity
projection/reconstruction/uptake receipt
robustness/cost/recovery shadow
independent evaluation
complete evidence ancestry
```

### Shared hard gates

1. **Identity gate** — role/relation identity preserved.
2. **Basis gate** — CognitiveFrame/semantic/activation/projection or operation-specific basis is compatible.
3. **Prediction gate** — formation/function prediction specified before the decisive observation.
4. **Null gate** — matched persistence/recurrence/complexity and permutation controls.
5. **Stage gate** — graph prior, Assembly/candidate availability, GWT selection, projection/uptake and re-entry manipulated independently or appropriately counterbalanced.
6. **Lesion gate** — targeted candidate mechanism lesion changes the predicted function.
7. **Mechanistic rescue gate** — causal path/equivalent mechanism restored, including downstream/re-entry.
8. **OOD gate** — role/function recur beyond discovery conditions.
9. **Multiscale gate** — macro/assembly contributes beyond tested lower-level decomposition under matched intervention information/capacity.
10. **Search-Goodhart gate** — partition/assembly discovery separated from scoring through preregistration, nested holdout or full search-null.
11. **Directed-information gate** — TE/GC-like directions conditioned for plausible common drivers and preferably intervention-tested.
12. **Integrity gate** — selected source path and reader projection/uptake are auditable where they mediate the effect.
13. **Robustness/cost gate** — causal benefit survives reasonable noise/recovery/cost shadows.
14. **Independence gate** — ancestry-distinct evaluation/replication before strong promotion.

The executable `E_CERT2_REFERENCE.py` intentionally distinguishes:
- `OPEN`;
- `MECHANISM_SUPPORTED`;
- `EMERGENT_ORGAN_CANDIDATE`.

Even the last status has `canonical_promotion=false` and `phenomenal_consciousness_inference=false`.
