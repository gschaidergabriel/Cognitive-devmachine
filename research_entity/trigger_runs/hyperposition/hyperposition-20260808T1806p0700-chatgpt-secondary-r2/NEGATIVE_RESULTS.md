# Hyperposition H4 — Negative / Limiting Results

1. **Clay-native is not being retested.** It is an owner-fixed target architecture. H4 only tests implementation/failure-mode consequences.
2. **One Clay language does not imply one trust/authority domain.** Symbolic unification cannot erase fact/activation/proposal/authority/effect distinctions without evidence.
3. **Textual `FACT`/`TEXT` tags are not proven security boundaries.** Concurrent C6 Groq 8B arms answered all 8 simple injected-memory questions correctly both with and without tags. That corpus did not discriminate the mechanisms.
4. **H4's alias collision rates are not estimates of Clay token collisions.** The namespace experiment is deliberately adversarial and only demonstrates the insufficiency of raw surface equality as authority identity.
5. **Typed cascade containment is partly by construction.** The six-domain hypergraph test encodes a gateway policy; its value is to expose measurable privilege/effect contamination and falsifiers, not to prove production safety.
6. **Firebreaks do not eliminate cognitive propagation.** At high beta the typed proxy still spread broadly across non-privileged cognitive domains; the large gain was containment of authority/effect escalation.
7. **Coverage is a hard economic constraint.** At 95% per-witness coverage the constructed fallback burden was ~14%; selective typing is not automatically cheap.
8. **Compact typed handles are not near-zero-latency proof.** The CPython microbenchmark only shows compact tuples were cheaper than verbose dict/string metadata; target Rust/C/Android cache and p99 costs remain open.
9. **OpenAlex success did not equal useful evidence.** The H4 query returned a weakly relevant review and was not used to strengthen a claim.
10. **Semantic Scholar remained rate-limited (HTTP 429).** arXiv, Tavily, Exa, native web and primary-source paths covered the relevant discovery space.
11. **Capability exposure is not per-call authorization.** H4 retains external deterministic value/effect authorization; a model-visible capability label cannot self-authorize.
12. **Record provenance is not selector integrity, and selector integrity is not origin authority.** These remain separate stages after Fusion/G10.
13. **Projection identity/hash is not semantic correctness proof.** A correctly hashed miscompile remains possible; reference/differential/mutation validation is still required.
14. **Semantic classes do not fix n-ary flattening.** H3's relation-instance/role-binding requirement survives unchanged.
15. **No emergence claim.** Cross-domain cascade, recurrence, integration and self-reference remain mechanism signals only; E1's null controls make bundled/persistence-only inference especially unsafe.
16. **No phenomenal-consciousness claim.** Nothing in H4 bears on phenomenal consciousness.
17. **No canonical promotion.** HYP-RC4 is OPEN pending live/twin, target-model, real selector/origin and low-level implementation tests.
