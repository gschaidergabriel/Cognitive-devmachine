## Core synthesis

**Receiver-Closed Workspace Emergence (RCWE):** functional emergence is not located in broadcast alone. A candidate mechanism requires an intervention-sensitive chain:

`competition -> selection/ignition -> addressability -> delivery -> receiver reconstruction -> uptake -> local causal use -> joint outcome -> provenance-bound re-entry -> changed later competition -> recurrence/transfer`

A high value at one stage must not conceal absence at another. RCWE refines, rather than replaces, the existing GWT measurement law:

`global_addressability != delivery != reconstruction != uptake != causal_use != outcome != reentry`

Functional emergence is provisionally present only when the closed receiver cycle predicts or controls outcomes better than isolated components or matched additive/null mechanisms. This does not establish phenomenal consciousness.

## External evidence and boundaries

1. Adversarial GNWT/IIT testing weakens a single stereotyped fixed-location ignition account without eliminating functional global availability. Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1
2. Shared workspaces can coordinate specialist modules, but coordination benefit depends on task structure and can reverse under overcoupling. Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01197 and https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01268-y
3. PID/ΦID synergy is relevant but estimator- and null-model-dependent. Source: https://elifesciences.org/articles/88173
4. Causal-emergence gains depend on coarse-graining, intervention distributions and comparator models. Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3856819/ and https://www.nature.com/articles/s44260-025-00028-0
5. Near-critical/metastable dynamics may facilitate flexible ignition but criticality proxies alone do not identify GWT or emergence. Sources: https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06613-8 and https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neural-circuits/articles/10.3389/fncir.2022.911245/full

## Hypotheses and counterhypotheses

### Immediate

- **RCWE-H1:** truthful, temporally correct, provenance-bound re-entry improves later selection/outcomes more than content-matched shuffled re-entry.
  - Null: any extra context explains the gain.
- **RCWE-H2:** receiver reconstruction and causal use explain outcome variance beyond broadcast coverage.
  - Null: the central controller already contains the complete solution.
- **RCWE-H3:** bounded workspace capacity produces an interior optimum between undercoupling and overcoupling.
  - Null: performance/transfer increase monotonically with coupling.
- **RCWE-H4:** a WorkspaceEpisode can be a useful causal macrostate under matched model complexity.
  - Null: random/task-label/learned alternative coarse-grainings perform equally.
- **RCWE-H5:** local receiver lesions yield role-specific loss and semantic rescue.
  - Null: redundancy or generic channel capacity explains both lesion and rescue.
- **RCWE-H6:** slow-state modulation changes fast ignition non-additively.
  - Null: direct prompt/context effects explain the interaction.

### Ambitious

- Multiple task-specific ignition thresholds outperform one global threshold.
- Re-entry acts primarily by changing the next candidate distribution.
- Hyperedges outperform the best pairwise reduction only when intervention predictions improve.
- Boot/recovery order is a slow GWT state variable only if effects survive full state restoration controls.
- Negative results can form an inhibitory meta-memory that changes candidate admission rather than merely wording.
- The useful macro-level may move over time among microstate, assembly, WorkspaceEpisode and recurring organ candidate.

### Extreme but GWT-consistent

- The workspace may be an episode-specific receiver coalition rather than a fixed bus.
- A functional emergent organ may be a recurring coalition that generates candidates and increases its own reconstruction probability through verified re-entry.
- The selected workspace content may be a causal counterfactual contract, not only a representation.
- Selective forgetting may be required to prevent workspace winner monopolization.
- A bounded readout-projector state may become a candidate without receiving authority to certify its own measurements.
- A selected topology-change contract may alter the next episode's communication geometry, but only through external capability/commit authorization.

All remain OPEN until discriminating evidence exists.

## Priority experiments

1. **Re-entry truth test:** truthful/provenance-correct vs time-shifted vs provenance-broken vs receiver-shuffled vs random vs none.
2. **Receiver closure test:** hold BroadcastEnvelope fixed while independently lesioning reconstruction, uptake, causal use and outcome access.
3. **Coupling phase diagram:** sweep workspace capacity, gain, delay and re-entry strength; measure transfer, specialization, perseveration, instability and cost.
4. **Moving macro-level:** compare micro, assembly, WorkspaceEpisode, organ-candidate and random coarse-grainings over sliding windows with matched complexity.
5. **Organ formation:** require preregistered recurrence, prediction, selective lesion, semantic rescue, OOD stability, causal contribution and implementation transfer.

Use frozen model outputs first; stochastic variants require repeated seeds and uncertainty intervals.

## Negative-result protections

The following outcomes must be retained rather than optimized away:

- high broadcast coverage with zero causal use;
- synergy without lesion sensitivity;
- macro gain that vanishes against matched alternative coarse-grainings;
- re-entry gain reproduced by arbitrary additional context;
- coupling gain that disappears after cost/instability shadows;
- recurring coalition without selective lesion/rescue or OOD transfer.
