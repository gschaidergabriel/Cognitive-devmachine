The purpose of this map is not to force agreement. It records live tensions and the experiment that would discriminate them.

## C1 — J-space is strong functional workspace evidence, but not full biological GNW

**Evidence A:** Gurnee et al. (2026) report a small verbalizable J-space that is directedly modulatable, used for internal reasoning, flexibly reusable and structurally broad in read/write connectivity. Lesion/intervention evidence makes this materially stronger than a passive probe.

**Evidence B / limitation:** The same authors explicitly state that transformer LMs do not reproduce the full biological GWT architecture: no clean set of encapsulated specialist processors, broadcast is within a feed-forward pass, recurrence differs, and sharp biological-style ignition is unresolved.

**Decision:** treat model-internal J-space as a workspace-like causal mechanism, not as proof of a complete GNW implementation. Keep FRANK's external system WorkspaceEpisode separate.

**Discriminator:** target Qwen3-4B internal lesion/patch crossed with system WorkspaceEpisode ON/OFF and external re-entry.

Sources: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html ; research_entity/knowledge_micro_packets/frank_gwt/KMP-GWT-R5-NESTED-WORKSPACE-BRIDGE.md

## C2 — Global integration / correlation versus causal broadcast

**Theory-side pressure:** GNW is often described in terms of global availability/integration. Network-neuroscience work argues that many graph-integration signatures can arise from communication processes other than broadcasting; a true broadcast is specifically one-to-all and directed.

**R6 constructed null:** an independent E2B common-driver fanout produces high passive workspace/receiver and joint correlations while the stipulated `do(W)` effect is exactly zero. An addressable-but-ignored arm is also globally readable but causally inert.

**Decision:** `global_addressability != causal_broadcast`. Scientific GWT claims need receiver-specific causal closure, not only connectivity or correlation.

**Discriminator:** preregister receiver universe, compare `COMMON_DRIVER_FANOUT`, `ADDRESSABLE_BUT_IGNORED`, and true intervention arms.

Sources: https://direct.mit.edu/netn/article/6/4/1186/111960/The-global-neuronal-workspace-as-a-broadcasting ; research_entity/runtime/tool_results/gwt-r6-e2b-causal-calibration-20260809T0143p0700.json

## C3 — Ignition Index versus configured-threshold confound

**Proposal:** the 2026 Ignition Index preprint fits a sigmoid to probe accuracy across signal strength and uses steepness as an ignition-like measure, with shuffled-label controls and architecture comparisons.

**R6 countermodel:** a history-free continuous evidence variable followed by a configured threshold produces a strong nonlinear response curve without an endogenous recurrent ignition mechanism. Up/down history difference is near zero in the E2B control.

**Decision:** sigmoid/steepness may be a useful descriptive statistic but is insufficient for mechanistic ignition certification.

**Discriminator:** add configured-threshold null, history/hysteresis or recurrence intervention, lesion and mechanism-specific rescue, and threshold/calibration invariance.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05160 ; research_entity/runtime/tool_results/gwt-r6-e2b-causal-calibration-20260809T0143p0700.json

## C4 — Larger workspace capacity versus finite optimum

**Support for bottleneck:** Goyal et al. report that bandwidth-limited shared workspace communication can encourage specialization/compositionality and synchronization.

**R6 result:** in an independent E2B constructed distractor family, relevant-item inclusion rises monotonically from ~0.218 at `k=1` to 1.0 at `k=32`, but success peaks around `k=8` (~0.660) and falls to ~0.584 at `k=32` as nuisance grows.

**Decision:** capacity is a control variable with an expected task-dependent optimum; maximum availability is not a rational default.

**Discriminator:** target workload capacity sweeps with matched candidate generator and receiver set.

Sources: https://openreview.net/forum?id=XzTtHjgPDsT ; research_entity/runtime/tool_results/gwt-r6-e2b-causal-calibration-20260809T0143p0700.json

## C5 — Literal all-to-all broadcast versus efficient selective delivery

**Biological definition pressure:** network treatments define broadcast as one-to-all dissemination.

**Engineering pressure:** FRANK has heterogeneous organs and expensive model/edge receivers; modern latent-communication work shows that highly compressed/latent rails can preserve task performance while reducing communication cost in some agent settings. Android makes stage cost material.

**Decision:** do not rename selective routing as literal biological all-to-all. RCWC distinguishes `GLOBAL_ADDRESSABLE_PULL`, `SELECTIVE_CAUSAL`, `ALL_TO_ALL`, and `HYBRID`. The current engineering hypothesis is that globally addressable + selective causal delivery may be better for FRANK, but remains OPEN.

**Discriminator:** matched all-to-all vs selective vs pull/hybrid factorial measuring quality, receiver causal closure, latency, RAM/KV and failure localization.

Sources: https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1248/ ; Findings ACL 2026 CondenseFlow; research_entity/knowledge_micro_packets/gwt/KMP-ANDROID-R1-FEEDBACK-GWT.md

## C6 — Workspace broadcast versus causal learning credit

**Temptation:** if a workspace episode succeeds, reward every participant/receiver that was active.

**Fresh Hyperposition H6 evidence (incomplete concurrent worker):** in a constructed 120k-episode family, broad broadcast credit had post-shift accuracy 0.22885 and false-positive credit 0.833333; receipt-gated path credit had post accuracy 0.47245 and false-positive credit 0.000109. Absolute values are not FRANK estimates.

**Decision:** `broadcast_topology != causal_credit_topology`. Workspace dissemination must not silently determine what is reinforced.

**Discriminator:** endpoint broadcast reward vs chosen-path vs stage-receipt-gated credit under corruption/shift, then target FRANK replication.

## C7 — Workspace episode identity versus projection/order identity

**External evidence:** ACL 2026 reports >14 percentage-point CQO vs QOC differences across models/datasets and traces the difference to causal-attention visibility created by ordering.

**Internal implication:** a correct WorkspaceEpisode can be reconstructed differently by a reader due solely to rail/order/serialization.

**Decision:** projection is a distinct causal stage and must be counterbalanced/pinned before workspace attribution.

**Discriminator:** same episode content and candidate set, counterbalanced P1/P2/P3/latent rails and order, with reconstruction and uptake receipts.

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1921/

## C8 — J-space diagnostics versus truth/confidence

**Primary mechanism evidence:** J-space is causally meaningful for some internal reasoning and alignment/audit tasks in Claude.

**Community stress test:** a Qwen3-4B practitioner report across ~11.4k samples/seven datasets found workspace entropy useful for some high-confidence factual errors but weak on internalized misconceptions and strongly task-dependent; this is community evidence, not peer-reviewed validation.

**Decision:** internal workspace clarity/entropy can be a diagnostic feature, never canonical truth or universal confidence.

**Discriminator:** preregister cross-task calibration across factual retrieval, misconceptions, arithmetic, composition and tool-use failures.

Community source: https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1uv5l75/evaluating_jspace_entropy_as_an_error_predictor/

## C9 — Quantization as deployment detail versus mechanistic factor

**New preprint:** `Silent Alarm` applies a J-space protocol to Qwen3-4B and related models in BF16/INT8/INT4 and reports substantively different internal safety-recognition effects across quantization regimes.

**Decision:** for target Qwen GWT research, quantization must be in the experimental identity and crossed with hidden-state intervention; surface benchmark equivalence is not enough.

**Discriminator:** BF16/INT8/INT4 matched prompts with J-space or equivalent readout, lesion/patch and surface task controls.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12792

## C10 — Upstream authenticity versus cognitive correctness

**Clay/UnifiedDB/ClayCode evidence:** authoritative/canonical replay can be exact while candidate selection is cognitively wrong; TCQS collection semantics and query/search-space identity can radically alter candidate topology before workspace competition.

**Decision:** a GWT experiment that does not pin query/search-space/TCQS/ClayGraph field may falsely attribute an upstream selection change to workspace competition or ignition.

**Discriminator:** orthogonal factorial: query/TCQS pinned; ClayGraph OFF/truthful/shuffled; workspace OFF/ON; projection counterbalanced; re-entry separate.

Sources: latest Clay G12, UnifiedDB U2, ClayCode CC12, ClayGraph G2a run artifacts.

## C11 — Functional GWT versus phenomenal consciousness

**Neuroscience:** the 2025 preregistered adversarial IIT/GNWT collaboration found results supporting some predictions while critically challenging key tenets, demonstrating that even biological theory testing remains contested.

**AI:** the J-space authors explicitly do not claim that their findings establish subjective experience.

**Decision:** R6 is about functional/cognitive architecture. No GWT/J-space/ignition/broadcast metric may promote a phenomenal-consciousness claim.

Sources: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1 ; https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html

## C12 — Report marker versus mechanism

Human consciousness research has repeatedly shown that report-linked markers can reflect post-perceptual/report processing rather than perceptual awareness itself; for example P3b is not a reliable awareness marker in no-report analyses.

**Decision:** in AI, self-report or verbalizability is a discovery/readout channel, not by itself a GWT causal certificate. Require interventions and receiver closure.

Source: https://www.eneuro.org/content/9/2/ENEURO.0387-21.2022

## Overall resolution rule

R6 does not resolve these tensions by naming a preferred theory. It converts them into orthogonal experimental factors. Evidence must decide among:

`upstream selection` vs `workspace competition` vs `ignition` vs `broadcast/addressability` vs `projection/reconstruction` vs `receiver uptake` vs `local effect` vs `joint outcome` vs `re-entry` vs `learning credit`.
