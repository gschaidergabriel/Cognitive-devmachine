`clay -> claygraph -> frank -> emergenz -> hyperposition -> claycode -> gwt history`

Run `tools/research_interconnect_preflight.py --trigger gwt --run-id <run_id>` before Fusion 1. The manifest must include all seven current scientific branches and deep ClayGraph history/material.

### ClayGraph — mandatory workspace substrate input

Every GWT run MUST deeply inspect the newest ClayGraph accepted/candidate architecture, activation/assembly semantics, self-use benchmarks, graph evolution decisions and negative results.

GWT research must explicitly distinguish:

- ordinary graph spreading activation from workspace candidate competition;
- graph salience/edge weights from selective ignition;
- graph reachability from actual global availability/broadcast;
- graph propagation from audience reconstruction/receipt;
- graph persistence from causal outcome/re-entry;
- retrieval success from workspace-mediated task effect.

ClayGraph may become a substrate for candidate generation, associative context, audience routing or re-entry, but those roles must be causally tested rather than assumed. If GWT results imply changes to ClayGraph assembly selection, activation decay, salience, episode identity, receipt/re-entry links or causal closure, write those findings back as ClayGraph-discoverable deltas.

## Focus on measurable mechanisms

- candidate generation and competition;

- outcome receipt;

- ClayGraph assembly/activation as a possible substrate or confound.

## Mandatory causal tests where feasible

- stage-presence baseline vs linked causal episode validation;
- lesion/rescue of selection, broadcast, reconstruction and re-entry separately;
- ClayGraph activation ON/OFF while holding workspace stages constant;
- workspace ignition ON/OFF while holding graph retrieval/activation constant;
- truthful vs shuffled re-entry;
- audience/effect receipt ablations;
- capacity/competition sweeps;
- coupling sweeps and noise amplification;
- graph fan-out/activation-depth sweeps;
- information-flow measures plus task utility.

The goal is to identify whether ClayGraph improves GWT by supplying structured associative state, whether GWT improves ClayGraph by selecting bounded assemblies, or whether one mechanism merely mimics the measurements of the other.

Do not infer subjective consciousness from workspace or graph functionality. Results should feed FRANK, ClayGraph, emergence, Clay, ClayCode and architecture packets when cross-domain relevance is demonstrated.
