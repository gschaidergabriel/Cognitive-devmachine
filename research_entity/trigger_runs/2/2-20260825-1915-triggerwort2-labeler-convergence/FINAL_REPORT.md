## 7. Adversarial review conclusions

### Creator

Generate D/E targets only from deterministic mechanics over the latent task state.

### Shortcut reviewer

Reject any example where label-bearing evaluator metadata is model-visible or where branch/order/codebook/nuisance signatures predict the target independently of semantics.

### Evidence-identity reviewer

Keep four concepts separate:

- exact evidence event identity;
- correlation/causal ancestry;
- payload identity;
- independence qualification/group.

Exact replay is idempotent. Distinct correlated events may remain visible for provenance but must not gain independent support merely by being repackaged or repeated.

### Hyperposition reviewer

The converged labeler must preserve the full branch-local carrier, including semantic value, context/applicability, positive/negative evidence, falsifier, scope, provenance, epistemic state and projection visibility. Projection cannot delete a branch. Branch birth and merge require explicit deterministic rules.

Synthetic task-local resolution must remain typed separately from admitted world/causal evidence. `canonical_truth_allowed=false` is necessary but insufficient if training fields themselves teach a semantic type error such as calling a synthetic proof `RESOLVED_BY_EVIDENCE` without qualification.

### Acceptance reviewer

No positive D/E integrated family is training-admitted merely because source labelers or tests exist. Exact committed execution, derivation-chain validation, family/provenance split, nuisance/MI tests and candidate admission are still required.
