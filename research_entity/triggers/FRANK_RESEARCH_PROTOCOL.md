## Mandatory Clay foundation

The `frank` trigger is downstream of the integrated Synthetic Clay research foundry. Before generating FRANK architecture hypotheses, the worker MUST load and synthesize the newest completed Clay research material, including where present:

- latest Clay `FINAL_REPORT.md`;
- Clay hypothesis matrix and transformed hypotheses;
- Clay experiment results and synthetic/live scope labels;
- Clay negative results;
- Clay architecture candidate/delta;
- Clay Topic Packets and surgical micro-packets;
- source/provenance refs and open questions.

Clay is the primary architecture foundation, not unquestioned truth. A newer FRANK run may revise a Clay result only by explicitly identifying the Clay dependency, presenting contrary evidence/constraints and preserving the contradiction/supersession lineage.

After Clay, incorporate the newest relevant GWT, ClayCode, Hyperposition and Emergence branches. The required priority is:

`clay -> gwt -> claycode -> hyperposition -> emergenz -> frank history`

Run `tools/research_interconnect_preflight.py --trigger frank --run-id <run_id>` and materialize its output as the run's `INTERCONNECT_MANIFEST.json` before Fusion 1. A FRANK report without this foundation manifest is incomplete and cannot strongly promote an architecture delta.
