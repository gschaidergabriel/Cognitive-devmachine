## Key invariant split

The design must keep these quantities distinct:

- **semantic equivalence**: behavior/policy meaning preserved under allowed representation transformation;
- **product identity**: the exact admitted artifact/runtime/service/state lineage that is allowed to be `self.root` now;
- **authority**: what effects are permitted and under whose scoped intent;
- **provenance**: what evidence supports identity/semantic claims;
- **freshness/currentness**: which epoch/generation/lineage is live;
- **causal credit**: what verified outcome may update reward/state;
- **truth**: never minted by provenance, salience, witness or repeated recurrence.

A hash cannot replace semantic equivalence. Semantic equivalence cannot replace exact ProductStart identity. Neither can mint effect authority.
