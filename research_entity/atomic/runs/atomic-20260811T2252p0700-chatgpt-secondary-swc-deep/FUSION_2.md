# ATOMIC SWC-001 — Fusion 2

Fresh external research destroys the broad novelty claim but strengthens a narrower engineering hypothesis.

The local-to-global structure is established across database relations, CSPs and distributed systems. Acyclic scope hypergraphs can admit local-to-global guarantees in the relevant relation setting, while cyclic scope structures can contain pairwise-compatible local states with no global realization. Structural decomposition and incremental decomposition are also established techniques. Recent multi-agent work already applies sheaf/global-section language, so a generic "sheaf workspace" is not a defensible novelty claim.

The surviving Frank-specific composition is therefore not "invent sheaves". It is:

**Compile each WorkspaceEpisode's typed receiver-scope family into the cheapest exact coherence plan justified by current ClayGraph topology and provenance, preserve every coherent global alternative as Hyperposition, emit an explicit obstruction witness when no global assignment exists, and prevent coherent re-entry on obstruction without granting truth, identity or effect authority.**

This candidate is now named **SWC-001 — Sectional Workspace Compiler**.

The most important simplification is negative: SWC should not become a new runtime organ unless evidence proves that an independently observable service boundary is necessary. Prefer compiler lowering into conventional local-consistency/join/decomposition/CSP machinery plus typed Clay witnesses.

The strongest counterhypothesis remains live: a conventional always-global validator may be simpler and fast enough on real Frank workloads. If so, topology adaptation is rejected. A second counterhypothesis is that receiver semantic scopes cannot be extracted reliably enough from actual Frank state; if scope extraction is unstable, the mathematical guarantee is irrelevant.

LCOP remains separate. Anthropic's 2026 reported J-space results make a native model workspace plausible for at least the studied Claude models, but SWC does not identify or manipulate latent model workspace state. It validates explicit Clay semantic receiver projections. The queued native-workspace x ClayGWT factorial should therefore proceed independently.
