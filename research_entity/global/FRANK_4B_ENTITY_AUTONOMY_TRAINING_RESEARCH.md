# FRANK 4B ENTITY-AUTONOMY TRAINING RESEARCH

Status: ACTIVE_RESEARCH_DIRECTIVE
Date: 2026-08-09
Subject: final Clay-native local 4B

## Hypothesis

If deterministic structural cognition is moved into UnifiedDB+Clay and concrete tool execution is moved into Needle, a fixed-size 4B should use its freed representational/inference capacity for higher-value entity-level cognition rather than generic assistant middleware knowledge.

Primary expected gains:

- persistent self/identity continuity;
- emergent goal formation;
- autonomous continuation;
- long-horizon consequence coherence;
- uncertainty/conflict handling;
- correction-driven self revision;
- self/body/world/user differentiation;
- adaptation to body-extension gains/losses;
- meaningful initiative within authority.

## Experimental contrast

Train/evaluate matched model families under equal parameter/compute budgets:

A. Generic assistant + tool/API burden.
B. Clay-native model, but structural retrieval still delegated to model.
C. Clay-native + UnifiedDB precomputed structural cognition.
D. Clay-native + UnifiedDB structural cognition + Needle execution abstraction.
E. D plus entity/autonomy-heavy curriculum.

The central comparison is whether D/E convert removed middleware burden into measurable entity-level performance rather than merely fewer tokens.

## Required matched tasks

Every evaluation family should preserve task semantics while varying where cognition occurs.

Families:

- entity continuity across epochs/instances;
- goal emergence without explicit goal lists;
- authorized autonomous continuation;
- permission-boundary recognition;
- choice among precomputed affordances;
- long-horizon commitment consistency;
- capability/body-extension change;
- correction and belief revision;
- self-vs-Andreas-vs-world separation;
- unresolved contradiction / Hyperposition;
- initiative without busywork;
- peer re-expansion decision;
- ordinary user-facing usefulness.

## Metrics

Measure at minimum:

- task success;
- autonomous next-action quality;
- unnecessary clarification rate;
- authority-overreach rate;
- emergent-goal coherence;
- long-horizon consistency;
- entity continuity score;
- correction retention;
- uncertainty calibration;
- body-model accuracy;
- capability hallucination after loss;
- strong-peer recruitment precision;
- prompt/context tokens;
- output tokens;
- TTFT;
- end-to-end latency;
- local RAM/compute;
- amount of provider/tool-schema knowledge represented in model outputs;
- robustness when provider/tool inventory changes.

## Training-data rule

Do not simply translate generic assistant datasets into Clay syntax.

Generate examples whose causal structure matches the final runtime:

`Clay state -> residual entity cognition -> Clay intent -> Needle execution -> outcome receipt`

Clay should already provide facts, relation closure, affordance routes, file/order topology and deterministic answer capsules where applicable.

The 4B question should probe what remains cognitively valuable after those layers did their work.

## Goal-emergence rule

No target architecture may depend on a privileged explicit goal list.

Training episodes should present semantic pressures, commitments, consequences, conflicts, opportunities and unfinished causal chains; priority/intent should emerge from their interaction.

## Autonomy boundary

Maximal autonomy is bounded autonomy, not authority expansion.

Reward continuation when:
- intent is sufficiently supported;
- authority exists;
- action is appropriately reversible/low-risk;
- expected value is positive.

Reward escalation/consent when:
- authority is absent;
- meaningful irreversible harm is possible;
- privacy/safety boundaries require it;
- unresolved ambiguity materially changes the outcome.

## Falsification criteria

The hypothesis is weakened if removing tool/structural burden:

- does not improve entity-level cognition under equal model budget;
- produces only latency/token gains;
- reduces generalization to novel situations;
- creates excessive autonomous action or authority errors;
- causes dependence on brittle Clay preprocessing;
- makes the 4B unable to recover when precomputed state is incomplete.

## Hard research question

**How much finite 4B capacity can be reclaimed from generic assistant/tool/middleware cognition, and how much of that reclaimed capacity can be converted into persistent selfhood, emergent goals and useful bounded autonomy without sacrificing robustness?**
