### Core
Synthetic Clay should be treated as one logical world and transition constitution spanning several state lifecycles, not as one giant mutable graph or one physical database. Canonical identity/history/provenance, contextual state, activation, temporary assemblies, transformations and execution authority can share identity while remaining semantically distinct planes.

### Evidence / boundary
This is the architecture conclusion of the RAE-8.3/G1 source run. It is a source-grounded research architecture, not a live-production proof. Later RAE revisions may refine the plane count or implementation.

### Implication
Physical storage and host runtime may remain plural while FRANK experiences one coherent semantic state medium.
