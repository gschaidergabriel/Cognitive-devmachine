### Core
Needle remains unchanged in semantic identity and fully used as the sole non-Clay classifier/router. Its output is sealed into a Clay `NeedleRouteCertificate` containing route, confidence, Needle version and input-event identity.

The Clay-native 4B may reason over the certificate and fill arguments, but route-class override requires a new Needle/policy decision. Capability/effect authority remains deterministic and separate from Needle.

### Boundary
A C6 exhaustive toy policy produced zero model/memory route overrides, but this does not test Needle's own classification accuracy. Confusion, abstention, latency and poisoning must be measured live.
