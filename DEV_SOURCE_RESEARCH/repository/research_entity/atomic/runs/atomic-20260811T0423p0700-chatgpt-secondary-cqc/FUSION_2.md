# FUSION 2 — after external collision search

The broad novelty claim collapsed: bisimulation minimization, state abstraction, causal abstraction under interventions, approximate abstractions and non-destructive equality spaces are established research families. CQC therefore cannot be promoted as new mathematics or as a generic new compiler paradigm.

The surviving Frank-specific mechanism is narrower:

`historical Frank baseline/replay model`
`-> compile protected observable alphabet`
`-> compile intervention alphabet from lesion/rescue/reentry/authority/recovery contracts`
`-> partition refinement / causal equivalence search`
`-> preserve every historical lineage identity inside quotient classes`
`-> emit smaller executable architecture candidate`
`-> emit QuotientCertificate bound to baseline refs + semantic epoch + observables + interventions + test corpus`
`-> any new material observable/intervention/epoch/failure invalidates certificate`
`-> target-VPS matched differential decides promotion, not the compiler`

This differs from ordinary output-preserving optimization because Frank's equivalence boundary deliberately includes causal-use receipts, identity/provenance, effect-authority decisions, verified outcomes, learning/re-entry and recovery. It also differs from a permanent proof claim: CQC-001 certificates are explicitly scoped evidence objects.

## Why this remains radical enough to test

The architectural departure is that human-named organs cease to be the default unit of implementation. They become historical lineage labels over equivalence classes discovered from measured causal behavior. A future one-module Frank can therefore be generated from evidence rather than manually designed module fusion, while still making the original organ ancestry reconstructable.

## Main risk

The intervention/observable alphabet is necessarily incomplete for a large adaptive stochastic cognitive system. The safest interpretation is not `equivalent`, but `no distinguishing counterexample within certificate scope`. This is why CQC-001 starts exact and finite, refuses approximate promotion, and treats novel failure evidence as an invalidation event.

## Strongest conventional competitor after research

Purpose-built regression/lesion/replay tests plus normal compiler optimization and manual fusion. CQC survives only if it reduces missed causal distinctions, architecture/test maintenance, or search complexity enough to justify the new compiler machinery on real Frank components.
